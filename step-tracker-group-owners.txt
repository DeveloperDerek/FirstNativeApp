STEP TRACKER - GROUP OWNERS AND APPLE SIGN-IN ON DELETION (BLUEPRINT)
Two things that go wrong when someone leaves:
  A. A group keeps going when its owner leaves or deletes their account
  B. Deleting an account revokes its Sign in with Apple token
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database and screens need, and the
decisions made (section 6). Items 1d and 1c of
step-tracker-recommendations.txt.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  GROUPS
  - Every group has one owner (groups.owner_id). Only the owner can
    rename it, delete it and remove members.
  - The owner's screen has "Delete group" but no "Leave group".
  - The database doesn't stop an owner leaving anyway (deleting their
    own membership row, e.g. from an older app or a direct call). The
    group is then owned by someone who isn't in it: nobody left in the
    group can rename it or remove members.
  - Deleting an account deletes every group it owns, for EVERY member
    (owner_id has "on delete cascade"): the group, its members list,
    its quests and quest history. The Delete account dialog says so
    ("any groups you own"), but the members aren't asked.

  APPLE
  - Sign in with Apple is built but turned off (it needs a paid Apple
    Developer account; EXPO_PUBLIC_APPLE_SIGN_IN).
  - Apple requires that when an account made with Sign in with Apple
    is deleted, the app revokes its Apple tokens, so the app no longer
    shows under the person's Apple ID > "Sign in with Apple". Today
    the delete-account Edge Function deletes the account but doesn't
    revoke anything (there is a TODO for it).
  - Google is already disconnected on the phone when an account is
    deleted (GoogleSignin.revokeAccess in src/api/account.ts).

======================================================================
2. PART A - THE GROUP PASSES ON
======================================================================

  THE RULE
  - A group always has an owner who is in it. When the owner goes, the
    group passes to the member who has been in it longest (the oldest
    joined_at), and carries on: members, quests, quest history and the
    invite code all stay.
  - Only when nobody else is left is the group deleted.

  WHO IS NEXT (DECIDED)
  - The longest-standing ACTIVE member (signed up, not
    under age, Terms accepted, not paused). If there are only inactive
    members left, the longest-standing of them (they may come back);
    the group is deleted only when there is nobody at all.
  - Blocks don't matter here: they are between two people, not about
    the group.

  WHEN IT HAPPENS
  - The owner leaves: a new "Leave group" for owners, in place of
    nothing. The confirmation names who takes over:
      "Leave Walkers? @sam will become the owner."
    or, alone in the group:
      "Leave Walkers? You're the only member, so the group will be
       deleted."
  - The owner deletes their account: every group they own passes on
    the same way, in the same step as the deletion.
  - The account is deleted by an admin (Part D of
    step-tracker-safety.txt) or by the under-age job: the same.
  - The owner's membership is removed by any other route (an older app
    build, a direct call): the same. The database does it, not the
    app, so every route is covered.

  HANDING IT OVER ON PURPOSE (DECIDED: YES)
  - The owner can pick the next owner themselves: "Make
    owner" on a member's card or row in the group (owner only), with a
    confirmation. The old owner stays in the group as a member.

  TELLING THE NEW OWNER (DECIDED: A PUSH)
  - A push "You're now the owner of Walkers", the same way
    friend request pushes go (only if they have push on). Without it,
    they find out when they next open the group ("Owner" next to it).

  THE DELETE ACCOUNT DIALOG
  - Today: "...and any groups you own." New: "Groups you own pass to
    the member who has been in them longest. A group with no one else
    in it is deleted."

  IN THE DATABASE
  - The owner_id link stops deleting groups along with the account
    ("on delete cascade" becomes "on delete restrict"), so a group can
    never vanish by accident; the step below always moves or deletes
    it first.
  - One function does the handing over, used by every route:
      pass_group_on(group, leaving person)
        - locks the group row first, so two things at once (the owner
          leaving while the next member also leaves) can't both pick
          the same person or leave it ownerless
        - picks the next owner by the rule above, or deletes the group
          if there is nobody
  - Called from:
      - a check that runs when a membership row is deleted: if it was
        the owner's, pass the group on
      - a check that runs before an account is deleted: pass on every
        group it owns (before the membership rows go)
      - make_group_owner(group, person): the owner hands it over on
        purpose; the person must be a current member
  - The group update rule keeps refusing a change of owner_id from the
    app, so ownership only moves through these functions.
  - Old data: groups whose owner isn't a member today are fixed once,
    by the migration, with the same rule.
  - Reports (step-tracker-safety.txt): a group report stays about the
    group, whoever owns it. Nothing changes there.

  BUILT (20261106000000_group_owners.sql)
  - As designed. Notes from building:
      - Every membership removal (not just the owner's) locks the group
        row first and then reads who owns it. Without that, a member
        leaving at the same moment as the owner could be picked as the
        next owner on the way out; the race test
        (supabase/tests/concurrency/group_owner_race.sh) showed it in
        all three cases before the fix.
      - The owner's screen now has Leave group too; the confirmation
        asks the server who would take over (next_group_owner), so the
        app and the database use one rule.
      - "Make owner" is in the ••• menu on a member's card, opened from
        the group screen, for the owner only.

3. PART B - REVOKING SIGN IN WITH APPLE
======================================================================

  WHAT APPLE NEEDS
  - A call to Apple's revoke endpoint with one of the account's Apple
    tokens, signed with the app's private Sign in with Apple key.
  - Supabase doesn't keep Apple tokens for the way the app signs in
    (the app sends Apple's identity token to Supabase, which checks it
    and keeps nothing else). So there is no token to revoke at deletion
    time unless the app gets one.

  HOW (DECIDED: ASK FOR APPLE AGAIN AT DELETION)
  - Ask for Apple once more at deletion. For an account
    that signed in with Apple, Delete account shows the Apple sheet
    ("Confirm with Apple"). Apple gives the app a one-time code
    (valid 5 minutes). The app sends it to the delete-account Edge
    Function, which:
      1. swaps the code with Apple for a token
      2. revokes that token with Apple
      3. deletes the account, as today
    Nothing is stored, and the person confirms the deletion with Apple
    at the same time.
  - The alternative: swap the code for a long-lived token at every
    sign-in and keep it (encrypted) on the server until deletion. No
    extra step at deletion, but a secret kept for every Apple account
    for as long as it exists. Not chosen.

  IF APPLE CAN'T BE REACHED (DECIDED: DELETE ANYWAY, AND LOG IT)
  - Delete the account anyway (deleting is what the person
    asked for and what the stores require), and record the failure in
    a log with the account id only, so you can see it happened. The
    person can still remove the app under Apple ID settings. Tried
    twice before giving up.
  - Not chosen: refusing to delete until Apple answers (a person
    could be unable to delete their account).

  SECRETS
  - The Sign in with Apple private key (.p8), its key ID, the team ID
    and the bundle ID go into the Edge Function's secrets in the
    Supabase dashboard. Never in the app or the repo.
  - Building it needs the paid Apple Developer account (the same one
    Sign in with Apple needs anyway). Until then it can be built and
    tested with the Apple call replaced by a stand-in, and stays off
    while Sign in with Apple is off.

  ACCOUNTS WITH BOTH APPLE AND EMAIL
  - An account can have more than one way in (DEVICE-TESTS.md §7). If
    Apple is one of them, Apple is revoked; the others need nothing.

  RELEASE NOTE
  - Google sign-in is offered, so check whether Apple's guideline 4.8
    (login services) requires offering Sign in with Apple on iOS before
    release. If it does, this part is needed for release too.

  BUILT (supabase/functions/delete-account, 20261107000000_apple_revocation_log.sql)
  - As designed, waiting for the paid Apple Developer account. Notes:
      - The swap and revoke live in delete-account/apple.ts, tested by
        `npm test` against a stand-in for Apple (apple.test.ts): two
        tries when Apple doesn't answer or fails on its side, none for a
        bad or old code, a failed revoke retried with the same token,
        and no token or Apple message in the logged reason.
      - Without the four Apple secrets the function still deletes the
        account and logs "Sign in with Apple is not set up on the
        server" (checked end to end against the local Supabase).
      - Not checked yet: signing Apple's client secret with a real key
        and Apple's real answers. That needs the key; the phone checks
        are in DEVICE-TESTS.md §15.
      - The app asks for Apple only when Sign in with Apple is turned on
        and the account uses it; cancelling the Apple sheet deletes
        nothing.

======================================================================
4. BUILDING ORDER
======================================================================

  1. Part A in the database: pass_group_on, the checks on membership
     and account deletion, owner_id no longer cascading, the old-data
     fix, make_group_owner, the push to the new owner.
  2. Part A in the app: "Leave group" for owners with the confirmation
     naming the next owner, "Make owner", the new Delete
     account wording.
  3. Part B, once the paid Apple Developer account exists: the
     "Confirm with Apple" step, the Edge Function's swap and revoke,
     the failure log, the secrets.

======================================================================
5. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  - Owner leaves: the longest-standing active member becomes owner;
    members, quests and the invite code are unchanged.
  - Owner leaves with only inactive members: the longest-standing of
    them becomes owner.
  - Owner leaves alone: the group is deleted.
  - Owner deletes their account (by themselves, by an admin, by the
    under-age job): each owned group passes on or, if alone, is
    deleted; groups they were only a member of are untouched.
  - The owner's membership removed directly (as an older app would):
    passes on the same way.
  - make_group_owner: only the owner can call it, only to a current
    member; the old owner stays a member.
  - Nobody can change owner_id from the app directly.
  - A group can't lose its owner by any route: after every test, every
    group's owner is one of its members.
  - Old data: a group whose owner wasn't a member is fixed by the
    migration.
  - The owner leaving while the next member leaves, from two sessions
    (supabase/tests/concurrency): the group ends with an owner who is a
    member, or is deleted if both were the last two.

  EDGE FUNCTION (part B, with Apple replaced by a stand-in)
  - An Apple account: the code is swapped and revoked, then the account
    is deleted.
  - Apple fails twice: the account is still deleted and the failure is
    logged with the account id only.
  - Not an Apple account: no Apple call.
  - A bad or old code: the account is still deleted, the failure logged.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - As the owner, Leave group: the confirmation names the next owner;
    on their phone the group now says Owner.
  - As the only member, Leave group: it says the group will be deleted.
  - Delete the owner's account: the group carries on for the others.
  - With Sign in with Apple on (later): Delete account asks to confirm
    with Apple; afterwards the app is gone from Settings > Apple ID >
    Sign in with Apple.

======================================================================
6. DECISIONS
======================================================================

  1. Next owner: the longest-standing active member; if none is
     active, the longest-standing member; the group is deleted only
     when nobody is left.
  2. "Make owner": yes, an owner can hand the group over on purpose.
  3. The new owner gets a push ("You're now the owner of <group>"), if
     they have push on.
  4. Apple: asked for again at deletion; nothing is stored.
  5. Apple unreachable: the account is deleted anyway (after two
     tries) and the failure logged with the account id only.
