STEP TRACKER - ACTIVITY BOARD (BLUEPRINT)
A list of what your friends and group mates have done lately ("Sam
reached their goal", "Ana unlocked A week in a row", "The Walkers won
a quest"), newest first, so you can see how everyone is doing.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what the board shows, where each line comes from, who can
see it, what the screens look like, and the choices to make before
building (section 10).

Nothing in this blueprint changes an existing rule. Coins, the daily
goal, streaks, achievements, blocking and the group rules all
stay exactly as their own blueprints describe them. The board only
SHOWS things the server already records, under the read rules that
already exist. Section 12 lists what an earlier draft asked for that
would have changed a rule, and what happens to it instead.

Builds on what the app already has:
  - daily coins at the goal (claim_daily_rewards, run by sync_day after
    each upload), one coin_transactions row per day ('daily_goal', the
    day), never taken back (step-tracker-goals-streaks.txt)
  - achievements, earned once and kept for good, each with its real
    day (user_achievements.earned_on; step-tracker-achievements.txt)
  - group quests and who was in each party (quests, quest_party;
    step-tracker-group-quests.txt)
  - the read rules: is_active_account, can_see, is_friend,
    shares_group, and the group functions (group_leaderboard,
    group_member_card), which already cover blocked, paused, hidden,
    under-age and unfinished accounts, and SAFE-1
  - the player card (src/components/player-card.tsx) and the badge
    art (assets/badges)
  - the tab bar (Today, History, Friends, Groups, Profile) and the way
    the tab badges refresh on open and on coming back to the foreground
    (step-tracker-notifications.txt)

======================================================================
1. THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE
======================================================================

  - Friends gets a third button next to Today and Last 7 days:
    Activity. (Each group gets the same view later, section 11.)
  - One line per person per day, saying what they did that day: reached
    their goal, unlocked achievements, or both. A group's view also has
    a line for each quest the group won.
  - Newest day first. "Show older" at the bottom for older days, up to
    30 days back.
  - Tap a line to open that person's player card.
  - No new table. Every line is read from records the server already
    keeps, at the moment you look, under the read rules that already
    exist. So nothing can be counted twice, nothing has to be kept in
    step, and a block or an unfriend takes effect on the next load.
  - No pushes, no tab badge, no coins.

======================================================================
2. WHAT A LINE SAYS
======================================================================

  THE DAY LINE (Friends and groups)
  One per person per day, when that day has any of:
    - the goal reached: there is a 'daily_goal' coin row for that day
      AND the uploaded day still exists (section 4)
    - achievements earned that day (earned_on)

    Sam reached their goal                         Today
    Sam reached their goal · unlocked first 10k    Yesterday
    Ana unlocked a week in a row                   Tue, Oct 13
    Ana unlocked a million steps and 100 days ...  Mon, Oct 12

  - Your own lines are in the list too (the ranking includes you), and
    only yours add the coins: "You reached your goal · +150 coins". The
    amount is the row's own amount, so a day from before the coin
    rebalance says +100.
  - The sentence after the name is all lowercase, achievement names
    included ("unlocked first 10k"). The name and the day label keep
    their capitals.
  - Several achievements on one day are listed together on one line
    ("and 2 more" past two).
  - A line that gains something later the same day (an achievement
    after the goal) is the same line, with more on it. It never becomes
    two lines.

  THE QUEST LINE (that group's view only)
    The Walkers won Weekend Wander · 5 walked      Sat Oct 11

  - One per quest with status 'completed', on the day it was completed
    (completed_at in the shop time zone, the same day the quest
    achievements use). "5 walked" is the size of its party.
  - Never shown in Friends: it would tell a friend outside the group
    about the group.

  WHAT A LINE NEVER SHOWS
  - Step counts. Friends and group mates see today's and this week's
    totals on the rankings, never a single past day's number, and the
    board doesn't change that.
  - Anyone else's coins, wallet or progress toward an achievement.
  - The goal number (it's each player's own daily target).
  - Anything about a person the read rules say you can't see.

  ICONS (PIXEL ART, NO SYSTEM EMOJI)
  - The person's character head, drawn from their avatar like the
    rankings, and the badge for what they did: the existing badge
    frame and symbols (shoe, footprints, flame, flag) for achievements,
    and the same frame with a green check mark (badge_goal, drawn in
    scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py) for a goal day. A quest
    line uses the quest badge (badge_quests, the flag). No new art
    beyond the goal badge.

======================================================================
3. WHO SEES WHAT
======================================================================

  FRIENDS > ACTIVITY
  - Your lines, and the lines of each person who is, at that moment:
      - your friend (is_friend) and someone you can see (can_see: both
        active, neither blocked, not paused or hidden)
      - sharing their steps (only people who share have coins or
        achievements at all; a new server-only check for a person,
        like notification_counts_for(), makes it explicit)
  - The caller must be signed in and active (is_active_account), as for
    every function the app calls. Friends is already closed until you
    share your steps (sharing-required.tsx), so a viewer always shares.

  A GROUP > ACTIVITY
  - The lines of the group's current members and the group's quest
    lines, under the same rules as group_leaderboard and
    group_member_card: you and they are both current members, both
    active. Group mates who aren't your friends are included, the same
    as on the group's ranking.
  - Blocking follows SAFE-1: blocked people still see each other in a
    group they share, through the group functions only. So a person you
    blocked still has lines in that group's view, and never in Friends.
  - A member who left: no longer listed from the next load. A quest
    line stays for the group (the quest is the group's), with its
    party size unchanged.

  WHY NOTHING ON SCREEN CAN BE STALE FOR LONG
  - Every load asks the server again, and the server applies the rules
    above to every line it returns. Unfriend, block, pause, sharing
    off, leaving a group, account deletion: the next load leaves those
    lines out. No separate removal messages are needed.
  - On this phone, blocking or removing a friend also drops that
    person's lines from the list straight away (the app already knows
    who it just acted on).

======================================================================
4. WHEN RECORDS CHANGE (THE EXISTING RULES, AS THEY SHOW HERE)
======================================================================

  - A day corrected lower (Apple Health or Health Connect changed it,
    the phone uploaded the last 7 days again): the coins stay, as
    claim_daily_rewards already decides, so "reached their goal" stays
    too. The board says what the server paid, nothing more.
  - An achievement: earned stays earned, so its line stays, with its
    real day.
  - A day the person deleted: the goal part of that day's line is left
    out (the uploaded day must still exist), so deleting a day removes
    it from other people's view. The coins stay, as now. Any
    achievement earned that day stays, as it does on the player card.
  - A day uploaded late (up to 7 days, the phone's upload window): its
    line appears under its own day, not at the top, on the next load.
  - A goal-day line needs the day to be after the switch to "the goal
    is the daily target" (goal_is_coin_target_from, GOAL-16). Before
    it, a coin row meant the coin target, not the goal, so those days
    show no goal line. They are older than 30 days in any case.
  - Manually typed-in steps already don't count toward coins, so they
    never make a line. The board adds no checks of its own.

======================================================================
5. ORDER, PAGES AND REFRESHING
======================================================================

  ORDER
  - Newest day first. The day is the phone's calendar day the steps
    were walked, as everywhere else in the app (the server doesn't
    know time zones, recommendations 3d), or for a quest the shop day
    of completed_at.
  - Within a day: the line whose latest part arrived most recently
    first (the coin row's created_at, an achievement's earned_at, a
    quest's completed_at), then by person id, so the order is always
    the same.
  - Day labels from the viewer's phone date: Today, Yesterday, then
    "Tue, Oct 13". Never a clock time: the server only knows the day.

  PAGES
  - 20 lines a page, the next page asked for with the last line's
    (day, time, id), never by page number, so nothing is skipped or
    repeated when new lines arrive in between.
  - The next page comes with a "Show older" button under the list, not
    by scrolling: Friends is one scrolling page with the requests and
    the search under the board.
  - At most 30 days back (choice ACT-3). Past that: "That's everything
    from the last 30 days."

  REFRESHING
  - Loads when the view opens, when the app comes back to the
    foreground with the view open (not again within 30 seconds), and on
    pull-to-refresh.
  - A refresh loads the first page again and replaces the list.
    Pull-to-refresh always does. An automatic one, when older pages are
    open, keeps the list as it is and shows a "New activity" button at
    the top, which swaps in the fresh page, instead of jumping.
  - Keeps only the newest answer for the signed-in account, like the
    tab badges; an answer for a previous account is thrown away.
  - No live updates and no checking in the background (choice ACT-4).
  - A failed load keeps what is on screen, with "Couldn't refresh. Pull
    to try again." above it. Offline the same. A small "Updated 5m ago"
    shows after a refresh, hidden when it was under a minute.

======================================================================
6. SCREENS
======================================================================

  FRIENDS
  - Three buttons at the top: Today, Last 7 days (the rankings, as
    now) and Activity (ACT-1). Last 7 days stays the default.
  - Each line: character head, display name, what they did, the badge,
    and the day label on the right.
  - Tapping the line opens the player card, the same as tapping a
    ranking row, with today's steps (the board loads the Today ranking
    for it).

  A GROUP (LATER)
  - The same Activity button on the group screen, the same lines, plus the
    group's quest lines. A member's line opens the card from the group
    (group_member_card), as the group's ranking does.

  EMPTY AND ERROR STATES
  - No friends: "Add friends to see what they're up to." with the
    existing Add friend button.
  - Friends, but nothing in 30 days: "Nothing yet. When friends reach
    their goal or unlock achievements, it shows here."
  - A group with nothing: "Nothing in this group yet."
  - The first load: the same loading look as the rankings.

  ACCESSIBILITY
  - Each line reads as one sentence: "Sam, reached their goal and
    unlocked first 10k, yesterday."
  - Lines grow with the phone's text size, and each line keeps at
    least a 44-point touch area.

======================================================================
7. DATABASE
======================================================================

  NO NEW TABLES
  - The lines are worked out when asked from coin_transactions
    ('daily_goal' rows), daily_steps, user_achievements, quests and
    quest_party. Nothing is written when someone walks, so there is
    nothing to deduplicate and no second record that could disagree
    with the first.
  - Two indexes, if the plan shows they're needed: coin_transactions
    on (user_id, reason, ref), which the unique key already gives, and
    user_achievements on (user_id, earned_on).

  FUNCTIONS (APP-FACING)
  - friends_activity(my_today, before): your lines and your friends'
    lines (section 3), 20 at most, newer than 30 days before my_today,
    older than the "before" position when given. Each line: day,
    person (id, display name, avatar), goal (true or false), coins
    (yours only), achievement ids, and the time used for the order.
  - group_activity(gid, my_today, before), LATER: the same for one group,
    plus quest lines (quest id, type, party size). Refuses with
    NOT_A_MEMBER unless the caller is a current member, like the other
    group functions.
  - my_today is the phone's date, checked with is_today_somewhere as
    the other functions do (BAD_DAY otherwise), so the 30 days can't
    be stretched.

  PERMISSIONS
  - Security definer, "set search_path = ''", fully qualified names,
    owned by the migration's role, like the existing functions. Each
    starts with is_active_account. "revoke all ... from public, anon,
    authenticated", then execute granted back to authenticated only.
  - coin_transactions and user_achievements keep their read rules: the
    app still can't read anyone else's rows directly. These functions
    return only the fields in section 2.

  COST
  - A page looks at at most 30 days for each friend or member: small
    for the group sizes the app has. If it ever isn't, a summary table
    filled by claim_daily_rewards can replace the joins later without
    changing what the functions return.

======================================================================
8. WHAT IS NOT ON THE BOARD (AND WHY)
======================================================================

  - Personal bests: the app has no such rule, and the day achievements
    (10,000, 20,000, 30,000) already mark the big days. Adding one
    would be a new reward rule, not a display.
  - Every streak of 7 days, every time: streaks are shown, not
    celebrated, apart from the streak achievements (7, 30, 100).
    Those are on the board.
  - Coins on their own: the check-in bonus, quest coins, the shop
    refresh payout. They say nothing to friends, and the wallet is private.
  - Character and outfit changes, passing someone on the road, new
    friends, joining a group: possible later, each as its own choice
    (section 11).

======================================================================
9. TRICKY CASES
======================================================================

  - Two phones, or a fast double pull: each load is a plain read, so
    two at once give the same answer; the newest one wins on screen.
  - A friend who walked "today" in a time zone ahead of yours: their
    line says Today on their date, which is your tomorrow. Shown as
    Today: the label is about their day, and it never shows a time.
  - A friend unfriends you while you're looking: their lines stay on
    screen until the next load, then go. Nothing about them is added
    meanwhile.
  - A friend turns sharing off: no lines from the next load. Turning it
    back on brings back their lines from the last 30 days, since the
    records were never deleted.
  - Someone deletes every day they uploaded: no goal lines; their
    achievement lines stay (they're on their player card too).
  - Account deleted: the rows go with the account (on delete cascade),
    so the lines do too.
  - A quest won by a group you joined afterwards: shown, since it's
    the group's history, like the group's quest list.
  - An old app that doesn't know a new achievement id: the line leaves
    that id out (the earnedList rule); a line with nothing left is not
    shown.

======================================================================
10. CHOICES (ACT-1 AND ACT-2 DECIDED OCTOBER 2026; THE REST SUGGESTED)
======================================================================

  ACT-1  Where it lives (DECIDED): a third button in Friends, next to
         Today and Last 7 days, not a new tab. Groups get it later.
    Why:  five tabs already, and the board is about the same people
    the rankings are about.

  ACT-2  One line per person per day (DECIDED): the goal plus that
         day's achievements, and (with groups, later) one per won
         quest.
    Why:  a busy day doesn't flood the list, and nothing has to be
    merged or deduplicated.

  ACT-3  30 days back.
    Why:  enough to scroll a month, and it keeps every load small.

  ACT-4  No live updates: open, foreground and pull-to-refresh only.
    Why:  nothing on the board is urgent, and it keeps the battery and
    the database quiet. Live updates can be added later (section 11).

  ACT-5  No pushes and no tab badge.
    Why:  a badge means "something needs you" (notifications blueprint,
    section 5), and a push for every friend's goal would be noise.

  ACT-6  Coins shown on your own lines only; no step counts on anyone's.
    Why:  the wallet is private, and friends only see today's and the
    week's totals now.

  ACT-7  A deleted day takes its goal line with it.
    Why:  deleting a day is how a person removes it; the coins stay
    as now.

  When these are settled, copy them into step-tracker-decisions.txt
  as a new section, like the other blueprints.

12. FROM THE EARLIER DRAFT
======================================================================

  The earlier draft of this file asked for several things that would
  have changed existing rules. They are left out; this is where each
  one went.

  - Taking back achievements and coins when a day is corrected: no.
    Achievements are kept for good and paid coins are never taken back
    (achievements section 2, claim_daily_rewards). The board shows what
    was paid.
  - Pending coins and a new coin ledger: no. Daily coins can be spent
    at once, and coin_transactions with its unique (user, reason, ref)
    already stops a reward being paid twice.
  - "+10 coins for 10,000 steps": coins are paid at the daily goal
    (150), and 10,000 pays nothing, so a goal line is the only one with
    coins on it.
  - An approved list of step sources, and Android records of unknown
    origin: the existing filters (no typed-in steps, at most 100,000 a
    day) stay as they are.
  - An activities table with dedupe keys, update cursors and removal
    messages: not needed, since every line is read live (section 7).
  - Cheers on lines: now kudos, in their own blueprint
    (step-tracker-kudos.txt): a clap on each line, an in-app notice,
    no pushes (so ACT-5 still holds).
  - A "synced 3h ago" label: the board shows past days only, never
    live progress, so nothing on it claims to be live.
  - Personal bests and repeated streak milestones: section 8.

13. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  What shows
  - A day at the goal gives one line; the same day's achievement joins
    that line, never a second one; an achievement on a day without the
    goal gives a line of its own.
  - Coins only on your own lines, with the row's amount; no step counts
    anywhere in the answer.
  - A won quest gives one line in that group's view and none in
    Friends; a failed or cancelled quest gives none.
  - A day corrected below the goal keeps its line (the coin row stays);
    a deleted day loses its goal part and keeps its achievements.
  - A late day appears under its own day.
  - Days before goal_is_coin_target_from give no goal line.

  Who sees what
  - Friends: a friend yes; a stranger, a former friend, blocked either
    way, paused or hidden, under-age or unfinished, not sharing: no.
  - Group: a member yes, including a non-friend and (SAFE-1) a blocked
    member; a former member no; a caller who isn't a member gets
    NOT_A_MEMBER.
  - Signed out or inactive caller: refused.
  - No EXECUTE for public or anon; authenticated only; security
    definer with an empty search_path.

  Order and pages
  - Newest day first, the same order every time; ties broken by
    person id.
  - Paging with "before": every line exactly once, none skipped, even
    when a new line arrives between pages.
  - Nothing older than 30 days; a my_today that isn't today anywhere
    is refused with BAD_DAY.

  APP (npm test)
  - The line text for each mix (goal, one achievement, several, both,
    yours with coins, a quest), and leaving out unknown ids.
  - Merging a fresh first page into the list, the "New activity" pill,
    keeping the list on a failed load, the 30-second foreground limit,
    and dropping a person's lines after blocking or removing them.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - Reach the goal: a friend's phone shows the line after pulling to
    refresh; then unlock an achievement the same day: still one line.
  - Unfriend: the lines go on the next load. Block from the card: they
    go at once.
  - Airplane mode: the list stays, with the "Couldn't refresh" note.
  - Large text and VoiceOver read each line as one sentence.
  - Android (emulator, since there is only an iPhone to test on): the
    same list and states.
