STEP TRACKER - CHEERS (BLUEPRINT)
Let people send a friend or group mate a quick cheer ("Keep going!",
"Goal crushed!") with one tap, and let the other person see it on
their road and cheer back.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what a cheer is, who can send one to whom, the limits, what
the person receiving it sees, and the choices to make before building.

Builds on what the app already has: friends and groups, the player
card (src/components/player-card.tsx), blocking and can_see()
(20261104000000_blocking.sql), push notifications sent from the
database (20261102000000_push_notifications.sql), the Notifications
setting on Profile, and the character on the Today road.

======================================================================
1. THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE
======================================================================

  - Open a friend's or group mate's player card and tap "Cheer". Pick
    one of six pixel-art stickers. Done.
  - They get a push, "@sam cheered you: Keep going!", and the next
    time they open Today, the sticker pops up above their character
    on the road, with Sam's name.
  - One tap to cheer back.
  - Only set stickers, never typed text. Nothing new to moderate, and
    it stays safe for 13-17 year olds (step-tracker-safety.txt).
  - One cheer per person every 20 hours. Cheers are a nice moment,
    not a chat, and they can't be used to spam someone.
  - Cheers pay no coins, to either person (section 6).
  - Built in three phases. Phase 1 is the whole feature on its own;
    Phases 2 and 3 make it quicker to use and add to it.

======================================================================
2. THE STICKERS
======================================================================

  Six to start. Each one is a small pixel-art sticker in the same style
  as the characters (no system emoji in the app itself), plus a short
  line of text. The database stores only the sticker's id.

    id             text              push emoji
    nice_walk      Nice walk!        👏
    keep_going     Keep going!       💪
    on_fire        On fire!          🔥
    goal_crushed   Goal crushed!     🏆
    lets_walk      Let's walk!       👟
    proud          Proud of you!     ⭐

  - Friendly only. Fitbit has "taunts" too; leave them out. Between
    teens and strangers in groups, a taunt is too easy to read badly.
  - The list of ids is a check in the database, so adding a sticker is
    a migration plus its art, and an old app version that doesn't know
    a new id shows it as "nice_walk" instead of breaking.
  - The emoji is only for the push text, which can't show pixel art.

======================================================================
3. WHO CAN CHEER WHOM
======================================================================

  You can cheer someone when ALL of these are true at that moment:
  - You are both active accounts and neither has blocked the other
    (can_see()).
  - You are friends (is_friend()) OR you are both members of the same
    group (shares_group()).
  - You have BOTH agreed to share your steps. Friends and Groups are
    already closed until you do (sharing-required.tsx); a cheer
    should not reach someone who hasn't opened those either. The
    database has has_sharing_consent() for the caller only, so it
    needs a server-only version that takes a person, like
    notification_counts_for().
  - It's not you.
  - You haven't cheered them in the last 20 hours (section 5).
  - You haven't sent 30 cheers in the last 24 hours (section 5).

  When a send is refused because of the first three rules, the answer
  is the same NOT_ALLOWED in every case, the way can_see() never says
  WHY you can't see someone. Being told "they blocked you" or "they
  turned off sharing" is not something a cheer should reveal.

  Group mates who aren't friends CAN cheer each other (decision
  CHEER-2). Group quests are where most of the teamwork happens, and
  a set sticker gives a stranger nothing to misuse. Blocking removes
  it in one tap.

======================================================================
4. PHASE 1 - SEND, RECEIVE, CHEER BACK (START HERE)
======================================================================

  THE DATABASE
  - A "cheers" table, one row per cheer:
      id          number, made by the database
      from_id     the sender (deleted with their account)
      to_id       the person cheered (deleted with their account)
      sticker     one of the ids in section 2
      created_at  when it was sent
      seen_at     when the person cheered saw it on Today, or empty
      pushed      whether a push was sent for it (for the push limit)
  - Read rule: you can read rows you sent or received, nothing else.
  - The app can never insert, change or delete rows directly. It
    goes through three functions:

    send_cheer(person, sticker)
      Checks every rule in section 3 and the limits in section 5,
      then saves the cheer. Takes lock_pair() first, so two taps (or
      two phones) at the same moment can't both get through the
      20-hour rule. Answers with when the next cheer to that person
      is allowed, or one of:
        NOT_ALLOWED    any rule in section 3 (never says which)
        TOO_SOON       you cheered them in the last 20 hours
        DAILY_LIMIT    30 sent in the last 24 hours
        NOT_SHARING    you haven't agreed to share your steps
        BAD_STICKER    not an id from section 2

    cheer_status(person)
      For the player card: can I cheer them right now, and if not
      because of the 20 hours, when can I. Gives NOT_ALLOWED the same
      way send_cheer does, so the card just hides the button.

    my_cheers()
      Cheers you received in the last 24 hours, newest first, with
      each sender's username and display name. Leaves out anyone you
      can't see now (can_see()), so a sender who was blocked or
      paused after cheering disappears from the list. Also says, for
      each one, whether you can cheer them back right now.

    mark_cheers_seen(up_to_id)
      Sets seen_at on your received cheers up to that id. Only your
      own rows.

  SENDING (THE PLAYER CARD)
  - The card gets a "Cheer" button next to Close, for anyone but you,
    wherever the card opens from (Friends, a group, or tapping a
    character on the Today road),
    shown only when cheer_status() says it's allowed. It loads with
    the rest of the card (loadPlayerProfile in src/api/players.ts,
    and the group version), and a failed check just hides the button.
  - Tapping it shows the six stickers in a small grid. Tap one: it
    sends, the sticker does a short bounce, and the button turns into
    "Cheered" (disabled). No confirm step: a cheer can't do harm.
  - Already cheered: the button shows "Cheered" with "again in 6h"
    under it.
  - TOO_SOON or DAILY_LIMIT from the server (another phone got there
    first): show the same "Cheered" state, or "You've sent a lot of
    cheers today" for the daily limit. No alert box.
  - Any other failure: "Couldn't send. Try again." under the grid,
    and the grid stays open.

  RECEIVING (TODAY)
  - Today asks my_cheers() when it loads and when the app comes back
    to the foreground, alongside the step refresh, keeping only the
    newest answer for the signed-in account (the same rule as the
    tab badges, step-tracker-notifications.txt section 2).
  - Unseen cheers pop up one after another above YOUR character on
    the road: the sticker in a little speech bubble with "@sam" under
    it, about 2 seconds each, at most 5 (then "+3 more"). Then
    mark_cheers_seen() with the newest id shown. If that call fails,
    they simply show again next time.
  - A small chip under the step count, "3 cheers", opens the Cheers
    sheet while any cheers from the last 24 hours exist. Hidden when
    there are none.
  - The Cheers sheet: one row each, sticker, name, "2h ago", and a
    "Cheer back" button (disabled with "Cheered" when the 20 hours
    say no). Cheer back opens the same sticker grid. Tapping a name
    opens their player card.
  - Signed out, or not sharing your steps: no chip, no bubbles.

  THE PUSH
  - Sent by the database, like friend requests: a trigger after each
    new cheer calls a server-only push_cheer(), wrapped so a failed
    push never stops the cheer being saved (like on_friend_request).
  - Before sending, push_cheer() checks again at that moment:
      - the person cheered can still see the sender (active, not
        blocked either way)
      - the Notifications setting is on, AND the new "Cheers" setting
        is on (below)
      - fewer than 10 cheer pushes to this person in the last 24
        hours (section 5); past that, the cheer is saved quietly and
        shows on Today
      - not night time where the phone is (below)
  - Text:  title "@sam cheered you"   body "Keep going! 💪"
  - No sound. A cheer is nice to see, not worth a ding.
  - The app icon number: the push carries the SAME total the friend
    request push uses (friend requests waiting). Cheers are not
    counted in it; they don't need an action. Leaving "badge" out
    would be fine on iPhone too, but sending the real total keeps the
    number right.
  - Tapping it opens Today with the Cheers sheet open.

  NO PUSHES AT NIGHT
  - The server doesn't know anyone's time zone (recommendations 3d
    is still open). The cheap fix: the app sends the phone's time zone
    with register_push_device(), which it already calls on every
    launch, and push_devices keeps it per device.
  - A cheer push to a device where it's between 9 PM and 8 AM is
    skipped. The cheer still shows on Today in the morning.
  - A device with no time zone saved (older app version) gets the
    push as now.

  SETTINGS (PROFILE, UNDER NOTIFICATIONS)
  - "Cheers" on/off, default on. It turns off cheer PUSHES only.
    Cheers still arrive and show on Today. It goes in
    notification_settings as its own column, with its own setter
    like set_push_enabled().
  - The main Notifications switch turning off also stops cheer pushes.
  - There is no "don't let people cheer me" switch in Phase 1 (decision
    CHEER-4). Blocking covers a person; the push setting covers the
    noise.

  CLEANING UP
  - Cheers older than 30 days are deleted by a nightly job (pg_cron,
    like push-cleanup). Nothing reads them after 24 hours in Phase 1;
    the extra days are for Phase 3's counts and for looking into a
    problem.
  - block_user() deletes every cheer between the two people, both
    directions, in the same step.
  - Account deletion: the rows go with the account (on delete
    cascade).
  - Removing a friend, or leaving the group you shared: old cheers stay
    (they were fine when sent), but you can't send new ones, and
    my_cheers() still shows them for the rest of the 24 hours. Small,
    and not worth special code.

======================================================================
5. LIMITS (AND WHY THESE NUMBERS)
======================================================================

  ONE CHEER PER PERSON EVERY 20 HOURS
  - 20 hours, not "per day", because there is no single day: the phone
    counts days in its own time and the server in its own (recommend-
    ations 3d). A rolling 20 hours means "about once a day" for
    everyone, with no midnight reset to argue about, and lets someone
    who cheers at 9 PM do it again at 6 PM the next day.
  - Counted per direction: Sam cheering you doesn't stop you cheering
    Sam back.

  30 CHEERS SENT IN 24 HOURS
  - Well above what one person cheering their friends and group mates
    once each would send, and low enough that a script or a bored
    person can't cheer a whole big group again and again.

  10 CHEER PUSHES RECEIVED IN 24 HOURS
  - Someone popular in three big groups could get 30 pings a day.
    After 10, cheers are saved quietly and wait on Today.

  All three are checked on the server only; the app shows what the
  server said. All three are kept as numbers in one place in the
  migration, easy to change.

======================================================================
6. CHOICES MADE (EASY TO CHANGE)
======================================================================

  CHEER-1  Set stickers only, never typed text.
    Why:  nothing to moderate or report, safe for teens, and it fits
    the game. A typed message is a chat, which is its own big job.

  CHEER-2  Friends AND group mates can cheer each other.
    Why:  groups are where people walk together. A set sticker is
    harmless from a stranger, and blocking is one tap.
    Other option: friends only. Simpler to explain, but quieter.

  CHEER-3  No coins for sending or receiving.
    Why:  coins for cheers can be farmed with a second account (cheer
    each other every day), and coin rewards make cheers feel like a
    chore. The coin economy is balanced around steps
    (step-tracker-coins-items.txt).
    Other option: an achievement later (Phase 3), which pays an item
    once, not coins every day.

  CHEER-4  No "who can cheer me" setting in Phase 1.
    Why:  blocking and the push setting cover the real cases. Add
    "Friends only / Friends and groups / No one" if people ask.

  CHEER-5  One cheer per person every 20 hours; 30 sent and 10 pushes
           received per 24 hours.
    Why:  section 5.

  CHEER-6  No tab badge for cheers.
    Why:  a badge means "something needs you" (notifications
    blueprint, section 5). Cheers are to enjoy, not to act on. The
    bubbles on Today and the chip are enough.

  CHEER-7  No sound, and no pushes from 9 PM to 8 AM in the phone's
           time.
    Why:  a small nice thing should never wake a teenager up.

  CHEER-8  No taunts. Friendly stickers only.

  When these are settled, copy them into step-tracker-decisions.txt
  as a new section, like the other blueprints.

======================================================================
7. PHASE 2 - QUICKER TO SEND
======================================================================

  - Cheer from the ranking: a small sticker button at the end of each
    leaderboard row (friends and group). Group rows already swipe to
    remove members for the owner, so it must be a button, not a
    swipe. The rows need cheer_status for each person, so the
    leaderboard functions add a "can cheer" field instead of one call
    per row.
  - A suggested sticker, picked from what the card already shows:
      reached their goal today          Goal crushed!
      streak of 7 days or more          On fire!
      after 5 PM and under half         Keep going!
      otherwise                         Nice walk!
    The grid opens with it highlighted; one more tap sends. Pure
    function, unit tested, using only what the person is already
    allowed to see.
  - Live updates while the app is open: listen for new rows in
    cheers sent to you (the read rule already limits it to yours) and
    show the bubble right away. Ask my_cheers() again on every
    reconnect, the same as the notification counts.

======================================================================
8. PHASE 3 - OPTIONAL
======================================================================

  - Cheer the whole party: on a group quest card, one button that
    cheers every member you can still cheer. Counts as one cheer
    each, so the limits still hold.
  - Cheers on the player card: "42 cheers received" (from a running
    total per person, since rows are deleted after 30 days).
  - Achievements: "Cheerleader" (cheered on 7 different days) and
    "Fan club" (cheered by 10 different people), each paying a reward
    item once (step-tracker-achievements.txt).
  - Seasonal stickers for events, if events happen (a pumpkin for
    October).

======================================================================
9. TRICKY CASES
======================================================================

  - Two taps, or two phones, at once: lock_pair() means only one cheer
    is saved; the other gets TOO_SOON and shows "Cheered".
  - Cheer, then the sender blocks the person (or the other way): the
    rows are deleted at once; if the person cheered had the bubble
    waiting, it's gone.
  - Sender's account paused or hidden after cheering: my_cheers()
    leaves them out (can_see()); push_cheer() checks again before
    sending, so a push still in the queue isn't sent.
  - The person cheered turns sharing off: they can't be cheered and
    don't see the chip; their old cheers come back if they turn it on
    within the 24 hours.
  - Cheered from a group, then one of you leaves the group and you
    aren't friends: no new cheers. "Cheer back" in the sheet is
    disabled, because my_cheers() says it's not allowed.
  - A shared phone and an account switch: the bubbles and the chip
    belong to the signed-in account; an answer for the previous
    account is thrown away (the notifications rule).
  - An old app that doesn't know a new sticker: shows "nice_walk".
  - A push arrives for a cheer that was then deleted (blocked):
    tapping it opens Today, which simply doesn't show it.
  - Push failed or the phone was off: the cheer is still on Today.
  - Cheering someone you can only see through a group card (not a
    friend, not sharing history): allowed (CHEER-2); the card shows
    nothing more about them than it does today.

======================================================================
10. TESTS
======================================================================

  Database (supabase/tests/database)
  - Every rule in section 3: friend, group mate, neither, blocked
    either way, paused, not sharing (either person), yourself. Every
    refusal from section 3 gives the same NOT_ALLOWED.
  - 20 hours per direction, 30 sent, bad sticker id.
  - Read rules: can't read other people's cheers; can't insert,
    change or delete rows directly, only through the functions.
  - mark_cheers_seen() touches only your own rows.
  - block_user() deletes cheers both ways; account deletion removes
    them.
  - push_cheer(): skipped when either push setting is off, when
    blocked, past 10 pushes, and at night in the device's time zone;
    can't be called from the app.
  - The nightly cleanup deletes only rows older than 30 days.

  Concurrency (supabase/tests/concurrency)
  - Two send_cheer() calls for the same pair at the same moment: one
    row.

  App
  - Unit tests: the sticker list (every id has text, art and an emoji;
    an unknown id falls back), and the Phase 2 suggestion.
  - On a real iPhone: push arrives with the right text and no sound,
    tapping it opens the Cheers sheet, nothing arrives at night, the
    icon number doesn't change.
  - Dev scripts: let the fake friends send cheers, so the bubbles and
    the sheet can be seen without a second phone.
