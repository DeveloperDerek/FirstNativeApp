STEP TRACKER - TODAY MINIMAP (BLUEPRINT)
A horizontal progress bar under Today's road that shows the whole day's
road at once: a minimap of the track, with milestone markers and a
small avatar icon for each person on it.
Last checked: October 2026

Status: built (October 2026). There is no code in this file. It
describes what was built, where, and how to check it. The decisions
are recorded in section 8.

One change from the first draft: everyone's icons stand ABOVE the bar
(yours higher, with a pointer), not others below it. Icons above and
below would have made the strip about 90 points tall; this way it is
68.

Everything here lives in these files:
  src/track/Minimap.tsx      NEW: the bar, markers, icons, viewport
  src/track/minimap-math.ts  NEW: the math (no UI, unit tested)
  src/track/minimap-math.test.ts  NEW: tests for the math (npm test)
  src/track/StepTrack.tsx    shows the minimap and wires it to the road
  src/avatar/Avatar.tsx      a head-only crop of the character (HeadIcon)

Nothing changes in the database. No new art files: the minimap is
drawn from Views and the existing character layers.

======================================================================
1. HOW THE ROAD WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  - StepTrack draws a road wider than the screen, scrolling sideways.
    1,000 steps is 100 points of road (PX_PER_STEP = 0.1), with 64
    points of empty road at each end (ROAD_PAD).
  - The road ends at 10,000 until someone passes it, then grows in
    5,000s (scaleMax). Nobody moves when it grows.
  - Landmarks stand every 5,000 steps: start (0), mid (5,000), goal
    (10,000) and far (15,000), and the far one repeats every 5,000
    after that (landmarksFor).
  - The goal flag stands at 10,000. Each player's own daily goal is
    their coin target (7,000 to 10,000), shown as a percentage in the
    header ("62% of 8,000 goal"), but nowhere on the road.
  - Up to 30 people are drawn (pickShown: the top 30 and you). The
    road opens centered on you and follows your count until you
    scroll by hand.
  - On a phone the screen shows about 390 points: about 3,900 steps of
    road. Someone at 4,000 and someone at 12,000 are never on screen
    together, and nothing shows how far along the day's road you are.

======================================================================
2. WHAT THE MINIMAP IS
======================================================================

One strip, the full width of the screen, that never scrolls:

     0      2.5k     5k      7.5k  ★ 10k            15k
     ├───────┼────────◆───────┼──█──⚑────────────────◆
     ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░                         (your fill)
            (A)          (You)    (B)(C)+2
               └──── [   what's on screen   ] ────┘

  a. THE BAR. Spans 0 to the road's end (the same scaleMax as the
     road), so 10,000 sits in the same place on both until someone
     passes it. Drawn pixel-art style like the rest of the app: a
     track 4 points tall with a 2-point outline, square ends, whole
     points only.

  b. YOUR FILL. From 0 to your steps, in a brighter color than the
     track. It turns gold once you reach your daily goal.

  c. MILESTONE MARKERS, standing on the bar:
       - each landmark (0, 5k, 10k, 15k, then every 5k): a 6 x 6
         pixel diamond, filled once you have passed it
       - the 10,000 flag: a small pixel flag, as on the road
       - YOUR daily goal (7,000 to 10,000): a star, drawn above the
         bar, so you can see where you get your coins. When your goal
         is 10,000 the star and the flag share the spot (star in front)
       - tick labels every 2,500 under the bar ("0", "2.5k", "5k"...),
         the same labels as the road's distance markers. When the road
         is long (20k+), only every 5k gets a label, so they never crowd

  d. AVATAR ICONS. A head-only crop of each person's character (hair,
     hat, face; their real colors) standing on their spot on the bar:
       - you: a little higher than everyone else, with a pixel pointer
         down to your spot, always drawn on top
       - everyone else: just above the bar
       - icons that would overlap gather into one icon with a count
         badge ("+2"): the leader of the group shows, the rest count
       - solo ("Just me"): only you

  e. THE VIEWPORT. A bracket over the part of the bar that is on screen
     right now. It slides as the road is scrolled, so the minimap
     always shows where you are looking.

  f. TOUCH:
       - tap anywhere on the bar: the road scrolls there (animated)
       - drag along the bar: the road follows your finger, like
         scrubbing a video
       - tap an icon (or a "+2" group): the road scrolls to center on
         them. Opening someone's profile stays on the road, where
         tapping a character already does it.
     After a tap or drag the road no longer auto-follows your count
     (as when you scroll the road yourself).

======================================================================
3. WHERE IT GOES
======================================================================

  Directly under the road, on the ground color, fixed (it does not
  scroll with the road or with the list below):

    [ header on the sky: coins, Today, count, goal % ]
    [ the road (scrolls sideways) ]
    [ the road's distance markers ]
    [ THE MINIMAP ]                      <- new, 68 points tall
    [ Who: Just me / Friends / groups ... the list scrolls ]

  It belongs to StepTrack, so it shows exactly when the road does: not
  when Friends or a group is picked without sharing on (the road is
  hidden then too).

  68 points: the icons (34), your pointer, the bar and the labels.
  The road itself does not get any shorter.

======================================================================
4. HOW IT WORKS (the code)
======================================================================

  4a. minimap-math.ts (pure functions, no UI)
      - barX(steps, max, barWidth): where a step count sits on the bar.
        Linear from 0 to max, rounded to whole points, inset so an
        icon at 0 or at the end isn't cut off.
      - stepsAt(x, max, barWidth): the reverse, for taps and drags.
      - viewportOnBar(scrollX, viewport, max, barWidth): the bracket's
        left and width, from the road's scroll position. Uses the
        road's own math (centerFor, roadWidth in scale.ts), so the
        two can't drift apart.
      - groupIcons(people, barWidth, iconWidth): the overlap rule.
        Sorts by position; anyone closer than 0.8 icon widths to the
        group before joins it. Returns each group's x, its leader
        (most steps) and how many others. You are never put in a
        group (your icon is in its own row above the bar).
      - labelsFor(max): tick labels every 2,500, or every 5,000 once
        the road is 20,000 or longer.

  4b. Minimap.tsx
      - Props: walkers (the same list the road draws), max, the theme,
        your daily goal, the road's scrollX (a shared value StepTrack
        already keeps for the hills) and the viewport width, plus
        onJump(steps) and onScrub(steps).
      - The bar, fill, markers and labels are plain Views, sized in
        whole points, in the theme's ink, ground and sky colors.
      - The viewport bracket is a Reanimated view whose left and width
        follow scrollX on the UI thread: no re-render while scrolling.
      - Touch uses one Pressable for taps and a pan gesture for drags
        (react-native-gesture-handler, already used by the
        leaderboard and the draggable rows). Dragging calls scrollTo on the road without
        animation, so the road keeps up with the finger.
      - Icons are positioned with the same "walk to the new spot"
        animation the road uses (600 ms) when counts change.

  4c. HeadIcon (in Avatar.tsx)
      - The same layers Avatar draws, in a Canvas that shows only the
        top of the sprite (the 8 rows of headroom and rows 0 to 25:
        hats, hair, face), at scale 1: 32 x 34 points.
      - Each layer image is already decoded once and shared
        (loadSprite), so 30 icons cost no more decoding than the
        leaderboard does today.
      - Held items and capes are left out, so a balloon or a wand
        doesn't stick into the next icon.

  4d. StepTrack.tsx
      - Renders <Minimap> under the distance markers, passing what it
        already computes (walkers, max, scrollX, viewport).
      - onJump / onScrub call scrollRef.current.scrollTo with the
        road's x for those steps (centerFor(steps) - viewport / 2),
        and set userScrolled so the road stops auto-following.
      - The daily goal is passed in from Today (index.tsx already has
        it as dailyGoal) as a new optional prop, myGoal.

======================================================================
5. ACCESSIBILITY
======================================================================

  - The minimap is one element for screen readers, read as a summary:
    "Today's road: you are at 6,200 steps, 1,800 to your goal and 3,800
    to the flag. 2nd of 5." In solo, without the ranking.
  - It is "adjustable": swiping up or down jumps the road to the next
    or previous milestone, so the map can be explored without dragging.
  - The icons, markers and labels inside are hidden from screen
    readers (the summary says it all, and the leaderboard below lists
    everyone).
  - Reduce Motion: icons jump to their new spots instead of sliding;
    the road scrolls without animation when tapped.

======================================================================
7. HOW TO CHECK IT
======================================================================

  - npm test: the new minimap tests pass with the old ones.
  - Solo, 0 steps: only your icon, at the left end; nothing filled.
  - Solo, past your goal: the fill is gold and the star is filled.
  - A group where someone has 23,000: the bar ends at 25,000, labels
    every 5k, and the 10,000 flag moves left on the bar (the bar is
    the whole road).
  - Five people within 300 steps of each other: one icon and "+4"
    below the bar; you stay above.
  - Scroll the road: the bracket slides with it, smoothly.
  - Tap the far end of the bar: the road scrolls there. Drag along the
    bar: the road follows.
  - VoiceOver: the summary reads correctly; swiping up and down moves
    the road from milestone to milestone.
  - Small screen (iPhone SE) and large: labels don't overlap, icons
    stay inside the bar's ends.

8. DECISIONS FOR YOU
======================================================================

  Decided October 2026:
  1. Place: under the road, on the ground.
  2. Milestones: the pixel diamonds, flag and star (not tiny landmarks).
  3. Tapping an icon scrolls the road to them; profiles open from the
     road as before.
  4. Your daily goal is a star on the bar.
