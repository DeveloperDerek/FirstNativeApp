STEP TRACKER - COINS AND ITEMS (ECONOMY REVIEW)
Everything about coins in one place:
  A. Every way to earn coins
  B. Every way to spend coins
  C. Every item and what it costs
  D. The rebalance: what changed and why
Last checked: October 2026

This is a reference and a design record only. There is no code in this
file. Sections 1 to 4 describe the economy as it is built now (the
numbers come from supabase/migrations). Section 5 records the
rebalance: each change, the old and new numbers, and the reasoning.

CHANGED IN OCTOBER 2026 (step-tracker-decisions.txt, COIN-1 to COIN-5)
  - New: a 300-coin welcome bonus with a player's first check-in.
  - Daily step reward 100 -> 150. Shop refresh payout 25 -> 10.
  - Quests: 1 coin per 100 steps (was 200), daily cap 400 (was 600).
  - Prices: pets up, maps down, plain bottoms down, odd prices rounded.
  Built in 20261115000000_coin_rebalance.sql. Still open: something to
  spend coins on once you own everything (5f, COIN-F).

======================================================================
1. THE BASICS
======================================================================

  - Coins live on the server (wallets). The app never writes them: it
    calls a database function and the database decides.
  - Every coin movement is a row in coin_transactions, with a reason.
    The unique (user, reason, ref) key stops anything paying twice.
  - Earning coins needs step sharing turned on (the consent screen).
    Without it, every earning function pays 0.
  - New players start with 0 coins, and get 300 with their first
    check-in, which the app makes as soon as sharing is turned on.
  - The "shop day" runs on Los Angeles time for everyone.

======================================================================
2. EVERY WAY TO EARN COINS
======================================================================

  2a. DAILY STEP REWARD .......................... 150 coins a day
      - Paid for each day your steps reach that day's coin target.
      - The target is 7,000 to 10,000 steps (in 500s), different for
        each player each day, fixed once shown (decision GOAL-2).
      - It IS the daily goal (GOAL-16): reaching your goal for the day
        is what pays. Streaks pay nothing.
      - Late uploads still pay: any day in the last 7 days counts.
      - Function: claim_daily_rewards()      reason: 'daily_goal'

  2b. CHECK-IN BONUS ......................... 10 coins every 6 hours
      - Collected by opening the app. Server clock, so changing the
        phone's time does nothing.
      - At most 4 a day (40 coins); 2 or 3 is realistic.
      - Function: claim_checkin()            reason: 'checkin'

  2c. WELCOME BONUS ................................ 300 coins, once
      - Paid with a player's first check-in ever, on top of the 10.
        The app shows "Welcome! +310 coins to start you off".
      - Players who checked in before the bonus existed don't get it.
      - Function: claim_checkin()            reason: 'welcome'

  2d. SHOP REFRESH ........................... 10 coins every 12 hours
      - Swaps today's 6 shop items for 6 new ones AND pays 10 coins.
      - At most 2 a day (20 coins).
      - Function: refresh_shop()             reason: 'shop_refresh'

  2e. GROUP QUESTS ............... varies, capped at 400 coins a day
      - Paid to each party member when a quest is completed.
      - Formula (decisions QUEST-6 to QUEST-10):
          party steps, counted up to 150% of the goal
          / 100                       (1 coin per 100 steps)
          x size bonus                (x1.2 for 2, +0.2 per member,
                                       at most x2.5)
          / party size                (split equally)
          rounded, at least 10 each
      - Quest goals grow 10% per member after the second, up to 8.
      - One quest at a time per group, 1 hour cooldown after each.
      - Function: quest_advance()            reason: 'quest'

      What a quest actually pays each person (at 150% of the goal,
      the most it can pay):

        Quest     Hours  Party  Goal     Coins each
        Sprint      2      2     8,000      72
        Sprint      2      4     9,600      58
        Sprint      2      8    12,800      58
        Patrol      4      2    15,000     135
        Patrol      4      4    18,000     108
        Patrol      4      8    24,000     108

      (Expedition, Day Trek and Weekend Journey exist but are turned
      off. A 2-member Day Trek would pay 450 and a Weekend Journey
      1,080, both over the 400 cap: look at the cap again before
      turning them on.)

  2f. ONE-OFF AND ADMIN MOVEMENTS (not something players do)
      - 'refund'        paid back when an item became a free starter
                        (hair_long, hair_ponytail, top_sweater_yellow,
                        bottom_skirt_pink)
      - 'grandfathered' items people were wearing when they became paid,
                        recorded at 0 coins
      - 'starter'       pet_dog, given to every new account at sign-up,
                        recorded at 0 coins
      - admin grants    admins own every item, recorded at 0 coins

  2g. THINGS THAT USED TO PAY AND DON'T ANY MORE
      - Streak bonuses: 200 coins at 7 days, 1,000 at 30 days. Removed
        in October 2026 (GOAL-6). Already paid bonuses stayed paid.
      - Achievements never paid coins. Some give an item instead
        (section 4).

  WHAT A DAY IS WORTH

        Player          Daily  Check-ins  Refresh  Quests   Total
        Casual           150      20         10        0      ~180
        Regular          150      30         20       72      ~270
        Very engaged     150      40         20      270      ~480
        Theoretical max  150      40         20      400       610

  Opening the app (check-ins + refresh) pays at most 60 coins a day,
  well under the 150 for walking. (Before the rebalance: 90 against
  100.)

======================================================================
3. EVERY WAY TO SPEND COINS
======================================================================

  There is exactly one: buying an item in the daily shop.

  - The shop shows 6 items, picked per player per day. Never-shown
    items come first, then the ones shown longest ago. Items you own
    never appear.
  - You can only buy what is in today's shop (NOT_IN_TODAYS_SHOP).
  - Buying an item does not bring a replacement until the next refresh
    or the next shop day.
  - Items are bought once and kept forever. Nothing is used up.
  - Function: purchase_item()                reason: 'purchase'

  There is no coin sink after you own everything. See 5f.

5. THE REBALANCE (OCTOBER 2026)
======================================================================

  Each item below was a recommendation in the first version of this
  file. 5a to 5e are built (20261115000000_coin_rebalance.sql); 5f is
  still open.

  5a. A FIRST PURCHASE ON DAY ONE (COIN-1) .................. BUILT
      Change:  one-time welcome bonus of 300 coins with the first
               check-in (reason 'welcome', ref 'once').
      Why:     a new player started at 0 and couldn't buy anything
               until day 2 or 3. The shop is the main reason to keep
               coming back; the first purchase should happen in the
               first session. 300 buys exactly one cheap item, so it
               teaches the loop without giving much away. The app
               checks in as soon as sharing is turned on, so the
               bonus also rewards the consent step coins need anyway.

  5b. WALKING WORTH MORE THAN OPENING THE APP (COIN-2) ...... BUILT
      Change:  daily step reward 100 -> 150.
               Check-in unchanged: 10 every 6 hours.
               Shop refresh payout 25 -> 10 (the refresh itself stays).
      Why:     opens paid up to 90 coins a day against 100 for
               walking. Now it's up to 60 against 150. Walking is the
               main way to earn, which is the point of a step tracker.

  5c. QUESTS PAY LIKE A REAL EFFORT (COIN-3) ................ BUILT
      Change:  1 coin per 200 steps -> 1 coin per 100 steps.
               Daily quest cap 600 -> 400.
      Why:     a 2-person Sprint was 2 hours of shared effort for at
               most 36 coins each, about a third of the daily reward.
                 Sprint, 2 people     36 ->  72 each
                 Patrol, 2 people     68 -> 135 each
                 Patrol, 8 people     54 -> 108 each
               Quests are the social feature; on a day you do one, it
               should feel like the best way to earn. The old cap of
               600 was unreachable (9 Patrols); 400 still allows ~3
               quests a day but stops someone in many groups farming
               them.

  5d. ITEM PRICES (COIN-4) .................................. BUILT
      Pets: the most visible thing a player can buy (it walks beside
      them everywhere) was priced below many hats and held items. The
      snail stays at 500 as the cheap entry pet; the rest went up.
      Maps: the most expensive slot, but only you see yours, and
      3,500 was ~25 days for a casual player. All down 500.
      Bottoms: plain colour swaps, now 300 impulse buys.
      Round prices: 350, 450, 750 and 850 are gone; face_starry 400
      so faces are 300 or 400; outfit_knight 2,500 so the big-prize
      tier is one price.
      Past purchases were not refunded or charged the difference.
      (When items became free starters, buyers were refunded, because
      the item became free for everyone. A price change is different.)
      The full list of changes is marked (*) in section 4.

  5e. A PRICE LADDER FOR NEW ITEMS (COIN-5) .............. DECIDED
      Use these five steps for every new item instead of picking a
      number each time:

        Common       300    colour swaps, plain faces and basics
        Uncommon     500    patterned or styled basics
        Rare         800    stand-out pieces (hats with ears, props)
        Epic       1,500    gold items, crowns, halos, big props
        Legendary  2,500    wings, the best outfits, rare maps

      Pets and maps can sit a step higher than clothes of the same
      feel, since each is one slot that's always on show. Existing
      items between steps (400, 600, 700, 900...) stay as they are.

  5f. A COIN SINK FOR WHEN THERE'S NOTHING LEFT TO BUY (COIN-F) . OPEN
      Problem: items are bought once and kept forever. With the new
               payouts a regular player owns everything in about 11
               months, a very engaged one in about 6, and then coins
               pile up with nothing to spend them on. The shop also
               gets thin near the end, because owned items never
               appear. The rebalance makes this sooner than before
               (it was ~14 and ~10 months).
      Options, cheapest to build first:
        - Keep adding items. At ~10 new items a month the catalog
          stays ahead of a regular player.
        - A "buy any item" option at a premium (e.g. 2x price) for an
          item not in today's shop, so long-time players aren't
          waiting on the rotation for the last few.
        - Seasonal items that are only in the shop for a few weeks.
      None of these need a new kind of coin movement: they're all
      'purchase' rows.

  5g. LEFT ALONE ON PURPOSE
      - The 7,000 to 10,000 random coin target (GOAL-2). It keeps the
        daily reward reachable for most people without being the same
        every day.
      - The 7-day back-pay window for late uploads.
      - The check-in amount and cooldown.
      - Streaks paying nothing (GOAL-6). Achievements and their items
        do that job without making streak loss feel like losing money.
      - Free starter items. Both starter looks need to stay free.
