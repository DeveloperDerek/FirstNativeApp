STEP TRACKER - ACHIEVEMENTS (BLUEPRINT)
One-time awards for milestones (first 10,000-step day, a million steps,
a 30-day streak, a first quest won), shown on your profile and on your
player card, and a few clothing items you can only get by earning them.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database, phone and screens need, and
the choices made before building (section 8). Item 2f of
step-tracker-recommendations.txt; step 7 of its suggested order.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  - Rewards are coins only: 100 a day at 10,000 steps, a check-in
    bonus, quest coins, and streak bonuses at 7 and 30 days
    (claim_daily_rewards, run by the phone right after each upload,
    one at a time per person under a lock).
  - What the server keeps, for people who share steps:
      - every uploaded day (daily_steps), with its date. People can
        delete their own days. The phone uploads only the last 7 days,
        so a day can arrive late, but never more than 7 days late.
      - each day's goal (daily_goal_changes). A new goal starts the day
        after it's chosen, so a past day's goal, and so a past streak,
        never changes.
      - quests (with completed_at) and who was in each party
        (quest_party).
    People who don't share have no uploaded days, coins or streaks.
  - Clothing, pets and maps: an item listed in shop_items must be owned
    (user_items) to be worn, enforced on the server
    (check_avatar_items, check_map_theme). An item with active = false
    is never offered in the daily shop and purchase_item refuses it.
    Admins are given every shop_items row automatically.
  - The player card shows a person's character, map, streak and last
    seen, to friends and group mates, through can_see and the group
    rules (which already cover blocked, paused, hidden, under-age and
    unfinished accounts on either side).

======================================================================
2. THE ACHIEVEMENTS (CHOICE 1: DECIDED)
======================================================================

  The first set: 11, all worked out on the server from data it already
  has. Each is earned once and kept for good. (First friend and Joined
  a group were dropped: see section 8.)

    STEPS IN A DAY
      first_10k        First 10K          a day of 10,000 or more
      big_day          Big day            a day of 20,000 or more
      huge_day         Huge day           a day of 30,000 or more

    STEPS ALL TOGETHER
      steps_100k       100,000 steps
      steps_1m         A million steps    (reward item: Winged sneakers)
      steps_5m         Five million steps

    STREAKS
      streak_7         A week in a row
      streak_30        A month in a row   (reward item: Ember cape)
      streak_100       100 days in a row  (reward item: Laurel wreath)

    QUESTS
      quest_1          First quest won    (reward item: Mimic pet)
      quest_10         Ten quests won

  - Coins: none for achievements. The streak bonuses already pay at 7
    and 30 days, and paying again for the same thing would make the
    coin economy easier without anyone choosing it.
  - The names and descriptions live in the app (like the item names in
    src/avatar/catalog.ts); the server knows only the ids, kinds and
    thresholds.

  EXACTLY WHAT COUNTS
  Each kind has one measure. It is worked out from the records that
  exist now; an achievement is earned the first time the measure
  reaches its threshold, and is never taken away afterwards.

    Kind          Measure                                Earned on
    ------------  -------------------------------------  ---------------
    Day steps     the highest uploaded day               the first day
                                                         at or over it
    Total steps   all uploaded days added up, each date  the day the
                  once (one row per date already)        running total
                                                         first reached it
    Streak        the longest run, under the existing    the day the run
                  streak rules (each day by its own      first reached it
                  goal; goal_runs)
    Quests        quests with status 'completed' that    the completed_at
                  you were in the party of, each quest   date (shop time
                  once                                   zone, like the
                                                         quest coin cap)
                                                         of the quest
                                                         that reached it

  - Total steps is added up in date order, oldest first, each date
    once; its earned date is the first date whose running total
    reaches the threshold. (Two runs over the same days always give
    the same date, whatever order they were uploaded in.)
  - "The day" for steps and streaks is the uploaded day itself (the
    phone's calendar day), not when the server noticed. So a day
    uploaded late still records the day it was walked.
  - Each quest counts once per person: quest_party has one row per
    person and quest, and a quest is completed only once.
  - Every earned date is exact: each kind has a dated record behind
    it, so there are no approximate dates.

  WHEN RECORDS CHANGE (KEEP, DON'T RECOUNT)
  - Earned stays earned: deleting step days or losing a quest record
    never takes an achievement away, and its earned date stays as it
    was.
  - Progress follows what exists now. A locked achievement's progress
    ("640,000 of 1,000,000") is worked out from today's records, so
    deleting days lowers it, and uploading or restoring days raises
    it. Progress toward an achievement already earned isn't shown.
  - Late and restored days count: the next check after an upload
    (claim_daily_rewards) sees them and can award an achievement,
    with the date the measure first reached it in the history as it
    now stands.
  - Goal changes can't change history (section 1), so they never
    change a streak achievement or its progress.

3. HOW THEY ARE EARNED
======================================================================

  WHO (CHOICE 2: DECIDED)
  - Every achievement needs step sharing, like coins and streaks: there
    is nothing on the server to count otherwise, and quests already
    need sharing. (With the friend and group achievements dropped,
    none are left that work without sharing.)

  WHEN
  - Checked on the server, never decided by the phone:
      - steps and streaks: inside claim_daily_rewards, after the coins,
        under the same per-person lock, so they're counted from the
        days just uploaded
      - quests: the moment a quest is won (a trigger on its status
        becoming 'completed'), for everyone in its party, rather than
        after the grace period when the coins are paid
  - Both call one internal function, award_achievements (section 6),
    which is safe to run any number of times, including at the same
    moment from two places.

  EARNED BEFORE LAUNCH (CHOICE 3: DECIDED)
  - Yes. The launch migration runs the same check for everyone once,
    so long-time users start with what they have earned. No coins are
    involved, so nothing in the economy changes. Reward items come with
    it (section 4), at 0 coins.
  - The earned date is the real one, worked out from the records, never
    the launch day pretending to be it:
      - steps and streaks: the exact day, from the uploaded days
      - quests: the completed_at of the quest that reached it
  - When there is no record at all (days deleted before launch), the
    achievement isn't given. Nothing is ever dated "launch day" as if
    it were the day it was earned.
  - Safe to stop and start again: the launch check is part of its
    migration, which runs as one transaction, so a failure part way
    leaves nothing behind. It runs without the per-person lock, so a
    big launch doesn't hold one lock per person until the end; the
    skip-what's-there inserts alone keep a claim at the same moment
    from adding anything twice (tested in achievement_race.sh). And because award_achievements only adds
    what's missing, running the check again after any partial run (for
    example by hand, person by person) ends with exactly what one full
    run gives.
  - The first time someone opens the new app, the achievements from
    the launch check come as one summary ("You've earned 6
    achievements") instead of six messages.

  SEEING A NEW ONE (DELIVERY)
  - The earned row is the record; the message is only a notice of it.
    An achievement shows on Profile and the player card from the moment
    it's earned, whether or not the message was ever seen.
  - Each earned row has a "seen" time, empty until acknowledged. The
    app:
      1. asks for the unseen ones (new_achievements) after each refresh
         and on app open
      2. shows the message
      3. only once it has been shown (the person closed it, or it was
         on screen), acknowledges those ids (mark_achievements_seen)
    If the app closes or loses its connection before step 3, nothing is
    acknowledged and the message comes again next time. Acknowledging
    is safe to repeat.
  - No duplicates on one phone: while a message is on screen, the app
    doesn't show the same ids again from a second fetch (it keeps the
    ids it is showing).
  - Two phones on the same account (CHOICE 6: DECIDED): whichever shows
    it first and acknowledges it stops it on the other from then on.
    If both are open at the same moment, both may show it once; that's
    accepted, since the alternative, a claim step, can lose a message
    when a phone dies mid-way.
  - Shown as an in-app message ("Achievement unlocked: A million steps.
    You can now wear the Winged sneakers."), with the character
    celebration of item 4d later. No push notifications (push isn't
    set up).

4. REWARD ITEMS (CHOICES 4, 5 AND 7: DECIDED)
======================================================================

  - Four achievements unlock one item each, which can't be bought:
    steps_1m, streak_30, streak_100 and quest_1 (shoes, a cape, a hat
    and a pet; the art is in "THE ART" below).
  - They launch together with the badges (CHOICE 7), not after the
    server-side anti-cheat, and the art is made now (CHOICE 5), so
    there is no badges-only period.
  - How: each is a shop_items row with active = false (so the shop
    never offers it and purchase_item refuses it) and a new column,
    unlocked_by, naming the achievement. Earning the achievement adds
    the item to user_items, recorded in the coin history at 0 coins
    ('achievement', the item id). Wearing it then works through the
    existing ownership checks; nothing about the avatar changes.
  - Exactly once, even at the same moment: the achievement row and its
    item are written in the same transaction by award_achievements,
    every insert is "on conflict do nothing" against a unique key
    (user_achievements per person and achievement; user_items per
    person and item; coin_transactions per person, reason and ref),
    and the per-person lock makes two calls run one after the other.
  - Items follow achievements, not the moment of earning: each run
    gives every reward item whose achievement the person HAS (earned
    now or long ago) and doesn't own yet. So a run that inserts nothing
    new still tops up a missing item, and an item added later (a fifth
    one, say) reaches everyone who already earned its achievement the
    next time award_achievements runs for them. The conflict-safe
    inserts make that once only: a second run, or two at once, add
    nothing. An achievement is never left without its item once the
    item exists, and an item is never given without its achievement.
  - Admins: they already get every shop_items row, so they get these
    too; it's how they test items.
  - In the Customize screen, a locked reward item shows with a lock and
    "Earn A million steps to unlock", which tells people the
    achievements exist.

  THE CHEATING RISK (ACCEPTED WITH CHOICE 7)
  - Steps are counted by the phone and uploaded; the server's checks
    (at most 100,000 a day, typed-in steps left out on the phone) keep
    numbers believable but are not proof the steps were walked. A
    modified app can upload a 30,000-step day.
  - Items no one can buy are a prize, and a prize is a reason to fake
    steps. Launching them before the server-side anti-cheat
    (recommendations 3e) means someone with a modified app could earn
    them. Accepted for now: they are cosmetic, cost nobody coins, and
    the quest item needs a whole party to win a quest.
  - When 3e is built, it doesn't take items back (earned stays earned);
    it only stops new faked days from counting.

  THE ART (CHOICE 5: MADE, OCTOBER 2026)
  - Four new sprites, drawn in scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py like
    the rest, under its rules: 32 x 56 for clothing (a back layer too
    where it shows behind), 16 x 16 two-frame walk for the pet, a
    1-pixel dark outline, no anti-aliasing, light from the top-left.
  - They look unlike anything the shop sells (it already has Gold
    sneakers, Rocket boots, Red and Royal capes, Gold crown and Halo),
    so wearing one says "earned":
      - steps_1m, shoes_winged, Winged sneakers: white high-tops with a
        gold stripe above the sole and a small gold wing at each heel.
      - streak_30, cape_ember, Ember cape: deep red at the shoulders to
        orange at a hem of flames, with sparks; matches the flame on
        the streak badges.
      - streak_100, hat_laurel, Laurel wreath: green and gold leaves
        around the head, the old prize for endurance.
      - quest_1, pet_mimic, Mimic: a treasure chest that walks beside
        you; closed in one frame (an ordinary chest), mid-hop in the
        other with the lid up, teeth, eyes and a tongue.

  ADDING A REWARD ITEM (THE MIGRATION)
  - At launch, the achievements migration inserts the four items; any
    later item gets its own migration. Either way it:
      1. inserts the shop_items row with active = false and
         unlocked_by set, in one statement: the item never exists in a
         state where it could be sold or offered
      2. runs the award for everyone who has that achievement (and
         only them), giving the item at 0 coins
  - A check constraint on shop_items keeps it that way for good:
    unlocked_by set means active must be false. Turning one into a
    shop item, by mistake or on purpose, needs a migration that drops
    unlocked_by first.
  - purchase_item and daily_shop already refuse or skip inactive items;
    check_avatar_items already refuses wearing an item not owned.
    Nothing about them changes.
  - Nobody else gets it: the award gives an item only for an earned
    achievement row; admins get it through their existing grant.

5. SCREENS
======================================================================

  PROFILE > ACHIEVEMENTS
  - A section on Profile with the earned count and the three most
    recent, and a row to the full list on its own screen.
  - The full list: earned ones first, newest first, with their date;
    then the locked ones, closest first, with progress where it's a
    number ("640,000 of 1,000,000 steps", "best streak 12 of 30", "3 of
    10 quests"), and the reward item each one unlocks.
  - Not sharing: the list shows "Share your steps to earn these", like
    the streak line, with each achievement locked and no progress.

  THE PLAYER CARD
  - The three most recently earned badges and the total ("9
    achievements"), on one compact row that fits small phones. Tapping
    it opens that person's earned list: names and dates only.
  - Only for anyone who can already open their card: friends, and
    group mates on a card opened from the group. The database decides
    (section 6); the app hiding the row isn't the protection.
  - Never shown to anyone else: progress numbers, lifetime totals and
    best streaks are only ever returned to the person themselves.
  - Privacy: badges say roughly how much someone walks. Friends and
    group mates already see their steps on the leaderboards, so this
    reveals nothing new to them.

  BADGE ART (CHOICE 6: DECIDED)
  - One pixel-style badge frame in the app's look, with a simple symbol
    per kind (a shoe for day steps, footprints for total steps, a
    flame for streaks, a flag for quests) and the number. 11 badges
    from 4 symbols, no art per badge. Locked ones grey.

6. DATABASE
======================================================================

  TABLES
  - achievements: id, kind (day_steps, total_steps, streak, quests),
    threshold, sort order. Readable by the app, never writable.
  - user_achievements: user, achievement, earned_on (date), earned_at,
    seen_at, at_launch (given by the launch check, for the one
    summary). Primary key (user, achievement). Row level security with
    NO policies at all: the app reads it only through the functions
    below, so the read rules can't be bypassed by querying the table.
  - shop_items gets unlocked_by (an achievement id, or null), and the
    four reward items. price may now be null: the check constraint
    shop_items_sold_or_earned allows either a sold item (no
    unlocked_by, a price over 0) or an earned one (unlocked_by, no
    price, never active).

  FUNCTIONS
  - achievement_measures(person): INTERNAL. Each achievement's measure
    from the records as they are now (the progress), and the day it
    first reached the threshold (or null).
  - award_achievements(person, at_launch default false): INTERNAL, not
    callable by the app.
    Works out the measures, inserts the newly reached achievements with
    their dates, and gives any reward item the person is owed (section
    4): one transaction, conflict-safe inserts. Does nothing for
    someone who doesn't share steps.
    Takes the per-person lock claim_daily_rewards already uses, so two
    calls for one person run one after the other. Called from
    claim_daily_rewards, the quest-won trigger, and the launch
    migration.
  - my_achievements(): yours only (the caller from the sign-in):
    'sharing', 'earned' (ids and dates, newest first) and, while you
    share, 'progress' (value and threshold of each one not earned).
    Nothing depends on the phone's date, so no my_today.
  - new_achievements(): your unseen ones, oldest first, each with
    at_launch and the reward item it unlocked (or null).
  - mark_achievements_seen(ids): sets seen on YOUR rows with those ids
    and nothing else; ids that aren't yours, or are already seen, are
    ignored. Safe to repeat.
  - player_achievements(person): a person's earned ids and
    dates, nothing else (no progress, no seen). The same rules as
    player_streak: yourself, or a friend now (is_friend) whom you can
    see (can_see); null for anyone else, without saying why.
  - group_member_card gains an 'achievements' field (ids and dates),
    under its existing rules (both in the group, the caller active, the
    person active).
  - Every reading function first checks the caller is signed in and
    active (is_active_account), as the existing ones do.

  PERMISSIONS ON FUNCTIONS
  - Postgres lets PUBLIC (everyone) execute a new function unless told
    otherwise, and Supabase adds anon and authenticated. So, as in the
    existing migrations, every function here is followed by
    "revoke all ... from public, anon, authenticated":
      - internal ones (award_achievements and any helper): nothing is
        granted back, so no signed-in user can call them, by RPC or
        any other way
      - app-facing ones (my_achievements, new_achievements,
        mark_achievements_seen, player_achievements): execute granted
        back to authenticated only, never anon
  - All are security definer with "set search_path = ''" and fully
    qualified names (public.…), owned by the migration's role
    (postgres), like the existing functions; the tables they touch have
    no write policies for the app.
  - claim_daily_rewards keeps returning the coins as now, so older app
    builds keep working; new achievements come from new_achievements().
  - Cost: lifetime steps is one sum over a person's days and the best
    streak is goal_runs, already run on each claim; both small.

7. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  Earning
  - Each kind is earned exactly at its threshold and not one step
    below, with the right earned date (the day, or the quest's date).
  - Once only, even when checked again.
  - At the same moment: two sessions call award_achievements for the
    same person at once (a concurrency script, like
    supabase/tests/concurrency/streak_race.sh): one achievement row,
    one item, one coin-history row; never an achievement without its
    item.
  - A reward item added later: earn an achievement that has no item,
    add an item for it (a migration like section 4's), run the award
    again: the person gets it exactly once; running it again, or two
    at once, adds nothing; someone without the achievement doesn't get
    it.
  - Reward items: one with unlocked_by can't be made active (the check
    constraint), isn't in the daily shop and can't be bought.
  - Total steps counted in date order: the same earned date whatever
    order the days were uploaded in.
  - Each quest counts once, even if checked again; failed quests
    and group members who weren't in the party don't count.
  - Not sharing: no achievements and no reward items.

  History changes
  - Earn A million steps, delete a qualifying day: still earned, same
    date, still owns the Winged sneakers; progress toward Five million
    goes down. Upload the day again: progress back up.
  - A missing day uploaded late completes a 7-day streak and earns it,
    dated the day the run reached 7.
  - A goal change doesn't change any past streak or its achievement.

  The launch check
  - The right achievements with the real dates, and the reward items
    for the four that have them; nothing dated launch day; running it
    twice adds nothing.
  - Stopped part way: run it for some people only (or a run that
    errors part way and rolls back), then the full check: the same
    result as one full run, nothing twice.

  Who can read what
  - player_achievements and group_member_card's achievements: none for
    a stranger; blocked in either direction; a former friend; a paused
    (hidden) account, an under-age or unfinished account, on either
    side (the caller, or the person); signed out.
  - No reader ever gets progress numbers or seen times except for
    yourself.
  - The table itself can't be read directly by the app (no policies).

  What the app can't do
  - Call award_achievements (or any internal function) directly, as a
    signed-in user by RPC: refused. Checked from the catalog too: no
    EXECUTE for public, anon or authenticated on any internal function;
    authenticated only (not anon) on the app-facing ones; each is
    security definer with an empty search_path.
  - Insert, update or delete user_achievements, or mark someone else's
    as seen (their ids are ignored).
  - Give itself a reward item: no insert into user_items; buying it is
    refused (ITEM_NOT_FOR_SALE) even when called directly; it's never
    in the daily shop; it can't be worn until owned
    (ITEM_NOT_OWNED); unlocked_by can't be changed.

  APP (npm test)
  - Progress text for each kind, and the order (earned first, newest
    first; then locked by closeness).
  - The unlock message: one, several, the first-time summary, the
    reward item line; ids being shown aren't shown again by a second
    fetch; acknowledged only after being shown.
  - The four new items are in the catalog (and pets list) with their
    sprites, and the lock text names the right achievement.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - Walk past 10,000 (or use a test account): the message comes once,
    the badge shows on Profile and on your card for a friend.
  - Close the app while the message is showing (before tapping it):
    it comes again on the next open.
  - Two phones on one account: dismiss on one; the other doesn't show
    it after its next refresh.
  - A quest won while the app was closed: the message on next open,
    and the Mimic walking beside the character once chosen.
  - A reward item: locked in Customize before, wearable after; each of
    the four looks right on the character and the player card.
  - Not sharing: the "Share your steps" note on the list.
  - A small phone: the card's badge row fits on one line.

9. BUILDING ORDER
======================================================================

  1. Art (done): the four item sprites (shoes_winged, cape_ember,
     hat_laurel, mimic_a/b in assets/pets) and the badge frame and
     symbols, each with a grey _locked copy (assets/badges), all drawn
     in scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py. Badges are 22 x 22 layers:
     the frame, then the kind's symbol; the app writes the number under
     them.
  2. Database (written, passing locally, not yet deployed):
     supabase/migrations/20261110000000_achievements.sql, tests in
     supabase/tests/database/achievements.test.sql and
     supabase/tests/concurrency/achievement_race.sh.
     The items' catalog.ts and pets.ts entries ship in the same release
     as this migration, never before it: an item with no shop_items
     row is free to wear, so listing them early would give them to
     everyone.
  3. Profile > Achievements and the full list with progress, the
     unlock message (acknowledged after showing; one summary the first
     time), and the lock on reward items in Customize (written): the
     names, badge numbers and reward items in
     src/achievements/achievements.ts (tested against the migration's
     ids, thresholds and reward rows), src/app/achievements.tsx, the
     Profile section, src/hooks/use-achievement-notices.ts. Customize
     now counts any shop_items row (active or not) as not free, and
     keeps an item from that list locked unless owned even if its row
     is missing; only then were the four items added to catalog.ts and
     pets.ts. The migration must still be deployed before this build
     ships (RELEASE-GATE.md step 12).
  4. Player card badges (friends and group cards) and the person's
     earned list (written): src/components/player-card.tsx shows the
     three most recent badges and the count on one row, from
     player_achievements (yourself and friends) or group_member_card's
     'achievements' (a card opened from a group); a failure or a null
     hides the row. Tapping it shows that person's earned list in place
     of the card's details: names and dates only, newest first, with
     Back to the card. The list and count come from earnedList in
     src/achievements/achievements.ts, which leaves out ids this app
     doesn't know.
