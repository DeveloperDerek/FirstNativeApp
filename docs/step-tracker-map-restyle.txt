STEP TRACKER - MAP RESTYLE: THE OTHER 8 MAPS (BLUEPRINT)
Gives forest, city, beach, mountain, dungeon, space, ocean and neon city
the same "Pixel Art 2.5D" treatment the village got: lit art, day /
sunset / night, shadows, depth and things that move.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. Edit
anything in it; it is read back before anything is built. The village
version is done (commit 3b17665, branch updates-2026-10-15) and is the
pattern for everything below.

How to mark it up:
  - Movements (section 3): change [KEEP] to [CUT] or [CHANGE: ...] on
    any line, or add lines for movements you want that aren't listed.
  - Decisions (section 4): write your pick after "Your pick:", or leave
    it blank to go with the recommendation.
  - Anything else: edit the text directly.

======================================================================
1. WHAT EVERY MAP GETS (THE VILLAGE RECIPE)
======================================================================

Built from each map's existing drawings; nothing is redrawn from
scratch.

  - Time of day: day, sunset (also used around sunrise) and night,
    from the phone's clock. Same as the village.
  - Lighting pass: outlines take a darker shade of their own color;
    edges facing the light get a warm rim, the far side a cool shade;
    colors a bit richer. Same as the village.
  - Sky: a banded pixel gradient with the sun or moon, fixed to the
    screen. Varies by map (see section 4).
  - Depth layers: two hill or skyline strips behind the street that
    slide slower than the road when you swipe. Shapes change per map.
  - Shadows: buildings and landmarks cast shadows on the road; long at
    sunset, short at midday. Same as the village.
  - Characters: lit to match the scene, with a shadow at their feet.
    Same as the village.
  - Clearings: open ground at both ends of each tile so landmarks never
    stand on buildings. Buildings are re-laid per map.
  - Screen colors: header, ground and text colors follow the time of
    day, with text contrast checked at 4.5:1 or better.

Movement runs off one shared clock at 12 frames a second. It pauses
when you leave the Today tab and stops entirely under Reduce Motion.

======================================================================
2. EACH MAP'S LOOK
======================================================================

Each map keeps its own buildings and landmarks. "Depth" is what slides
behind the street when you swipe; "Clearing fix" is how its tiles get
open ground for the landmarks.

FOREST
  Day:      soft green-gold light through the trees
  Sunset:   amber light, long tree shadows
  Night:    deep blue-green, moonlight on the treetops
  Depth:    two rows of hazy pine silhouettes
  Clearing: move edge pines inward

CITY
  Day:      clear blue sky
  Sunset:   orange sky, windows start lighting up
  Night:    navy sky, most windows lit
  Depth:    a far skyline and a nearer skyline
  Clearing: drop one building per tile

BEACH
  Day:      bright turquoise, sparkling sea
  Sunset:   pink-orange sky, sun on the horizon line
  Night:    dark sea, moon reflection on the water
  Depth:    a distant headland and the sea band
  Clearing: move edge palms and umbrellas inward

MOUNTAIN
  Day:      crisp pale blue, bright snow
  Sunset:   alpenglow: peaks turn pink
  Night:    starry sky, blue snow
  Depth:    far snowy range and near foothills
  Clearing: move edge pines and rocks inward

DUNGEON
  Day:      pale light through high grates
  Sunset:   warmer torchlight
  Night:    darkest; torches carry the light
  Depth:    deeper arches in the gloom
  Clearing: keep the wall; no arches or banners at tile ends

SPACE
  Day:      harsh white sun, sharp shadows
  Sunset:   Earthrise glow on the horizon
  Night:    no sun; base lights and starlight
  Depth:    far crater ridges
  Clearing: move edge domes inward

OCEAN
  Day:      sunbeams from the surface
  Sunset:   warm amber water near the top
  Night:    deep blue, glowing sea life
  Depth:    distant reef and rock shelves
  Clearing: move edge kelp and rocks inward

NEON CITY
  Day:      overcast gray, signs off
  Sunset:   signs flicker on against an orange sky
  Night:    full neon (today's look)
  Depth:    far skyline with lit windows
  Clearing: drop one building per tile

======================================================================
4. DECISIONS TO MAKE
======================================================================

Four maps have no ordinary sky, so day and night need their own
meaning there.

4a. Dungeon: what does night mean underground?
    Recommendation: light from high grates by day, torches only at
      night, so it still changes.
    Other option:   the same torchlit look all day.
    Your pick: Recommendation

4b. Space: the sky is always black.
    Recommendation: keep it black; day has a harsh sun, sunset an
      Earthrise, night only base lights.
    Other option:   a dim blue glow by day.
    Your pick: Recommendation

4c. Ocean: underwater day and night.
    Recommendation: sunbeams by day, amber water at sunset, deep blue
      with glowing sea life at night.
    Other option:   the same blue all day, sea life only.
    Your pick: Recommendation

4d. Neon City: today's look is already night.
    Recommendation: gray overcast by day with signs off; signs flicker
      on at sunset.
    Other option:   keep neon at all times; only the sky changes.
    Your pick: Recommendation

4e. Rain in Neon City.
    Recommendation: some nights only, so it feels like weather.
    Other option:   every night.
    Your pick: Recommendation

4f. Previews: two maps per preview (as in section 5), or all eight
    previews at once?
    Your pick: No preview

======================================================================
5. BUILD ORDER
======================================================================

Two maps at a time, starting with the ones closest to the village.
Each pair is approved from a preview before it goes into the app.

  1. Shared groundwork: the lighting script and movement layer handle
     any map, not just the village, and movements become a per-map
     list.
  2. Forest and Beach: outdoor skies, nearest to the village.
  3. Mountain and City: City's tiles need the most re-laying for
     clearings.
  4. Ocean and Space: the underwater and space versions of day and
     night.
  5. Neon City and Dungeon: the two that start out dark.

For each pair:

  1. Claude renders a preview image of both maps at day, sunset and
     night.
  2. You approve it or ask for changes.
  3. Claude builds it into the app, checks it with tests, and you look
     at it on your phone.
  4. It's committed to that day's branch.
