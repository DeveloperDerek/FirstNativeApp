STEP TRACKER - DAILY ROLL (BLUEPRINT)
When a player reaches the day's goal, they get one free roll of a
prize machine (a gacha). The roll gives either a shop item they
don't own yet, with rarer items less likely, or a pile of coins. It
takes the place of the 150 daily coins.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes how the roll is earned, what it can give, how the server
picks the prize, what changes in the economy and on each screen, what
the database keeps, and the choices to make before building (section
11). All choices (ROLL-1 to ROLL-16) were decided on 2026-10-18.
ROLL-1 was changed on 2026-10-19: the capsule machine became a slot
machine (section 5, "The roll screen"). ROLL-17 is decided (C).
Paid spins, planned for later, are in section 12; their choices
(ROLL-18 to ROLL-24) are still open.

What this changes in other blueprints (and nothing else):
  - step-tracker-coins-items.txt, 2a ("Daily step reward, 150 coins
    a day"): from the switch day (section 7) a goal day pays 0 coins
    and gives a roll instead. The 'daily_goal' row is still written,
    at 0 coins, because the activity board and kudos use it to mark
    a goal day (section 7). "What a day is worth" and "How long
    things take" need redoing (section 6).
  - step-tracker-coins-items.txt, 5f (COIN-F, the coin sink): the
    roll makes it more pressing (section 6).
  - step-tracker-activity-board.txt: a goal line says what was won
    (ROLL-11). Your own: the item or "+250 coins", instead of "+150
    coins". A friend's: the item only; coins wins stay private, as
    coins are now (ACT-6).
  - step-tracker-history-reminders.txt: one more reminder text for a
    roll that's waiting (ROLL-12).
Goals, the 7,000 to 10,000 target (GOAL-2), streaks, achievements,
check-ins, the welcome bonus, quests and the shop rotation are
unchanged.

Builds on what the app already has:

    claim_daily_rewards() inside sync_day() on every step refresh
    (20261112000000_goal_range_coin_steps.sql,
    20261114000000_sync_day.sql)
  - the day rule: is_today_somewhere(day, now())
    (20261108000000_goals_streaks.sql)
  - the item tables: shop_items (price, active, unlocked_by),
    user_items, coin_transactions with its once-only key

    20261110000000_achievements.sql)
  - the price ladder COIN-5 (step-tracker-coins-items.txt, 5e)

    20261117000000_goal_is_coin_target.sql)
  - the item art: src/avatar/ItemIcon.tsx, and the pixel-art generator
    scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py

======================================================================
1. THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE
======================================================================

  - On Today, the line under the step track says "Daily roll at
    goal" where it now says "150 coins at goal".
  - When your steps reach today's goal and the app refreshes, a
    small pixel-art slot machine shows on Today (ROLL-1). There is no
    Roll button anywhere: the machine is what you tap.
  - Tap it: the roll screen opens on the big slot machine. Tap that
    machine: its reel spins through prizes and stops on either an
    item you didn't own, with its rarity (Common to Legendary), or
    coins (100, 250 or 500). A prize card pops out of the machine.
    The prize is yours at once. For an item, "Wear it" opens the
    character editor; "Done" goes back.
  - One roll per goal day, and only on that day (ROLL-4): if you
    don't roll before midnight on your phone, that day's roll is
    gone.
  - The roll never gives an item you already own. Once you own
    every item it can give, every roll is a coins roll (ROLL-3).
  - It's free and always will be: no buying rolls, no rerolls
    (ROLL-9).
  - Friends see the items you win on the activity board (ROLL-11).

======================================================================
2. EARNING A ROLL
======================================================================

  WHO
  - The same people who earn coins now: signed in, active account,
    sharing steps. Without sharing, no roll (same as no coins).
  - Admins own every item, so every roll of theirs is a coins roll.

  WHICH DAY
  - A roll belongs to one day: the player's phone date, sent as
    my_today like every other call. The server checks it with
    is_today_somewhere(my_today, now()), or refuses with BAD_DAY.
  - The roll for my_today exists when that day has a 'daily_goal'
    row (claim_daily_rewards wrote it because steps reached
    coin_steps_on for that day). One source of truth for "goal
    reached": the same row the board and kudos use.
  - So the steps have to reach the server before the roll can be
    taken. The app refreshes steps on open and on coming back to the
    foreground, then shows the button.

  USE IT OR LOSE IT (ROLL-4)
  - A day's roll can be taken only while that day is my_today on the
    phone. After midnight the app sends the new date, and yesterday's
    roll is gone.
  - Late uploads no longer bring a reward. Before, the 7-day back-pay
    window paid 150 coins for a goal day uploaded up to a week late.
    Now a goal day uploaded after midnight still counts for the
    streak, the board and achievements, but gets no roll and no
    coins. This is the cost of ROLL-4; the reminder (ROLL-12) is
    there to cut it down.
  - The server's "today" is "today somewhere on Earth", as for
    everything else, so a changed app could take yesterday's roll a
    few hours after midnight (for California, until about 5 am,
    when the day ends at UTC-12). The real app always sends the
    phone date. Accepted: it is the same window every other function
    has, and it can never give more than one roll per day.

======================================================================
3. WHAT THE ROLL CAN GIVE
======================================================================

  TWO KINDS OF PRIZE (ROLL-2)
  - Coins: 100, 250 or 500 (ROLL-16).
  - An item from the pool below.

  THE ITEM POOL
  - Every shop item that is on sale (active = true), not an
    achievement item (unlocked_by is null), and not owned by the
    player.
  - Not free starter items (they have no shop row), not retired items
    (active = false), not achievement items.
  - Whether the item is in today's shop doesn't matter. If it is, its
    shop slot empties until the next refresh, exactly as if it had
    been bought (ROLL-14).
  - Items in a set (Cowboy, Pirate...) are rolled one piece at a
    time, like they are sold.

  THE CHANCES (ROLL-6, ROLL-7)
  Coins are one outcome; the items are split into five rarities by
  price, using the COIN-5 ladder:

    Outcome     Prices          Items now   Average value   Chance
    Coins       -                  -             185          40%
    Common      300 - 400          20            335          31%
    Uncommon    500 - 700          32            620          19%
    Rare        800 - 1,200        27            980           6%
    Epic        1,500 - 2,000      18          1,640           3%
    Legendary   2,500 - 3,000       8          2,560           1%

  - The server picks an outcome by these chances first. For a rarity
    it then picks one item from that rarity at random, all equally
    likely. For coins it picks the amount (below).
  - Coins stay at 40% for as long as there is any item left to win.
  - A rarity with nothing left for you is skipped, and its share goes
    to the other rarities in proportion, so items stay 60% (with no
    Commons left: Uncommon about 39%, Rare 12%, Epic 6%, Legendary
    2%). So rolls get better as your collection grows, and the
    last item you need always comes.
  - Picking the rarity first keeps the chances the same however many
    items each rarity has, so adding 10 new hats doesn't change the
    odds of a Legendary.
  - Rarity is worked out from the price, not stored, so a new item
    priced on the ladder is in the right rarity with no extra step.

  THE COINS PRIZE (ROLL-16)
  - A coins roll pays:
        100 coins   6 in 10
        250 coins   3 in 10
        500 coins   1 in 10
    About 185 coins on average, a bit more than the old 150.
  - Shown on the reel as a tile of spinning gold coins (one coin for
    100, three for 250, six for 500), then "+250 coins" on the prize
    card.

  WHEN THE POOL IS EMPTY (ROLL-3)
  - Every roll is a coins roll, with the same three amounts.
  - When new items are added, they go into the pool and the roll
    gives items again (at the chances above).

  NO ODDS IN THE APP (ROLL-8)
  - The chances aren't shown anywhere in the app; the rarity name
    and its colour are the only hint.
  - The store rules that require odds (Apple 3.1.1, Google Play's
    loot box policy) are about random prizes that are paid for. The
    roll is never paid for (ROLL-9), so they don't apply. If rolls
    were ever sold, showing the odds would be required first.
  - Still tested and written down here, so the numbers are known if
    a store reviewer or a parent asks.

======================================================================
4. THE ROLL ITSELF (SERVER)
======================================================================

  - roll_daily_prize(my_today) decides everything; the app only shows
    the result.
  - The prize is decided at the tap, before any animation, and saved
    at once. Closing the app mid-animation doesn't give another try:
    the next open shows the same prize, until it has been seen.
  - Calling it again for a day already rolled returns the same prize,
    with no error. Two taps, a retry after a timeout, or two phones
    all get one roll.
  - It takes the player's coin lock (the same advisory lock as
    claim_daily_rewards and purchase_item), so a roll and a purchase
    can't both take the last item.
  - Randomness: Postgres random(), on the server. Nothing the phone
    sends changes the result.

  THE ROLL SCREEN (a modal, src/app/roll.tsx)
  Preview (private link, made 2026-10-19; nothing built):
  https://claude.ai/artifact/8R6oU9BE2bujoUZSa5QER5

  The machine
  - A slot machine in the app's pixel art (2.5D, like the maps), drawn
    at 120 x 152 pixels and scaled up by a whole number (3x on most
    phones): a red body with a darker right side, a purple sign on
    top reading DAILY ROLL with a ring of bulbs, a chrome frame round
    one reel, gold arrows either side of the middle line (the
    payline), a prize tray under the reel, and a lever with a red
    knob on the right.
  - The reel window shows three tiles; the middle one is on the
    line. Each tile shows a prize on cream, with two small gems and
    a faint edge in its rarity colour. The window is shaded at the
    top and bottom so the reel looks round.
  - Colours: Coins yellow, Common grey, Uncommon green, Rare blue,
    Epic purple, Legendary gold.
  - Before the tap the machine bobs gently, the bulbs chase slowly,
    and "Pull the lever to roll!" blinks under it.

  Starting the roll
  - There is no Roll button on this screen: you pull the lever. The
    knob follows your finger down; let go past 60% of the way and it
    springs back up as the reel goes, otherwise it just springs back.
    Tapping the machine does nothing.
  - It works with a screen reader ("Pull the prize machine's lever",
    a button): activating it pulls the lever all the way.
  - One roll per screen: the lever doesn't move while it spins or
    while the prize card is up.

  The spin (times from letting go of the lever)
    0 ms           Let go. The hint hides, the lever springs back,
                   roll_daily_prize is called. Light haptic.
    0 - 160        Wind-up: the reel nudges back a quarter tile.
    160 - ~2,000   Full speed. Bulbs chase fast; a soft tick each time
                   a tile crosses the line (at most one every 45 ms),
                   and a selection haptic with it (at most one every
                   50 ms, so full speed is a steady buzz).
    ~2,000 - 5,160 Slows down (ease-out); the ticks and haptics space
                   out until the last tile clicks into place. About
                   40 tiles go by in all.
    5,160 - 5,420  Stops a fifth of a tile past the prize and settles
                   back onto it. The machine shakes; the payline and
                   bulbs flash in the rarity colour. A success haptic
                   (a double buzz), plus a heavy one just after for
                   Epic, Legendary and 500 coins.
    then           The reveal (below). mark_roll_seen.
  - Rarer prizes spin longer, with a slower crawl at the end: Epic
    5.6 s (44 tiles), Legendary 6.2 s (48 tiles), everything else
    5.0 s (40 tiles).
  - The spin only shows the result. The prize comes back from the
    server first (section 4), and the reel is built with that prize
    at the stop, so it can't land on anything else. The light haptic
    fires as the lever is let go; the reel starts its
    wind-up once the answer is back (usually a fraction of a
    second). Built: it doesn't spin while waiting.
  - The other tiles are things the player could win: items from
    their own pool and coin tiles. How often each rarity shows up on
    the reel is ROLL-17. The tiles next to the prize are never the
    same prize, so the stop is easy to read.

  The reveal
  - An item: it grows out of the reel window to 2.4x with a glow in
    its rarity colour, holds, then shrinks back into its tile
    (about 0.76 s). Rare gets a few sparkles; Epic and Legendary
    more (Legendary twice).
  - Coins: the moment the reel stops, the coins leave their row,
    which stays empty, and gold coins spray out of the tray (the
    machine's mouth), more for bigger wins (7, 14 or 26). The card
    follows 0.6 s later, and the wallet at the top counts up to the
    new balance.
  - Then the prize card pops out of the tray: it starts as a speck
    at the tray, flies down and grows to just past full size, then
    settles (about 0.56 s), with a puff of white sparkles at the
    tray and a heavy haptic. It shows the prize, its rarity (or
    "Coins") and its name (or "+250 coins").
  - Buttons on the card: "Wear it" and "Done" for an item ("Wear
    it" opens the character editor with the item picked); "Done"
    only for coins.

  The coins
  - One 12 x 12 pixel coin: a dark navy outline, a gold body, a pale
    yellow rim on the top left, orange shading on the bottom right,
    and a raised orange square in the middle. No number on it.
  - It spins in four frames, 120 ms each: front, three-quarter turn,
    edge, three-quarter turn the other way. Every third turn a white
    shine crosses the front.
  - Each coin floats up and down 2 pixels. Coins in one tile spin
    and float out of step with each other.
  - Tiles: 100 is one coin, 250 is three in a row (the middle one
    higher), 500 is two staggered rows of three. Every coin on the
    reel is the same size (the size that lets the 500 tile fit).
  - They keep spinning on the reel and on the prize card. The wallet
    icon is the front frame, still.

  Sound
  - Short 8-bit sounds: a tick per tile, a thunk on the stop, a
    rising jingle that gets longer with rarity, a pop when the card
    comes out, and ticks as the balance counts up. Silent when the
    phone is on silent. NOT BUILT YET: the first build has haptics
    only (it needs sound files and an audio module).

  Reduce Motion
  - No spin, no lever, no shake, no growing item, no sparkles or
    coin spray, no flying card. The prize sits on the line, the card
    is shown in place, and the balance changes at once. Coins show
    their front frame and don't float or spin.

  ACTIVITY BOARD (ROLL-11)
  - Your own goal line: "reached your goal · won pirate hat" or
    "reached your goal · +250 coins", instead of "+150 coins" (no
    "a", like "bought red cap").
  - A friend's goal line, when they won an item: "reached their goal
    · won pirate hat", with the item's icon. When they won coins,
    or haven't rolled: "reached their goal", as now.
  - The win is a new part of the line, so a line moves up when the
    roll is taken, as it does for a later achievement. Kudos on the
    line are unchanged (same person, same day).
  - Who sees it is the board's rule, unchanged: friends you can see,
    who share steps.

  SHOP, CUSTOMIZE

  REMINDER (ROLL-12)

6. THE ECONOMY AFTER THE SWITCH
======================================================================

  WHAT A GOAL DAY IS WORTH
  - Before: 150 coins.
  - After: about 430 on average at the prices above: items about
    355 (0.31 x 335 + 0.19 x 620 + 0.06 x 980 + 0.03 x 1,640
    + 0.01 x 2,560) and coins about 75 (0.40 x 185). So reaching the
    goal is worth about 3x more than now, mostly paid in items.

  WHAT A DAY PAYS IN COINS

        Player          Check-ins  Refresh  Quests  Roll*   Total
        Casual             20         10        0     ~75    ~105
        Regular            30         20       72     ~75    ~195
        Very engaged       40         20      270     ~75    ~405

        * the average from coins rolls, on a goal day. A single day
          is 0 (an item), 100, 250 or 500.

  Coins now come from opening the app, the welcome bonus, quests and
  coins rolls (down from ~180 a day for a casual player). They
  become the way to pick the exact item you want, while the roll
  fills the collection.

  HOW LONG A COLLECTION TAKES
  - 105 items; 6 goal days in 10 give one, so about 175 goal days.
    Someone reaching the goal 5 days a week owns everything in about
    35 weeks from rolls alone, sooner with purchases. Before: about
    380 days for a regular player.
  - After that every roll is coins, about 185 on average, a little
    more than today's 150, so nobody ends up worse off than now.

  WHAT THIS MEANS FOR COIN-F
  - The shop thins out faster (owned items never show), and coins
    have less to buy. The coin sink (5f) is now needed within a few
    months of the switch, not ~11. Adding items at ~10 a month keeps
    the pool from running dry; "buy any item at 2x" gives coins a
    use near the end.
  - If the collection fills too fast, the first thing to change is
    the chances (ROLL-6), not the prices.

7. DATABASE
======================================================================

  THE SWITCH DAY
  - app_settings 'daily_roll_from': a day, stored like
    'goal_is_coin_target_from' (days after 2000-01-01). Empty = the
    roll is off and everything works as now.
  - It is set only once a build with the roll screen is out (ROLL-5).
    Older builds don't know about rolls: on a day after the switch
    they'd say "+150 coins" and pay nothing. So: ship the build, wait
    about two weeks, then set the day. Older builds can be told to
    update with the builds blueprint's update prompt.

  claim_daily_rewards() (CHANGED)
  - Days before the switch day: 150 coins, as now (late uploads of
    those days still pay inside the 7-day window).
  - The switch day and after: the 'daily_goal' row is still written,
    with amount 0. The activity board (friends_activity,
    activity_line_exists) and kudos read that row to know a day was a
    goal day, so they keep working with no change. The wallet isn't
    touched for a 0.

  daily_rolls (NEW TABLE)
    user_id    uuid, references profiles, on delete cascade
    day        date
    item_id    text, references shop_items, null when coins were paid
    coins      integer: 0 for an item, else 100, 250 or 500
    rarity     text: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, coins
    rolled_at  timestamptz, default now()
    seen_at    timestamptz, null until the result has been shown
    primary key (user_id, day)
  - Read own rows only (RLS). No insert, update or delete for the app;
    only the functions below write it.

  roll_rarity(price) (NEW, INTERNAL)
  - The ladder in section 3, in one place. Tests pin it.

  roll_daily_prize(my_today date) (NEW)
  - Returns (item_id, rarity, coins, new boolean). new = false when
    the day was already rolled.
  - Checks, in order: signed in (else 'Not signed in'), active
    (ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE), sharing (NO_SHARING), is_today_somewhere
    (BAD_DAY), the switch day set and my_today on or after it
    (ROLL_OFF), a 'daily_goal' row for my_today (GOAL_NOT_REACHED).
  - Takes the coin lock. If daily_rolls has the day, returns it.
  - Otherwise picks the outcome, then the item or the amount (section
    3) and, in one transaction: inserts daily_rolls; inserts
    user_items for an item; inserts coin_transactions (reason 'roll',
    ref the day, amount 0 for an item, like 'starter' rows are 0, or
    the coins); adds the coins to the wallet when there are any; runs
    award_achievements.

  mark_roll_seen(my_today date) (NEW)
  - Sets seen_at. The roll screen calls it once the prize card has
    opened.

  sync_day() (CHANGED)
  - Its result gets one more key, 'roll', for my_today:
      null                    the roll is off, or not sharing
      {"state": "locked"}     goal not reached yet
      {"state": "ready"}      goal reached, not rolled
      {"state": "rolled", "item_id", "rarity", "coins", "seen"}
  - Older builds ignore the extra key.

  activity_feed() and activity_parts() (CHANGED)
  - The board is activity_feed now (one line per thing done), so the
    prize goes there; friends_activity is only for older builds,
    which don't know rolls, and is unchanged.
  - Every goal line gets 'prize_item' from daily_rolls for that day:
    the item_id, or null for a coins roll or no roll yet. Your own
    goal lines also get 'prize_coins' (null on friends' lines, like
    'coins').
  - A goal line's 'at' is its roll's rolled_at when that is later,
    so taking the roll moves the line up and paging still works.
  - activity_line_exists is unchanged: a win is only ever on a goal
    day, which is already a line.

  CLEANUP
  - daily_rolls rows are kept (a few bytes per goal day, and a record
    of where an item came from). Deleted with the account.

8. WHAT THE ROLL IS NOT
======================================================================

  - Not for sale. No buying rolls, no paying coins for an extra roll
    or a reroll, no premium machine (ROLL-9). Selling random prizes
    brings loot-box rules (store odds disclosure, some countries'
    gambling laws) and is a poor fit for 13 and up. Paid spins are
    planned for later; section 12 is what they need first.
  - Not a way to get achievement items. They still come only from
    their achievement.
  - Not a duplicate-giver: there's nothing to trade or sell back.
  - Not a push from the server. The reminder is the existing local
    one (ROLL-12).

======================================================================
9. TRICKY CASES
======================================================================

  - Goal reached, app not opened that day: the server never learns
    the goal was reached before midnight, so no roll (ROLL-4). The
    reminder can only fire if an earlier refresh that day already
    saw the goal.
  - Roll taken, then steps re-uploaded lower or the day deleted: the
    item stays. A roll, like a purchase, is never taken back.
  - The item won is in today's shop: the slot empties (ROLL-14).
  - The pool has 1 item left and the player buys it while the roll
    screen is open: the lock orders them; whichever is second sees
    the item owned (the purchase fails ALREADY_OWNED, or the roll
    picks from what's left, or is a coins roll if nothing is).
  - An item retired (active = false) after being won: kept, like a
    bought one.
  - Two phones on one account: one roll; both see the same prize.
  - Phone date changed to tomorrow: is_today_somewhere allows it only
    when it's already tomorrow somewhere, and tomorrow's steps still
    have to reach tomorrow's goal. At most one roll per day, as for
    coins.
  - Midnight while the roll screen is open: the tap sends the date
    the screen was opened with. If that day is no longer today
    anywhere, BAD_DAY: "This roll ended at midnight." Otherwise it
    goes through.
  - Sharing turned off after the goal: no roll (NO_SHARING), same as
    no coins.
  - The switch day itself: a goal day before it paid 150 coins and
    has no roll; the switch day has a roll and its 'daily_goal' row
    pays 0 (a coins roll pays through its own 'roll' row). No day
    gets both.

12. PAID SPINS (PLANNED, NOT BUILT)
======================================================================

  Later, players may be able to buy an extra spin with real money.
  That makes the machine a paid loot box, which brings rules the free
  roll doesn't have. This section is what has to be true before it
  ships. It needs ROLL-9 changed, and ROLL-8 changed for paid spins
  (ROLL-18 to ROLL-24 below are its own choices). The rules change
  quickly: a lawyer who works on games checks this before launch
  (REG-D in step-tracker-decisions.txt). Last checked October 2026.

  Why it can be legal
  - Gambling is paying for a chance at a prize worth real money.
    Coins and items are worth nothing outside the app, and that has
    to stay true for good: never cashed out, never traded or gifted
    between players, never sold anywhere. Trading is what made
    "skins" a gambling problem in the US and Europe. No real-world
    prizes (gift cards, merchandise) from a paid spin, ever.

  The odds, before every paid spin
  - Apple (App Store guideline 3.1.1) and Google Play require the
    odds of each kind of prize to be shown before purchase. South
    Korea and China require it by law; the US FTC treats hidden or
    misleading odds as deceptive.
  - The odds are per player: a rarity with nothing left to win is
    skipped and its share goes to the others (ROLL-6). So the screen
    shows that player's odds now, from the server (a new function
    next to roll_outcome), not a fixed table. "Odds" sits by the
    buy button, one tap away, and every percentage on it adds to
    100.
  - The reel shows the real chances on a paid spin (ROLL-17 option
    A): a reel showing items more often than people win them could
    count as a misleading display, which Korea's law names. The free
    roll can keep option C (ROLL-23).

  Who can buy
  - Players are 13 and up (step-tracker-register.txt). In January
    2025 the FTC's order against HoYoverse (Genshin Impact, $20M)
    banned selling loot boxes to under-16s without a parent's
    consent and required real-money prices. Brazil (2025) bans loot
    boxes in games for children and teens. The UK's industry rules
    need a parent's consent under 18. Australia (2024) rates games
    with paid loot boxes M or higher.
  - Paid spins are 18 and up (ROLL-19), checked on the server from
    the birthday the app already has. Under 18 the buy button
    doesn't show; the free roll is unchanged.
  - The App Store and Google Play age-rating forms say the app has
    random paid items.

  Where
  - Belgium treats paid loot boxes as illegal gambling: no paid
    spins there (ROLL-20). The list of countries is a server
    setting, changed without a new build, since more countries are
    moving the same way. The free roll works everywhere.

  Buying
  - Through Apple's and Google's in-app purchases, never our own
    payment page.
  - The price in real money on the button. If spins are ever bought
    with coins that were bought, the real-money price shows next to
    the coin price too (EU consumer authorities, 2024; the FTC
    order).
  - No pressure: no countdowns on spins, no near-miss animations
    (the reel stopping just short of a Legendary), no "buy 3 more"
    rewards, no discounts that run out. These are what regulators
    are going after now.
  - No "collect the whole set" rewards for things only paid spins
    give (Japan bans "complete gacha").
  - A purchase history on Profile: each paid spin, the date, the
    price and the prize. A spending limit the player can set
    (ROLL-21).

  On the server
  - The prize is still picked and saved on the server at the pull
    (ROLL-13), after the store's receipt is checked there.
  - Each version of the odds is kept, and each paid spin records the
    odds version it used, so we can show that the odds shown were
    the odds used.
  - A database test runs many outcomes and checks they match the
    odds shown.
  - Paid spins are their own table, apart from daily_rolls, so
    refunds, age checks and reports stay simple. A refund from the
    store takes the prize back (ROLL-22).

  Paperwork
  - Terms: coins and items have no cash value, can't be moved
    between accounts, and what a refund does.
  - Privacy policy: purchase data.
  - Store listings: in-app purchases and the random-items notice.

  The simpler choice
  - Selling items directly ("this hat, $1.99", or a pack with a
    stated item) has no chance in it, so almost none of the above
    applies. Many apps for teens do this instead (ROLL-24).
