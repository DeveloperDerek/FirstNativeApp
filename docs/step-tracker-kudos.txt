STEP TRACKER - KUDOS (BLUEPRINT)
A kudos button on each line of the activity board, so friends can
cheer each other's goal days and achievements ("Ana gave you kudos"),
and a notice inside the app when you get one. No push notifications.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what kudos are, who can give them, how the person who gets
one is told inside the app, what the database keeps, and the choices
to make before building (section 10).

What this changes in other blueprints (and nothing else):
  - step-tracker-activity-board.txt, section 12 ("Cheers on lines:
    not built at this time"): kudos are the cheers. The board's
    lines, who sees them, paging and refreshing all stay as they are,
    and ACT-5 (no pushes) still holds.
  - step-tracker-notifications.txt: notification_counts() gets one
    more number, new_kudos, and the Friends tab can show a small dot
    for it (KUD-6). Pushes, the app icon number and the friend
    request badge are unchanged.
Coins, goals, streaks, achievements and blocking are unchanged. Kudos
pay nothing and unlock nothing (KUD-9).

Builds on what the app already has:
  - the activity board in Friends: friends_activity(), one line per
    person per day, worked out live (20261121000000_activity_board.sql,
    src/components/activity-list.tsx, src/hooks/use-friends-activity.ts)
  - the read rules: is_active_account, has_sharing_consent, can_see,
    is_friend, is_today_somewhere
  - the tab badges: notification_counts() and its shared counts in
    src/hooks/use-notification-counts.tsx, refreshed on open, on
    coming back to the foreground and on opening Friends
  - the badge art generator (scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py)

======================================================================
1. THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE
======================================================================

  - Every friend's line on Friends > Activity gets a small kudos
    button (a pixel-art clap, KUD-2) with a count next to it.
  - Tap it to give kudos. One per person per line: the button fills
    in and the count goes up by one. Tapping again does nothing
    (KUD-3: kudos can't be taken back).
  - Your own lines show the count too, and tapping it lists who gave
    you kudos for that day.
  - When you get kudos, you find out the next time you open the app
    or come back to it: a small dot on the Friends tab, and "3 new
    kudos" at the top of Friends > Activity. Tapping that opens a list
    of every new kudos, whatever day it's on; only seeing that list
    clears them (KUD-11). Nothing is sent to the phone while the app
    is closed.
  - A kudos is one small row: who gave it, to whom, for which day.
    The line itself is still worked out live, so a kudos always sits
    on the line for that person and day, however the line changes.

======================================================================
2. WHAT A KUDOS IS ATTACHED TO
======================================================================

  - The board has one line per person per day (ACT-2), so a line is
    named by (person, day). A kudos is (giver, person, day).
  - If the line gains something later that day (an achievement after
    the goal), it is still the same line and keeps its kudos. Nobody
    is asked to give kudos again.
  - If the line goes away (the person deleted the day and earned no
    achievement on it), its kudos are no longer shown or counted
    anywhere. The rows stay until the cleanup (section 7) and come
    back only if the line does.
  - Only lines that are on the board can get kudos: a goal day or an
    achievement day, inside the date window below, of someone you can
    see on the board right now (section 3). The server checks this
    again on every give; the app's button is only a shortcut.
  - Never on your own line.

  THE DATE WINDOW (THE ACTIVITY BOARD'S, UNCHANGED)
  Kudos add no date rules of their own. Every kudos function uses
  exactly the window friends_activity already uses
  (20261121000000_activity_board.sql), written out here so the tests
  can pin it:
  - my_today is the caller's phone date ('YYYY-MM-DD'), sent with each
    call, as for every function the app calls. The server can't work
    it out (it doesn't know the phone's time zone), so it VALIDATES
    it: is_today_somewhere(my_today, now()) must be true, meaning
    my_today is today somewhere between UTC-12 and UTC+14. Otherwise
    BAD_DAY. So a phone can pick at most the two or three dates that
    are today somewhere on Earth at that moment; it can't stretch the
    window by days.
  - Today is day 0. The oldest day in the window is my_today - 29:
    30 calendar days counting today, both ends included.
  - The newest day is my_today + 1: a friend in a time zone ahead of
    yours can already be on your tomorrow, and their line for that
    day is on your board, so it can get kudos.
  - So a line can get kudos when
      my_today - 29 <= day <= my_today + 1
    and is shown, counted and listed under the same rule. Everything
    else about whether the line exists (the goal, achievements, a
    deleted day) is the board's rule too.
  - The window is the GIVER's (the caller's my_today) for give_kudos,
    and the line owner's (their my_today) for new_kudos and
    line_kudos. Each person sees kudos on the lines their own board
    shows.
  - Around midnight a line can leave the window between loading the
    board and tapping the clap (the phone's date moved on). The
    server then refuses with BAD_DAY or NO_SUCH_LINE as it would for
    any out-of-range day; the app puts the button back and reloads.

======================================================================
3. WHO CAN GIVE AND WHO SEES WHAT
======================================================================

  GIVING
  - The giver: signed in, active, sharing steps (Friends is closed
    without sharing anyway).
  - The person: exactly the people friends_activity would show you
    other than yourself, at that moment: an accepted friend you can
    see (can_see: both active, neither blocked, not paused or hidden),
    who shares their steps.
  - So a block, an unfriend, a pause or sharing turned off stops new
    kudos at once, in both directions.

  THE COUNT ON A LINE
  - Counted only from givers who are, right now, the line's person's
    friend, someone they can see (can_see) and sharing steps (KUD-5).
    An unfriended, deleted, paused or blocked giver's kudos drop out
    of the count on the next load, the same way their own lines drop
    out.
  - Everyone who sees the line sees the same count. A count says how
    many, never who, so it tells you nothing about the person's other
    friends.

  WHO GAVE KUDOS (YOUR OWN LINES ONLY, KUD-4)
  - Tap the count on your own line: a short list of character heads
    and display names, newest first, each opening that person's card.
  - Givers who no longer pass the rule above are left out. The count
    on the line and the "new kudos" number use the same rule, so they
    always agree.
  - On a friend's line you see the count and whether YOU gave kudos,
    never the list.

  GROUPS (LATER)
  - Kudos come to a group's Activity view with group_activity
    (activity board, section 11), with the group's rules: current
    members. SAFE-1 lets blocked people see each other in a shared
    group, but a kudos is contact, so the button is hidden and the
    server refuses between a blocked pair, even in a group.
  - Quest lines (the group's, not a person's) get no kudos.

======================================================================
4. TELLING THE PERSON (INSIDE THE APP ONLY)
======================================================================

  NO PUSHES (KUD-7)
  - Kudos never send a push notification, never play a sound, and
    never change the number on the app icon. A person who isn't in
    the app hears about kudos the next time they open it.

  "NEW" MEANS NOT YET SHOWN TO YOU (KUD-11)
  - One time per account, kudos_seen_at, kept by the server, so two
    phones agree. New kudos = kudos on your lines (counted as in
    section 3) that arrived after it.
  - Opening Activity does NOT move it. Activity is paged (20 lines,
    up to 30 days back), so a kudos on a line from 12 days ago may
    be on a page the person never opens. Clearing on the first page
    would mark it seen without it ever being on screen.
  - It moves only when the "New kudos" list (section 5) has shown
    every new kudos, and only up to the newest kudos that list
    actually contained, never to "now". A kudos that arrives while the
    list is open, or between loading it and marking it, stays new.
  - Since a person has at most one line a day and kudos only go on
    the last 30 days, the list is at most 31 lines: always one
    screen's worth of loading, no paging, so "everything new" can
    really be shown at once.

  THE DOT ON THE FRIENDS TAB (KUD-6)
  - notification_counts() returns new_kudos beside friend_requests,
    so it arrives in the same trip, at the same moments: opening the
    app, coming back to the foreground, opening Friends.
  - A friend request still shows its red number. With no requests
    and some new kudos, the Friends tab shows a small dot (no number):
    a dot is "something to see", a number is "something needs you",
    so the two never mean the same thing.
  - The dot goes once the "New kudos" list has been seen (the counts
    are asked for again right after it is marked seen). Opening
    Activity alone leaves it.

  IN THE ACTIVITY VIEW
  - "3 new kudos" at the top, above the lines, when new_kudos is more
    than 0. Tapping it opens the "New kudos" list (section 5), not a
    scroll: the lines it points to may not be loaded.
  - Your own lines with new kudos show a small dot by the count, on
    whichever page they are. The dots stay until the "New kudos" list
    is seen; then the next load drops them.
  - Tapping the count on one of your lines (who gave kudos for that
    day) doesn't clear anything. "New" is one time for the account,
    so clearing it from one line would also clear other lines' kudos
    that haven't been shown. Only the list that shows them all can.
  - Friends opens on Last 7 days by default (ACT-1). With new kudos,
    the Activity button in the picker shows the same small dot, so
    the person knows where to look.

  IF MARKING SEEN FAILS OR IS CUT OFF
  - Two separate calls: new_kudos() loads the list, then
    mark_kudos_seen(up_to) records it. The rule is that kudos stay
    new until the server confirms the second call. Nothing is cleared
    on the phone first.
  - The list loads, the seen call fails (offline, an error): the list
    stays on screen. The dots, the bar and the tab dot stay. The app
    keeps that up_to in memory and tries once more when the app next
    comes back to the foreground, and again whenever the list is
    shown. No error message: the person has already seen the kudos,
    and the worst outcome is that they show as new once more.
  - The app closes, crashes or loses its connection between the two
    calls: nothing was recorded, so the same kudos are new on the next
    open. They are shown again; nothing is lost.
  - The seen call reached the server but the answer was lost: the
    time moved, and the next counts load shows 0. Repeating the call
    with the same up_to changes nothing (greatest(), section 7), so
    a retry is always safe.
  - The remembered up_to is for the signed-in account only. It is
    dropped on sign-out or an account switch, never sent for another
    account, and not saved to the phone (a lost retry only means the
    kudos show as new again).
  - Once the server confirms, the app asks for the counts again
    (tab dot and bar) and drops the dots on the next board load.

  WHEN IT UPDATES
  - Only at the existing moments above, and on pull-to-refresh in
    Activity. No live updates (ACT-4) and nothing in the background.
  - A failed refresh keeps the last good counts, as the tab badges do
    now; signing out clears them.

======================================================================
5. SCREENS
======================================================================

  A FRIEND'S LINE
    [head] Sam                                       [badge]
           reached their goal · unlocked first 10k
           Yesterday                             [clap] 4

  - The clap is an outline when you haven't given kudos, filled in
    (gold) when you have. The count is hidden at 0.
  - The clap has its own touch area (at least 44 points), apart from
    the line, which still opens the player card.
  - Tap: fills in and counts up straight away; a small one-time
    "pop" (two frames, pixel art, no system emoji). If the server
    refuses or the phone is offline, it goes back to the outline and
    shows "Couldn't send kudos." under the line for a few seconds.
  - Already given: the button is filled and does nothing on tap.

  YOUR OWN LINE
    [head] You                                       [badge]
           reached your goal · +150 coins
           Yesterday                          [clap] 4 •

  - No button to give; the count (with a dot when new) opens "Kudos
    for Thu, Oct 16": the list of givers (section 3).
  - At 0 nothing is shown.

  THE "NEW KUDOS" LIST (opened from the "3 new kudos" bar)
    New kudos
    Thu, Oct 16  reached your goal
                 [head][head] Ana and Sam
    Mon, Oct 6   unlocked a week in a row
                 [head] Lee

  - One row per line of yours with new kudos, newest kudos first,
    each with the line's words (the same lineText as the board), the
    day, and the new givers' heads and names ("Ana, Sam and 2 more"
    past two). Kudos you'd already seen on that line aren't repeated.
  - A giver's head opens their card. Closing the list returns to
    Activity.
  - When the list has loaded and is on screen, the app marks it
    seen (mark_kudos_seen with the list's up_to, section 7). If the
    load fails, nothing is marked and the bar stays.
  - The app shows the dots and the bar until the server confirms
    the seen update (below). It never hides them early.
  - A line the old app can't describe (an unknown achievement id)
    still gets a row ("your day") so its kudos can be seen and
    cleared.

  ACCESSIBILITY
  - The clap reads "Give Sam kudos, 4 so far" / "You gave Sam kudos,
    4 in all". Your own: "4 kudos, 1 new, opens who gave them".
  - Each line still reads as one sentence; the kudos part comes at
    the end: "Sam, reached their goal, yesterday, 4 kudos."
  - The tab dot reads as "Friends, new kudos".

  ART (PIXEL ART, PER THE MAP DIRECTION)
  - kudos_clap and kudos_clap_filled, and a two-frame pop, drawn in
    scripts/generate-avatar-sprites.py beside badge_goal, at the
    same pixel scale as the badges.

======================================================================
6. ORDER, LOADING AND OLD APPS
======================================================================

  - friends_activity() returns two more fields on every line:
      kudos     the count (section 3)
      my_kudos  true if you gave kudos on it (always false on yours)
    and, on your own lines, new_kudos: how many arrived since
    kudos_seen_at.
  - The board's order and paging don't change: kudos don't move a
    line up, so "Show older" can't skip or repeat anything.
  - An app from before kudos ignores the extra fields (and the extra
    count in notification_counts). An app with kudos talking to a
    server without them sees no field and hides the button and the
    dot, so the migration and the build can ship in either order.
  - After giving kudos, the app keeps its own +1 on that line until
    the next load replaces the list, then trusts the server's count.

======================================================================
7. DATABASE
======================================================================

  NEW TABLE
    kudos
      giver_id    uuid, references profiles, on delete cascade
      user_id     uuid, references profiles, on delete cascade
                  (the person whose line it is)
      day         date (the line's day)
      created_at  timestamptz, default now()
      primary key (giver_id, user_id, day)
      check (giver_id <> user_id)
    index on (user_id, day) for the counts, and on
    (user_id, created_at) for "new since".
  - Row level security on, and no access from the app at all (like
    push_devices). Everything goes through the functions below.

  NEW COLUMN
  - notification_settings.kudos_seen_at timestamptz, null. A missing
    settings row means "never seen", so every kudos in range is new.

  FUNCTIONS (APP-FACING)
  - give_kudos(p_user uuid, p_day date, my_today date): checks
    section 3 and that (p_user, p_day) is a line on the board
    (a 'daily_goal' coin row for that day with the day still uploaded
    and after goal_is_coin_target_from, or an achievement earned that
    day), my_today passes is_today_somewhere (BAD_DAY otherwise) and
    my_today - 29 <= p_day <= my_today + 1 (BAD_DAY otherwise; the
    window in section 2). Then "insert ... on conflict do nothing", so
    a double tap or two phones give one kudos. Returns the line's new
    count. Errors: NOT_SIGNED_IN, ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE,
    SHARING_REQUIRED, CANT_SEE, NO_SUCH_LINE, BAD_DAY, SELF.
  - line_kudos(p_day date, my_today date): the givers on YOUR line
    for that day (id, display name, avatar, created_at), filtered as
    in section 3. my_today checked and p_day inside the window
    (section 2), BAD_DAY otherwise.
  - new_kudos(my_today date): the "New kudos" list. Every kudos on
    your lines inside the window (section 2, my_today checked, BAD_DAY
    otherwise) that arrived after kudos_seen_at,
    filtered as in section 3, grouped by line: day, goal,
    achievements, and the new givers (id, display name, avatar,
    created_at). At most 31 lines, no paging. Also returns up_to: the
    newest created_at in the answer (null when empty).
  - mark_kudos_seen(p_up_to timestamptz): sets kudos_seen_at to
    greatest(kudos_seen_at, p_up_to), and never past now(). Called by
    the app once the "New kudos" list is on screen, with that list's
    up_to. Because it moves only as far as what was shown, a kudos
    that arrived after the list was loaded stays new; because it
    never moves back, a slow call from a second phone can't bring old
    kudos back. Kept separate from the reads so friends_activity and
    new_kudos stay "stable" functions.
    (Two kudos with the same created_at as up_to: a later-loaded one
    with an identical time would be cleared unseen. created_at is
    microseconds and each give is its own transaction, so this is
    ignored; if it ever matters, up_to becomes (created_at, giver,
    day).)
  - friends_activity(): replaced, same arguments, adds kudos,
    my_kudos and new_kudos (section 6).
  - notification_counts_for() / notification_counts(): add
    new_kudos (0 for an account that isn't active or isn't sharing,
    like friend_requests). The push code keeps reading only
    friend_requests and quest_votes, so the app icon number is
    unchanged.
  - All: security definer, set search_path = '', revoke from public,
    anon, authenticated, execute granted to authenticated only.

  SERVER ONLY
  - A daily pg_cron job deletes kudos older than 35 days (KUD-10).
    Lines older than 30 days aren't on the board, so the table stays
    at most a month of rows.
  - No trigger and nothing sent: giving a kudos is a single insert.

  COST
  - The count is a lookup on (user_id, day) per line, 20 lines a
    page; new_kudos is one indexed count per counts call. Small, like
    the rest of the board.

======================================================================
8. WHAT KUDOS ARE NOT
======================================================================

  - Not a push notification (KUD-7).
  - Not a message: no text, no emoji choice, nothing to report. Block
    is the answer to someone you don't want kudos from.
  - Not coins, not progress toward an achievement (KUD-9). A reward
    for receiving kudos would invite asking friends to tap.
  - Not a ranking: no "most kudos" anywhere.
  - Not on the Today or Last 7 days rankings: only on the board's
    lines, which say what someone did.

======================================================================
9. TRICKY CASES
======================================================================

  - Double tap, or two of your phones at once: one row (the primary
    key).
  - Sam unfriends you after you gave kudos: your row stays in the
    table, but you're no longer Sam's friend, so it drops out of the
    count, Sam's list and Sam's new_kudos (KUD-5). Becoming friends
    again brings it back, while the row exists (35 days, KUD-10) and
    the line is in the window, to the count and the list (KUD-12).
  - Is a restored kudos "new" again? Only if Sam never had it shown.
    "New" is decided by the kudos' own created_at against Sam's
    kudos_seen_at, and neither changes when the friendship does:
      - Sam had seen it (it was in a "New kudos" list Sam saw): its
        time is before kudos_seen_at, so it comes back in the count
        and the list, with no dot and no "new kudos" bar.
      - Sam had never seen it (the unfriend came first): it is still
        new, because it was never shown. It brings the dot back once.
    The same holds for a block lifted, a pause ended or sharing
    turned back on. Intentional (KUD-12).
  - Blocked either way: the kudos disappears from count, list and
    dot on the next load, and no new kudos either way.
  - A giver deletes their account: their rows go (on delete cascade).
  - Sam deletes the day: no line, so no kudos shown or counted as new
    (section 2). If Sam earned an achievement that day, the line
    stays, with its kudos.
  - A friend ahead of you in time zones is on "today" while you're on
    yesterday: their line's day is your my_today + 1, the newest day
    in the window (section 2), so it shows and kudos work.
  - A friend two dates ahead: UTC-12 and UTC+14 are 26 hours apart,
    so for up to two hours a day a friend's date can be your
    my_today + 2. The board doesn't show that line yet, so it can't
    get kudos (give_kudos refuses it with BAD_DAY). It appears, and
    can get kudos, once your own date moves on. The same as the board
    today; kudos don't change it.
  - Kudos on the oldest line (my_today - 29) just before midnight:
    after midnight the phone sends the new date, the line is now
    my_today - 30, and the server refuses with BAD_DAY. The button
    goes back to the outline and the board reloads without that line.
  - The phone's clock or time zone is wrong: a my_today that isn't
    today anywhere is refused with BAD_DAY, as on the board.
  - Kudos arrive while you're in the app: nothing changes on screen
    until the next refresh moment (foreground, opening Friends,
    pull-to-refresh). This is the trade for no pushes and no live
    updates; live updates can be added later (section 11).
  - Kudos arrive while the app is closed: nothing happens on the
    phone. The dot is there the next time the app opens.
  - Ten friends give kudos: "10 new kudos" in Activity, and still
    one dot on the tab.
  - Two phones: seeing the list on one clears "new" on the other at
    its next refresh (the seen time is on the server). Both open the
    list at once: each marks up to what it showed, the larger wins,
    nothing goes backwards.
  - A kudos on a line from 12 days ago: its line is on page 2 of
    Activity, but it's in the "New kudos" list, and stays new until
    that list is seen.
  - The person opens Activity, never taps the bar: the dots stay.
    They go when the kudos leave the 30 days (the line is no longer
    on the board), or when the list is seen.
  - A kudos arrives while the "New kudos" list is open: it isn't in
    the list, so it stays new and the bar shows "1 new kudos" after
    the next refresh.
  - An old app: no button, no count, no dot.

======================================================================
10. CHOICES (ALL DECIDED OCTOBER 2026, AS SUGGESTED)
======================================================================

  KUD-1  Name: "Kudos".
    Why:  it's what the request calls it, and one word fits the line.
    Other: "Cheer" (the activity board's earlier word).

  KUD-2  Icon: a pixel-art clap (two hands), outline and gold.
    Why:  a heart reads as "like" and a thumbs-up as "approve"; a
    clap reads as "well done", which is what a goal day earns.
    Other: a high-five, a star, a flame.

  KUD-3  One per person per line, and it can't be taken back.
    Why:  simple, and no "they took it back" surprises. The cost is
    a mis-tap stays. With no pushes, taking back would be harmless
    too, so this is an easy one to flip.
    Other: allow taking back (the row is removed).

  KUD-4  Who gave kudos: listed on your own lines only; others see a
    count.
    Why:  the list on Sam's line would show you Sam's other friends,
    who may not be yours.
    Other: anyone who can see the line sees the list (filtered to
    people they can see too).

  KUD-5  The count, the list and "new" use the same live rule: givers
    who are still the person's friend, can be seen, and share steps.
    Why:  they never disagree, and unfriend / block clean up on their
    own, like the board.

  KUD-6  A small dot (no number) on the Friends tab and on the
    Activity button for new kudos.
    Why:  without pushes, the dot is how people find out; a dot, not
    a number, keeps the red number meaning "something needs you"
    (notifications blueprint, section 5).
    Other: no tab dot, only "new kudos" inside Activity (quieter,
    but easy to miss); or a short in-app banner ("Ana gave you
    kudos") when the app opens with new kudos.

  KUD-7  No push notifications for kudos.
    Why:  the person asked for an in-app notice only.

  KUD-8  (Dropped with KUD-7: no "Kudos" switch under Notifications
    and no Android channel are needed.)

  KUD-9  No coins and no achievements for kudos.
    Why:  coins and achievements reward walking. Rewarding kudos
    would reward asking for them.
    Other, later: an achievement for GIVING kudos (e.g. 50 given).

  KUD-10 Kudos kept 35 days, then deleted.
    Why:  the board shows 30 days; nothing older is ever shown.

  KUD-11 "New" clears only when the "New kudos" list has shown every
    new kudos, and only up to the newest one it showed. Opening
    Activity doesn't clear it.
    Why:  Activity is paged over 30 days, so loading its first page
    says nothing about what the person saw. One list of at most 31
    lines can always show everything new at once, so one seen time per
    account is enough; no per-kudos "seen" flag is needed.
    Other: a "seen" mark on each kudos row, set as lines are shown
    (needs the app to report which lines were on screen; more writes
    and more to test). Or a narrower window: only kudos on the last 7
    days' lines count as new, so they're always on the first page.
    That changes what "notified" means, so it would be a product
    decision, not a fix.

  KUD-12 Kudos hidden by an unfriend, block, pause or sharing turned
    off come back to the count and the list when that is undone (while
    the row is kept and the line is in the window). They are new again
    only if they had never been shown; a kudos already seen never
    brings the dot back.
    Why:  "new" means "not yet shown to you", and a restored
    friendship doesn't change what you've seen. No extra state needed:
    created_at and kudos_seen_at already decide it.
    Other: drop the rows on unfriend or block, so nothing comes back.

  KUD-13 The date window is the activity board's, unchanged: today is
    day 0, oldest my_today - 29, newest my_today + 1, my_today
    checked with is_today_somewhere (section 2).
    Why:  a kudos button must work on exactly the lines the board
    shows. A second set of date rules would leave lines you can see
    but can't cheer, or the other way round.

  These are in step-tracker-decisions.txt, section 15. Each "Other"
  above is the option not chosen.

12. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database/kudos.test.sql)
  Giving
  - A friend's goal line and achievement-only line: kudos works and
    returns the count; a second give returns the same count and adds
    no row.
  - The window, at its exact edges (the same days as the board's
    tests): my_today - 29 accepted, my_today - 30 refused;
    my_today + 1 (a friend ahead of you) accepted, my_today + 2
    refused; my_today itself accepted. A my_today that isn't today
    anywhere (e.g. current_date + 5) refused with BAD_DAY, by
    give_kudos, line_kudos and new_kudos alike. For each edge,
    friends_activity shows the line exactly when give_kudos accepts
    it.
  - Refused: your own line (SELF); a day with no line, a deleted day
    with no achievements, a day before goal_is_coin_target_from
    (NO_SUCH_LINE); outside the window or a bad my_today (BAD_DAY);
    a stranger, a former friend, blocked either way, paused, hidden,
    not sharing, under-age or unfinished (CANT_SEE); a caller who is
    signed out, inactive or not sharing.
  Counting, listing and "new"
  - friends_activity returns kudos and my_kudos on every line and
    new_kudos on yours only; an unfriended, blocked or deleted giver
    drops out of the count, line_kudos and new_kudos together.
  - line_kudos only ever returns your own lines' givers.
  - notification_counts: new_kudos counts kudos after kudos_seen_at
    (all of them with no settings row); 0 when not active or not
    sharing; friend_requests and quest_votes unchanged.
  - new_kudos(): returns every new kudos in the 30 days, including
    one on a line 12 days old; no kudos already seen; grouped one row
    per line; up_to is the newest created_at returned.
  - friends_activity and new_kudos change nothing: calling them
    leaves kudos_seen_at as it was.
  - mark_kudos_seen(up_to): a kudos created after up_to stays new;
    an older up_to never moves the time back; a future up_to is held
    to now(); the same up_to twice changes nothing.
  - Friendship restored: a kudos seen before the unfriend comes back
    in the count and line_kudos but not in new_kudos; one never seen
    comes back in new_kudos too. The same for a block removed.
  - Giving a kudos sends nothing: no push_sends row.
  Permissions
  - No direct read or write on kudos for anon or authenticated; the
    functions are security definer with an empty search_path; execute
    for authenticated only.
  Cleanup
  - The daily job removes rows older than 35 days and nothing newer.

  APP (npm test)
  - The clap's states (none, given, own line, new dot) and labels.
  - Giving: the +1 straight away, going back on an error, keeping the
    +1 until the next load, and a load replacing it.
  - The tab shows a number for requests, a dot for kudos only, and
    the number when there are both.
  - Opening Activity doesn't mark seen; the "New kudos" list marks
    seen only after it loads, with its own up_to; a failed load marks
    nothing.
  - A failed seen call keeps the dots and the bar, and retries with
    the same up_to on the next foreground and the next time the list
    shows; a confirmed one refreshes the counts; an account switch
    drops the pending up_to.
  - Hiding the button and the dot when the server sends no kudos
    fields.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
