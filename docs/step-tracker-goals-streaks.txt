STEP TRACKER - THE DAILY GOAL, AND STREAKS (BLUEPRINT)
Two things people notice every day:
  A. The daily goal: each player's daily target, 7,000 to 10,000 steps,
     the same one that pays the daily coins (see the first change below;
     people could pick their own goal until October 2026)
  B. Streaks: days in a row at the goal (shown, never paid in coins)
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database and screens need, and the
decisions made (section 6). Items 2a and 2b of
step-tracker-recommendations.txt.

CHANGED IN OCTOBER 2026: THE GOAL IS THE DAILY TARGET (step-tracker-
decisions.txt, GOAL-16)
  - A day's goal is the player's daily coin target: 7,000 to 10,000
    steps in 500s, different for each player each day, worked out by
    the server (coin_steps_on). One number now does both jobs: reach it
    and the day counts for your streak, History and reminders, and pays
    the 150 daily coins.
  - Today: "62% of 8,500 goal · Daily roll at goal", then the daily
    roll once reached (step-tracker-daily-roll.txt, section 5; until
    October 2026 "· 150 coins at goal", then "+150 coins").
  - The step track's flag stays at 10,000, where the road ends until
    someone passes it; that tick reads "10k" rather than GOAL. There is
    no per-player marker (see GOAL-15 on why it was removed).
  - It started on the day it shipped. The switch counts from the day
    before the server's date, so it covers "today" on every phone; a
    target is never above 10,000, so no day got harder. Days before the
    switch keep their goal (10,000, or a personal goal from before).
  - Built in 20261117000000_goal_is_coin_target.sql: goal_on() returns
    coin_steps_on() from the switch day (the app_settings row
    goal_is_coin_target_from, in days since 2000-01-01), and the stored
    goals before it. Every reader of the goal goes through goal_on, so
    my_goal, my_history, the streaks and the reminders follow.
  - Tested in supabase/tests/database/goal_is_coin_target.test.sql. The
    older goal tests switch the setting off, since they check the
    stored goals that past days keep.

CHANGED IN OCTOBER 2026: PERSONAL GOALS REMOVED (step-tracker-decisions.txt,
GOAL-15; the goal then became the daily target, GOAL-16, above)
  - People can no longer pick their own goal. It was one more setting
    on a crowded Profile page and one more marker on the road. The
    daily goal is 10,000 for everyone again.
  - Gone from the app: Profile's "Daily goal" section (the picker, the
    start-date note, and the best-streak line that sat under it), and
    the MINE marker on the step track. 10,000 always reads GOAL.
  - Unchanged: Today's progress, History, streaks and goal reminders
    still follow "the goal", which is now 10,000. The daily coin target
    (7,000 to 10,000, different each day) is separate and stays.
  - Past days keep the goal that applied to them, so History and
    streaks don't change looking back. Anyone with another goal moved
    to 10,000 from the day after the change (that day kept its goal,
    like any goal change); changes still waiting to start were dropped.
  - Built in 20261116000000_remove_personal_goal.sql: those two data
    steps, and set_daily_goal() dropped, so the app can't set a goal.
    set_daily_goal_at() stays: it is internal (never callable from the
    app) and the tests use it to give past days other goals.
  - Part A below is kept as the record of how personal goals worked.
    Where it describes picking or changing a goal, it no longer
    applies.

CHANGED EARLIER IN OCTOBER 2026 (step-tracker-decisions.txt, GOAL-1, 2 and 6)
  - The goal range is now 5,000 to 20,000 (was 3,000 to 25,000).
  - Daily coins have their own target: 7,000 to 10,000 steps, random
    for each player each day (was 10,000 for everyone).
  - Your own goal earns no coins at all: the 7- and 30-day streak
    bonuses are gone. Streaks are still shown everywhere they were.
  Built in 20261112000000_goal_range_coin_steps.sql. The sections
  below describe the rules as they are now; where an older rule was
  replaced, it says so.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKED BEFORE GOALS AND STREAKS
======================================================================

  - The goal was 10,000 steps for everyone, written in several places:
      Today's progress ring and "% of 10,000 goal"
                                     (DailyStepGoal, constants/theme.ts)
      History's bars (full colour at 10,000)
      Coins: 100 coins for each day at 10,000 or more
                                     (claim_daily_rewards, the server)
      The step track's scale and its finish flag at 10,000 (scale.ts,
      themes.ts): the road everyone walks on together
      The admin view of suspicious steps
  - No streaks. The server keeps every uploaded day (daily_steps), so a
    streak can be worked out from what is already there.
  - Steps reach the server only for people sharing them (the consent
    screen); coins already need sharing.

======================================================================
2. PART A - YOUR OWN DAILY GOAL (REMOVED IN OCTOBER 2026, GOAL-15)
======================================================================

  Everything in this part about picking, changing or showing your own
  goal no longer applies: the goal is the daily target (GOAL-16). The
  coin rules (COINS, below) still do, and the target they describe is
  now the goal too.

  WHAT YOU CAN PICK (DECIDED)
  - From 5,000 to 20,000, in steps of 500. Starts at 10,000,
    so nothing changes for anyone until they pick.
    (Was 3,000 to 25,000. When the range changed, anyone with a goal
    outside it moved to the nearest end of it from the next day; their
    past goals were left as they were.)
  - Set in Profile ("Daily goal") and offered once on Today the first
    time (a small "Set your goal" hint, dismissed for good).

  WHAT FOLLOWS YOUR GOAL
  - Today: the progress percentage and its label are relative to your
    goal for that day (whatever it is: "% of 10,000 goal" for someone
    who never changed it, "% of 7,500 goal" after picking 7,500).
  - History: a day at your goal is shown full colour.
  - Streaks (Part B).
  - Nothing else. In particular, your goal never pays coins.

  WHAT STAYS THE SAME FOR EVERYONE
  - The step track: it's one road that friends and group mates share,
    so its scale and finish flag stay at 0 to 10,000 for all. Your own
    goal shows as a small marker on the road, on your phone only
    (decided).
  - Group quests: their goals are the group's (they grow with the
    party, step-tracker-group-quests.txt), never anyone's own goal.
  - The admin's suspicious-steps view: its checks stay exactly as they
    are (days over 40,000, round numbers, days at 10,000). Personal
    goals never change how cheating is spotted; a 5,000 goal makes no
    difference to it.

  COINS (DECIDED: A DAILY COIN TARGET, NOTHING FOR YOUR OWN GOAL)
  - 150 coins for each day you reach that day's coin target (was
    100 until the coin rebalance, COIN-2). The
    target is 7,000 to 10,000 steps, in 500s, picked at random for
    each player for each day. It has nothing to do with your goal: a
    5,000 goal and a 20,000 goal get the same kind of target.
  - Random but fixed: the server works it out from the player and the
    day, so it is the same every time it's asked, on every phone, and
    can't be rerolled. my_goal() returns it as coin_steps, and Today
    shows it as the goal ("· Daily roll at goal" until it's reached;
    the daily roll replaces the 150 coins, step-tracker-daily-roll.txt).
  - Why random: everyone has the same chances over time, so the shop
    stays fair between people, and a target that moves keeps the day
    a little less predictable than a fixed 10,000.
  - Your own goal pays nothing: no coins for reaching it and no streak
    bonuses (Part B). It is for you, not for the coin economy.
  - Not chosen: coins for reaching your own goal, scaled by it (it
    would make a low goal the best way to earn); and, earlier, a
    fixed 10,000 for everyone (the rule until October 2026).

  CHANGING YOUR GOAL
  - A new goal counts from tomorrow: the day after the phone's date
    (exact rule below). Today keeps the goal it started with, so in the
    app, lowering it at 9pm doesn't rescue today's streak. A phone
    whose date has been tampered with can get around that: see "What a
    tampered phone can do" below (accepted, decision 14).
    Profile shows the start date the server returns.
  - Every change is kept (which goal from which day). Changing your
    goal never changes the goal that applied to a past day. Late step
    uploads within the accepted window can still update past results:
    whether a day reached its goal, and the streaks.
  - Changed more than once on the same day: the LAST choice wins for
    the start date the server returned, and every change stays in the
    history. Example: on Monday, 8,000 in the morning, then 6,000 in
    the afternoon; both start on Tuesday (the date the server returned
    for each), so Tuesday's goal is 6,000.
  - Never changed: 10,000, for every past day and every future day,
    until the first change takes effect.
  WHICH DAY IS "TOMORROW" (DECIDED: THE PHONE'S TOMORROW)
  - Goal days are the same local dates the phone uploads steps under
    (daily_steps.day), so a goal and a day's steps always line up.
  - set_daily_goal(goal, my_today) takes the phone's local date, the
    same way the leaderboards do (step-tracker-safety.txt, section 2):
    the server accepts it only if it is "today" somewhere on Earth
    right now (between the UTC-12 and UTC+14 dates), and the new goal
    starts the day after it. The server returns that date and Profile
    shows it: "Your new goal starts on Tue, Nov 12".
  - Travelling: the date is the phone's date when the goal is changed;
    days keep being the phone's local dates, as for steps.
  WHAT A TAMPERED PHONE CAN DO (ACCEPTED, decision 14)
  - By sending an earlier date that is still "today" somewhere, it can
    make a new, lower goal apply to a day already under way:
      - its own today, almost anywhere
      - for people in UTC+13 / UTC+14, at some hours, its own yesterday
        too (the earliest accepted date can be two days behind theirs)
    So yes: it can rescue a streak on a day it would otherwise have
    missed.
  - What it can NOT do: earn anything. Your goal pays no coins, so a
    rescued day earns nothing a real one wouldn't (the daily coins
    follow the coin target, which the phone can't touch). And its
    streak can never be longer than an honest player's who simply set
    the lowest goal, 5,000, and left it: every goal is at least 5,000,
    so any day it rescues has at least 5,000 steps, and the honest
    5,000 player is credited for that same day. Friends don't see
    anyone's goal, so the streak on the player card looks the same too.
  - What it buys, then: showing a higher goal on your own screen while
    being judged at no more than 5,000 when that suits you. Every change
    is in the goal history, so it can be seen afterwards.
  - Not worth closing at the cost of the strict rule below. If that
    ever changes (e.g. streaks pay coins again), the fix is the strict
    rule, which no phone can move.
  - Not chosen: starting from the day after the latest "today" on
    Earth (UTC+14). It can't be moved by the phone, but for most of the
    world, for most of the day, the new goal would start the day after
    tomorrow (US Pacific from about 3am, Western Europe from about
    11am).

  - In the database: a small table of goal changes (who, from which
    day, the goal, and when it was chosen), readable by you only,
    written only through a function that checks the range and works
    out the day as above. The goal for a day is the latest change whose day
    is on or before it; two changes for the same day are told apart by
    when they were chosen, the later one winning. The table itself
    still accepts the old range (3,000 to 25,000), so changes made
    before October 2026 stay as they were; only set_daily_goal checks
    the new one.
  - Friends and group mates don't see your goal (they do see your
    streak, Part B).

======================================================================
3. PART B - STREAKS
======================================================================

  WHAT A STREAK IS
  - The number of days in a row, ending today or yesterday, on which
    you reached the goal you had that day.
  - Today counts as soon as you reach it. Until then, a streak that ran
    through yesterday is still alive, and Today says so:
      "5-day streak. 2,140 steps to keep it going."
  - Missing a whole day ends it. No rest days or "streak freezes" for
    now (decided).
  - Your best streak ever is kept too.

  WHERE IT COMES FROM
  - Worked out by the server from the uploaded days (daily_steps) and
    your goal history, every time it's asked (it's never stored), so
    it's the same on every phone and survives a new phone. It needs step sharing, like coins. Someone not sharing
    sees "Share your steps to keep a streak" instead.
  - Days are the local dates the phone uploads, as everywhere else
    (step-tracker-step-refresh.txt), so travelling doesn't break it.
  - Older days can't be changed (the server already refuses uploads for
    days more than 8 days back), so a streak can't be built backwards
    further than that.

  LATE UPLOADS
  - A day can look missing because the phone hadn't synced yet. When
    its steps arrive later (within the 8-day window), the streak is
    simply worked out again with them: a streak that looked broken
    comes back, and the best streak can go up.

  WHEN IT LAUNCHED (DECIDED: SHOW HISTORY)
  - Current and best streaks are shown from the days already uploaded,
    judged against 10,000 (everyone's goal until then). Someone already
    on a 12-day run saw "12-day streak" on day one.

  NO COINS FOR STREAKS (DECIDED, OCTOBER 2026)
  - Streaks pay no coins. They are shown on Today, History and the
    player card, and they count for the streak achievements (whose
    rewards are items, not coins: step-tracker-achievements.txt).
  - Before October 2026 a streak paid 200 coins on reaching 7 days and
    1,000 on reaching 30, following your own goal. That was removed
    with the rest of "your goal pays no coins" (Part A, Coins).
    Bonuses already paid stay paid, and stay in the coin history as
    "streak_7" and "streak_30"; none are paid from then on.
  - Gone with them: the launch-day cutoff for bonuses (the
    streak_bonus_from setting), the "paid once" rules for runs joined
    by a late upload, and the two-phone streak race test.
  - Still in place: claim_daily_rewards takes a per-person lock before
    it reads the uploads, so two phones claiming at once go in turn.
    That keeps the daily coins and the achievements check (which needs
    to see every upload) right; see
    supabase/tests/concurrency/achievement_race.sh.

  WHO SEES YOUR STREAK (DECIDED: FRIENDS AND GROUP MATES)
  - Shown on your player card to friends and group mates ("12-day
    streak"), the same people who already see your steps. Off for
    people who aren't sharing.

  SCREENS
  - Today: the streak under the progress ("5-day streak · best 12"),
    and on the day it's at risk, how many steps keep it going. The
    progress line reads "62% of 8,500 goal · Daily roll at goal",
    then the daily roll once it's reached (the goal is the coin target,
    GOAL-16; step-tracker-daily-roll.txt, section 5).
  - Profile: nothing (until October 2026 it had the Daily goal
    picker, a note on coins and your best streak; GOAL-15).
  - Player card: "12-day streak".
  - History: days at the goal stand out, each judged by the goal that
    applied that day (the daily target since GOAL-16; 10,000 or a
    personal goal before it).

  BUILT (20261108000000_goals_streaks.sql, then
  20261112000000_goal_range_coin_steps.sql)
  - Notes from building:
      - Goals: daily_goal_changes, set_daily_goal / my_goal; the goal
        for a day is goal_on(). Streaks: goal_runs / streak_info, worked
        out on every read; my_streak, player_streak, and 'streak' in
        group_member_card (added to its approved fields).
      - The coin target for a day is coin_steps_on(player, day), an
        internal function (7,000 + 500 x a hash of the two, mod 7). The
        day goes into the hash as a day number, not text, so the
        database's date format can't change it.
      - claim_daily_rewards pays 100 for each day in the last 7 at or
        over that day's coin target, under a per-person lock
        (coins:<id>), then checks achievements. It pays no streak
        bonuses (the first version did, 7 and 30 days).
      - Today counts as done the moment the phone's own steps reach the
        goal, before the upload, so the streak line moves at once.
      - The track: the flag at 10,000, its tick reading "10k" (GOAL-16;
        it read GOAL before that). (Until GOAL-15 a personal goal
        showed as a MINE tick or marker, and 10,000 read GOAL only
        while it was your goal too.)

======================================================================

  1. The goal: the goal history table and set_daily_goal(), Today and
     History reading your goal, the Profile picker.

  3. The 7- and 30-day bonuses (removed in October 2026).
  4. The player card.
  5. October 2026: the 5,000 to 20,000 range, the daily coin target on

     20261116000000_remove_personal_goal.sql); the Profile picker and
     the MINE marker taken out of the app.

     20261117000000_goal_is_coin_target.sql); Today shows one number.

5. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database/goal_is_coin_target.test.sql)
  - The goal before the switch is the stored one (10,000 if none);
    from the switch day it is the coin target, 7,000 to 10,000 in 500s,
    changing day to day; my_goal's goal and coin target are equal;
    walking exactly the target 5 days is a 5-day streak, one step short
    today isn't done; History judges by the target.

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database/goals_streaks.test.sql; the switch
  is off here, so recent days use the stored goals)
  - The app can't set a goal (set_daily_goal is gone). The tests below
    give past days other goals through the internal set_daily_goal_at,
    as personal goals did before GOAL-15.
  - Goals: 5,000 and 20,000 accepted; 4,500, 20,500 and 7,250 refused;
    a change counts from the day after the phone's date; only your own
    goal can be read or changed.
  - A past day is judged by the goal you had then: raise the goal, and
    yesterday's streak doesn't change.
  - Streaks: 5 days at goal ending yesterday = 5 and "at risk" today;
    reaching today makes it 6; a missed day in between ends it; best
    streak kept after a break; days not at goal don't count; a day with
    no upload breaks it; a missing day uploaded late joins the run back
    together.
  - No coins for your goal: 7 days in a row pay only the daily coins
    (no bonus); a 5,000 goal met 7 days with no day at the coin target
    pays nothing at all and still has a 7-day streak; 100 days in a
    row pay no streak coins of any kind.
  - The coin target: every value is 7,000 to 10,000 in 500s, each one
    comes up, one player's target changes from day to day, my_goal
    returns today's, exactly the target pays and one step short
    doesn't.
  - Two goal changes on the same day: the later one is the goal from
    the returned start date; both are kept.
  - The start date: the day after the phone's date; a date that isn't
    "today" anywhere on Earth is refused; an earlier date that is still
    valid starts the goal on that date's next day (the accepted
    loophole), never on a day before it.
  - The loophole's limit, tested directly: a tampered date lowering
    a day's goal does rescue the streak; over the same days, a player
    who set 5,000 from the start has a streak at least as long, and
    the two earn the same coins.
  - Never changed: 10,000 for past and future days.
  - The suspicious-steps view gives the same answers with any
    personal goal.
  - Not sharing: no streak, no coins.
  - The internal helpers (including coin_steps_on) can't be called
    from the app.

  APP (npm test)
  - Progress and "steps to keep it going". (The goal picker's tests
    went with the picker, GOAL-15.)

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - Profile has no Daily goal section (GOAL-15). Today shows "% of N
    goal" with N from 7,000 to 10,000, the step track's 10,000 tick
    reads "10k" with the flag, and there's no MINE marker (GOAL-16).
  - Today shows the day's coin target; walking past it shows "+100
    coins", walking past only your goal doesn't.
  - Walk past your goal: the streak goes up, Today says so and offers
    the daily roll.
  - On the 7th day in a row: no coins for the streak.
  - A friend's player card shows your streak.

======================================================================
6. DECISIONS
======================================================================

  1. (No longer applies, GOAL-15: the goal is 10,000 for everyone.
     Was a range of 5,000 to 20,000 in steps of 500, starting at
     10,000; 3,000 to 25,000 before that.)
  2. Daily coins: a target of 7,000 to 10,000 steps, random for each
     player each day, whatever their goal; shown on Today. The step
     track still runs 0 to 10,000. (Was 10,000 for everyone.)
  3. (No longer applies, GOAL-15: no MINE marker on the track.)
  4. Your streak shows on your player card to friends and group mates.
  5. No rest days or streak freezes for now.
  6. Your own goal pays no coins: no streak bonuses. (Was 200 coins at
     7 days and 1,000 at 30, following the personal goal, until
     October 2026; bonuses already paid are kept.)
  7. (No longer applies, GOAL-15: goals can't be changed.) Past
     changes stay in the goal history and still judge their days.
  8. Launch: streaks are shown from existing history.
  9. Late uploads (within the 8-day window) update the streak.
  10. (Removed with the streak bonuses: joined runs and payments.)
  11. (Removed with the streak bonuses: the launch cutoff.)
  12. Claiming is done one at a time per person (a lock), with the
      coin history's once-only rule as a second layer.
  13. The suspicious-steps checks are unchanged by personal goals.
  14. A new goal starts the day after the phone's date (checked to be
      "today" somewhere on Earth); the server returns the date and
      Profile shows it. Goal days are the same local dates as
      daily_steps. A tampered phone can lower the goal for a day
      already under way (today; yesterday too in UTC+13/+14 at some
      hours) and so rescue a streak, but never earn any coins by it,
      and never have a longer streak than an honest player with a
      5,000 goal. Accepted; tested. (No longer applies, GOAL-15:
      goals can't be changed.)
  15. Personal goals removed (October 2026): the goal is 10,000 for
      everyone; past days keep the goal they had. (Changed by 16.)
  16. The goal is each player's daily target, the one that pays the
      daily coins (7,000 to 10,000, different each day). Days before
      the change keep their goal.
