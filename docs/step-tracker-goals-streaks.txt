STEP TRACKER - YOUR OWN DAILY GOAL, AND STREAKS (BLUEPRINT)
Two things people notice every day:
  A. A personal daily goal (today everyone's is 10,000)
  B. Streaks: days in a row at your goal, with coin bonuses
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database and screens need, and the
decisions made (section 6). Items 2a and 2b of
step-tracker-recommendations.txt.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  - The goal is 10,000 steps for everyone, written in several places:
      Today's progress ring and "% of 10,000 goal"
                                     (DailyStepGoal, constants/theme.ts)
      History's bars (full colour at 10,000)
      Coins: 100 coins for each day at 10,000 or more
                                     (claim_daily_rewards, the server)
      The step track's scale and its finish flag at 10,000 (scale.ts,
      themes.ts): the road everyone walks on together
      The admin view of suspicious steps
  - No streaks. The server keeps every uploaded day (daily_steps), so a
    streak can be worked out from what is already there.
  - Steps reach the server only for people sharing them (the consent
    screen); coins already need sharing.

======================================================================
2. PART A - YOUR OWN DAILY GOAL
======================================================================

  WHAT YOU CAN PICK (DECIDED)
  - From 3,000 to 25,000, in steps of 500. Starts at 10,000,
    so nothing changes for anyone until they pick.
  - Set in Profile ("Daily goal") and offered once on Today the first
    time (a small "Set your goal" hint, dismissed for good).

  WHAT FOLLOWS YOUR GOAL
  - Today: the progress percentage and its label are relative to your
    goal for that day (whatever it is: "% of 10,000 goal" for someone
    who never changed it, "% of 7,500 goal" after picking 7,500).
  - History: a day at your goal is shown full colour.
  - Streaks (Part B).

  WHAT STAYS AT 10,000 FOR EVERYONE
  - The step track: it's one road that friends and group mates share,
    so its scale and finish flag stay the same for all. Your own goal
    shows as a small marker on the road, on your phone only (decided).
  - Group quests: their goals are the group's, unchanged.
  - The admin's suspicious-steps view: its checks stay exactly as they
    are (days over 40,000, round numbers, days at 10,000). Personal
    goals never change how cheating is spotted; a 3,000 goal makes no
    difference to it.

  COINS (DECIDED: STAY AT 10,000)
  - The question: with a goal of 3,000, should a day at 3,000 earn the
    same 100 coins as 10,000 does today?
  - Coins stay at 10,000 steps, as today. The shop stays
    fair between people, and nobody loses coins they earn now. Reaching
    your own goal is rewarded by the streak (Part B) instead.
  - Not chosen: coins for reaching your own goal, scaled by it.

  CHANGING YOUR GOAL
  - A new goal counts from tomorrow: the day after the phone's date
    (exact rule below). Today keeps the goal it started with, so in the
    app, lowering it at 9pm doesn't rescue today's streak. A phone
    whose date has been tampered with can get around that: see "What a
    tampered phone can do" below (accepted, decision 14).
    Profile shows the start date the server returns.
  - Every change is kept (which goal from which day). Changing your
    goal never changes the goal that applied to a past day. Late step
    uploads within the accepted window can still update past results:
    whether a day reached its goal, and the streaks.
  - Changed more than once on the same day: the LAST choice wins for
    the start date the server returned, and every change stays in the
    history. Example: on Monday, 8,000 in the morning, then 6,000 in
    the afternoon; both start on Tuesday (the date the server returned
    for each), so Tuesday's goal is 6,000.
  - Never changed: 10,000, for every past day and every future day,
    until the first change takes effect.
  WHICH DAY IS "TOMORROW" (DECIDED: THE PHONE'S TOMORROW)
  - Goal days are the same local dates the phone uploads steps under
    (daily_steps.day), so a goal and a day's steps always line up.
  - set_daily_goal(goal, my_today) takes the phone's local date, the
    same way the leaderboards do (step-tracker-safety.txt, section 2):
    the server accepts it only if it is "today" somewhere on Earth
    right now (between the UTC-12 and UTC+14 dates), and the new goal
    starts the day after it. The server returns that date and Profile
    shows it: "Your new goal starts on Tue, Nov 12".
  - Travelling: the date is the phone's date when the goal is changed;
    days keep being the phone's local dates, as for steps.
  WHAT A TAMPERED PHONE CAN DO (ACCEPTED, decision 14)
  - By sending an earlier date that is still "today" somewhere, it can
    make a new, lower goal apply to a day already under way:
      - its own today, almost anywhere
      - for people in UTC+13 / UTC+14, at some hours, its own yesterday
        too (the earliest accepted date can be two days behind theirs)
    So yes: it can rescue a streak on a day it would otherwise have
    missed, and that can complete a 7- or 30-day bonus.
  - What it can NOT do: earn more than an honest player who simply set
    the lowest goal, 3,000, and left it. Every goal is at least 3,000,
    so any day it rescues has at least 3,000 steps, and the honest
    3,000 player is credited for that same day. Every streak the
    exploit can make, the 3,000 player has too, at least as long, so
    its streaks and bonuses can never be bigger. Friends don't see
    anyone's goal, so the streak on the player card looks the same too.
  - What it buys, then: showing a higher goal on your own screen while
    being judged at no more than 3,000 when that suits you. Every change
    is in the goal history, so it can be seen afterwards.
  - Not worth closing at the cost of the strict rule below. If that
    ever changes (e.g. bonuses become larger), the fix is the strict
    rule, which no phone can move.
  - Not chosen: starting from the day after the latest "today" on
    Earth (UTC+14). It can't be moved by the phone, but for most of the
    world, for most of the day, the new goal would start the day after
    tomorrow (US Pacific from about 3am, Western Europe from about
    11am).

  - In the database: a small table of goal changes (who, from which
    day, the goal, and when it was chosen), readable by you only,
    written only through a function that checks the range and works
    out the day as above. The goal for a day is the latest change whose day
    is on or before it; two changes for the same day are told apart by
    when they were chosen, the later one winning.
  - Friends and group mates don't see your goal (they do see your
    streak, Part B).

======================================================================
3. PART B - STREAKS
======================================================================

  WHAT A STREAK IS
  - The number of days in a row, ending today or yesterday, on which
    you reached the goal you had that day.
  - Today counts as soon as you reach it. Until then, a streak that ran
    through yesterday is still alive, and Today says so:
      "5-day streak. 2,140 steps to keep it going."
  - Missing a whole day ends it. No rest days or "streak freezes" for
    now (decided).
  - Your best streak ever is kept too.

  WHERE IT COMES FROM
  - Worked out by the server from the uploaded days (daily_steps) and
    your goal history, every time it's asked (it's never stored), so
    it's the same on every phone and survives a new phone. It needs step sharing, like coins. Someone not sharing
    sees "Share your steps to keep a streak" instead.
  - Days are the local dates the phone uploads, as everywhere else
    (step-tracker-step-refresh.txt), so travelling doesn't break it.
  - Older days can't be changed (the server already refuses uploads for
    days more than 8 days back), so a streak can't be built backwards
    further than that.

  LATE UPLOADS
  - A day can look missing because the phone hadn't synced yet. When
    its steps arrive later (within the 8-day window), the streak is
    simply worked out again with them: a streak that looked broken
    comes back, and the best streak can go up.
  - A late upload can make a streak reach 7 or 30, and then the bonus
    is paid then (see below), but a late upload never causes a second
    payment for the same run of days (see "Paid once").

  WHEN IT LAUNCHES (DECIDED: SHOW HISTORY, NO BACK-PAY)
  - Current and best streaks are shown from the days already uploaded,
    judged against 10,000 (everyone's goal until now). Someone already
    on a 12-day run sees "12-day streak" on day one.
  - Bonuses are NOT paid for milestones reached before the launch: a
    run that is already past 7 days at launch doesn't pay the 7-day
    bonus. Only a milestone reached on or after the launch day pays.
    The launch day is stored by the migration, so the rule is exact.
  - "Reached" means the DAY the run reached the milestone, not the day
    its steps arrived. A late upload on Nov 12 showing a run reached 7
    on Nov 8, before a Nov 10 launch, pays nothing; one showing it
    reached 7 on Nov 11 pays.
  - So launching never hands out a batch of coins.

  COIN BONUSES (DECIDED: 7 AND 30 DAYS)
  - 200 coins when a streak reaches 7 days in a row, and 1,000 coins
    when it reaches 30. Nothing at 100 or beyond. Paid by the server
    when the streak reaches that length, once per streak: a streak that
    breaks and builds up to 7 again pays the 7-day bonus again.
  - NOT tied to the 10,000-step daily coins. The bonus follows YOUR
    goal: with a 7,500 goal, seven days at 7,500 pay the 7-day bonus,
    even though none of those days earned the daily coins. The two are
    checked separately, in the same step.

  PAID ONCE
  - The whole step is done one at a time per person: the server takes a
    per-person lock (held until the step finishes), THEN reads the
    uploaded days fresh, works out the runs and pays. Two phones
    uploading different missing days at the same moment each claim in
    turn, and the second sees what the first saw plus its own day.
  - Each payment is also recorded with the day the run reached the
    milestone ("streak 7, reached 2026-11-14") in the coin history,
    which already refuses the same payment twice: a second layer if
    the lock were ever missed.
  - A late upload can join two runs into one (days 1-3, a gap on day
    4, then days 5-11, which already paid its 7-day bonus; day 4
    arrives late, making days 1-11 one run that reaches 7 on day 7).
    Before paying, the server checks whether this run of days already
    has that milestone paid anywhere inside it; if so, nothing more is
    paid. A late upload never causes an extra payment.
  - Already paid twice, then joined (DECIDED: NO CLAW-BACK): days 1-7
    paid a 7-day bonus, days 9-15 paid another, then day 8 arrives
    late and joins them into one run. Both bonuses were earned as the
    days looked at the time, and coins already received are never
    taken back. The joined run pays nothing more for 7 days (it already
    has that milestone paid inside it); it can still go on to 30.
    So a joined run may hold two earlier 7-day payments; what's
    guaranteed is that nothing is paid twice because of a late upload.
  - Recorded in the coin history like the daily goal coins ("7-day
    streak", "30-day streak"), and the same "You earned..." message on
    Today.
  - Paid in the same step as the daily coins (after steps upload), so
    it can't be claimed twice, even with two phones at once (the coin
    history already allows each payment only once).

  WHO SEES YOUR STREAK (DECIDED: FRIENDS AND GROUP MATES)
  - Shown on your player card to friends and group mates ("12-day
    streak"), the same people who already see your steps. Off for
    people who aren't sharing.

  SCREENS
  - Today: the streak under the progress ("5-day streak · best 12"),
    and on the day it's at risk, how many steps keep it going.
  - Profile: Daily goal (the picker, and "Your new goal starts on
    <date>"), and your best
    streak.
  - Player card: "12-day streak".
  - History: days at your goal stand out, as now, but by your goal.

  BUILT (20261108000000_goals_streaks.sql)
  - As designed. Notes from building:
      - Goals: daily_goal_changes, set_daily_goal / my_goal; the goal
        for a day is goal_on(). Streaks: goal_runs / streak_info, worked
        out on every read; my_streak, player_streak, and 'streak' in
        group_member_card (added to its approved fields).
      - The bonuses are inside claim_daily_rewards, after a per-person
        lock (coins:<id>). The two-phone race test
        (supabase/tests/concurrency/streak_race.sh) uses a 7,500 goal so
        no daily coins are involved: with 10,000-step days the two
        claims already wait on each other's daily-coin rows, which hid
        the lock's effect. Without the lock it pays twice; with it, once.
      - Today counts as done the moment the phone's own steps reach the
        goal, before the upload, so the streak line moves at once.
      - The track: when your goal falls on a distance tick (e.g. 7,500)
        that tick reads MINE; otherwise a small MINE marker is drawn.
        10,000 reads GOAL only while it is your goal too.

======================================================================
4. BUILDING ORDER
======================================================================

  1. The goal: the goal history table and set_daily_goal(), Today and
     History reading your goal, the Profile picker.
  2. Streaks: my_streak() (current, best, at risk, steps to go), Today
     and Profile.
  3. The 7- and 30-day bonuses: added to the daily coins step; the
     message.
  4. The player card.
  Then a preview build for your friend to try.

======================================================================
5. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  - Goals: 3,000 and 25,000 accepted; 2,500, 25,500 and 7,250 refused;
    a change counts from the day after the phone's date; only your own goal can be read or
    changed.
  - A past day is judged by the goal you had then: lower the goal, and
    yesterday's streak doesn't change.
  - Streaks: 5 days at goal ending yesterday = 5 and "at risk" today;
    reaching today makes it 6; a missed day in between ends it; best
    streak kept after a break; days not at goal don't count; a day with
    no upload breaks it.
  - Bonuses: reaching 7 pays 200 once and reaching 30 pays 1,000 once;
    100 pays nothing; claiming again pays nothing; a new streak
    reaching 7 later pays again; two claims at the same moment (two
    sessions) pay once.
  - The bonus follows the personal goal: a 7,500 goal met for 7 days
    with no day at 10,000 pays the 7-day bonus and no daily coins.
  - Two goal changes on the same day: the later one is the goal from
    the returned start date; both are kept.
  - The start date: the day after the phone's date; a date that isn't
    "today" anywhere on Earth is refused; an earlier date that is still
    valid starts the goal on that date's next day (the accepted
    loophole), never on a day before it.
  - The loophole's limit, tested directly: a tampered date lowering
    today's goal (and, at a fixed UTC+14 moment, yesterday's) does
    rescue the streak; over the same days, a player who set 3,000 from
    the start has a streak at least as long and at least the same
    bonuses.
  - Never changed: 10,000 for past and future days.
  - Launch: a run already 12 days long at launch shows 12 but pays no
    7-day bonus; a run reaching 7 after launch pays.
  - Late uploads: a missing day uploaded later joins the run back
    together and the streak goes up; joining two runs where one already
    paid its 7-day bonus pays nothing more (the example in "Paid
    once"); joining two runs that BOTH paid keeps both payments and
    pays nothing more.
  - Late uploads and launch: one revealing a milestone reached before
    the launch day pays nothing; one revealing a milestone on or after
    it pays.
  - Goals: Today's label and percentage for a 10,000 goal and for a
    7,500 goal (more than one goal tested).
  - The suspicious-steps view gives the same answers with any
    personal goal.
  - Two sessions at once (supabase/tests/concurrency): two phones
    uploading different missing days that together complete 7 days in
    a row, then claiming at the same moment: exactly one 7-day bonus.
  - Coins for the daily goal unchanged (still 10,000 steps).
  - Not sharing: no streak, no bonus.

  APP (npm test)
  - The goal picker's steps and range, and "steps to keep it going".

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - Pick 7,500 in Profile: it shows the start date (tomorrow); Today
    keeps the old goal until that date, then shows 7,500.
  - Walk past your goal: the streak goes up and Today says so.
  - On the 7th day in a row: the bonus message, 200 coins added once.
  - A friend's player card shows your streak.

======================================================================
6. DECISIONS
======================================================================

  1. Goal range: 3,000 to 25,000 in steps of 500, starting at 10,000.
  2. Coins stay at 10,000 steps for everyone.
  3. Your goal shows as a small marker on your own step track.
  4. Your streak shows on your player card to friends and group mates.
  5. No rest days or streak freezes for now.
  6. Streak bonuses: 200 coins at 7 days, 1,000 coins at 30 days.
     None at 100. They follow the personal goal, not the 10,000-step
     daily coins.
  7. Several goal changes in one day: the last one wins; all are kept.
     Never changed means 10,000, past and future.
  8. Launch: streaks are shown from existing history, but no bonus for
     a milestone reached before the launch day.
  9. Late uploads (within the 8-day window) update the streak and can
     pay a bonus; a late upload never causes a second payment.
  10. Two rewarded runs later joined by a late upload: both bonuses are
      kept (no claw-back), nothing more is paid.
  11. A milestone counts by the day it was reached, not the day its
      steps arrived, for the launch cutoff.
  12. Claiming is done one at a time per person (a lock), with the
      coin history's once-only rule as a second layer.
  13. The suspicious-steps checks are unchanged by personal goals.
  14. A new goal starts the day after the phone's date (checked to be
      "today" somewhere on Earth); the server returns the date and
      Profile shows it. Goal days are the same local dates as
      daily_steps. A tampered phone can lower the goal for a day
      already under way (today; yesterday too in UTC+13/+14 at some
      hours) and so rescue a streak, but never earn more than an
      honest player with a 3,000 goal. Accepted; tested.
