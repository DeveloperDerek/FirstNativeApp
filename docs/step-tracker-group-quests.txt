STEP TRACKER - GROUP QUESTS (BLUEPRINT)
A group takes on a quest together: reach one shared step goal within
a set number of hours. Any member can start one, and it is on at once
for everyone in the group who shares their steps. If the group makes
it, everyone earns coins.
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes how quests should work, what the screens show, how coins
are paid, and the tricky cases to decide before building.

CHANGED IN OCTOBER 2026 (step-tracker-decisions.txt, QUEST-1, 2 and 5)
  - No voting: nobody has to accept and nobody can decline. Any member
    sharing steps starts a quest, and it is locked in at once.
  - The party is everyone in the group sharing steps at that moment
    (it used to be the whole group, all of whom had to accept).
  - The goal grows a bit with the party: +10% for each member after
    the second, up to 8 (it used to be the same for any group).
  Built in 20261111000000_quests_without_voting.sql. The sections
  below describe the rules as they are now; where an older rule was
  replaced, it says so.

CHANGED IN OCTOBER 2026 (step-tracker-decisions.txt, COIN-3)
  - Quests pay twice as much: 1 coin per 100 steps (was 200).
  - The daily cap is 400 quest coins (was 600).
  Built in 20261115000000_coin_rebalance.sql.

Builds on what the app already has: groups, step sharing, the coin
wallet, the daily coin reward (150 coins at a target of 7,000 to
10,000 steps, step-tracker-goals-streaks.txt), the check-in bonus
(10 coins every 6 hours), and the shop (items cost 300 to 3,000
coins).

======================================================================
1. THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE
======================================================================

  - Anyone in a group can start a quest, for example:
      "Forest Sprint: 9,600 steps together in 2 hours" (4 people)
  - There is no vote. It starts straight away, or at a time the
    starter picks within the next 24 hours, for everyone in the group
    who shares their steps.
  - While it runs, the group page shows one shared progress bar, a
    countdown, and how much each member has added.
  - The goal is the group's total, and grows a little with the size of
    the party. It does not matter who walks the steps: one person can
    carry the whole party.
  - When the bar fills before time runs out, the quest is complete.
    The group can keep walking until the time is up to earn more.
  - Coins depend on how many steps the group walked and how big the
    group is (section 5), and are shared equally by every member.
  - If time runs out first, the quest fails. Nobody loses anything;
    the group can try again after a short cooldown.

======================================================================
2. THE LIFE OF A QUEST
======================================================================

  STARTED  ->  ACTIVE  ->  COMPLETED or FAILED  ->  PAID

  1. STARTED
     A member who shares steps picks a quest type (section 3) and a
     start time: "Right now", or a set time later today or tomorrow
     (5 minutes to 24 hours ahead). The quest is locked in at that
     moment: the party is every active member sharing steps, and the
     goal is set for that party (section 4).
     (Until October 2026 there was a VOTING step here: every member
     had to accept within 24 hours, and any decline cancelled it.)

  2. ACTIVE (the quest window, e.g. 2 hours)
     Steps taken by any party member during the window count toward
     the shared goal. People who join the group later, even before a
     quest set for later begins, are not in it. A quest set for later
     is already "active" in the database, but takes no steps until
     its start time.

  3. COMPLETED or FAILED
     - Completed: the moment the group's total reaches the goal.
       The celebration shows straight away, but the quest keeps
       counting until the window ends: every extra step (up to 150%
       of the goal) adds coins (section 5).
     - Failed: the window ends below the goal.
     After the window closes there is a 1-hour grace period, so
     members who have not opened the app yet can still send their
     steps (section 6).

  4. PAID
     After the window and its grace period, the server works out the
     coins from the final step total and pays every member of the
     party once. The quest then moves to the group's quest history.

  Only ONE quest can be under way in a group at a time (from the
  moment it's started until its grace period ends). After a quest
  ends (or is cancelled), the group waits 1 hour before the next one.

  Quests that were still waiting for votes when voting was removed
  were cancelled, without the 1-hour wait.

======================================================================
3. QUEST TYPES
======================================================================

  Each type has a length and a base goal: the group goal for 2 people.
  The goal grows with the party (section 4). The coins are not fixed
  per type: they come from the steps walked and the size of the group
  (section 5). The names can match the group's map theme (Forest
  Sprint, Beach Stroll, City Dash...) to tie quests to the art.

  Type              Length     Goal: 2 people   4 people   8 or more
  ----------------  ---------  --------------   --------   ---------
  Sprint            2 hours         8,000         9,600      12,800
  Patrol            4 hours        15,000        18,000      24,000
  Expedition        8 hours        30,000        36,000      48,000
  Day Trek         12 hours        50,000        60,000      80,000
  Weekend Journey  48 hours       120,000       144,000     192,000

  Coins each member gets when the group hits exactly the goal
  (worked out with the formula in section 5, for the goal of that
  party size):

  Type              2 members   4 members   8 members
  ----------------  ---------   ---------   ---------
  Sprint                24          19          19
  Patrol                45          36          36
  Expedition            90          72          72
  Day Trek             150         120         120
  Weekend Journey      360         288         288

  Walking past the goal pays more, up to 1.5 times these numbers
  (e.g. a 2-member Sprint that reaches 12,000 steps pays 36 each).

  Why these numbers:
  - A brisk walk is roughly 4,000 to 6,000 steps an hour, and an
    ordinary day is 5,000 to 8,000 steps.
  - A Sprint can be carried by one keen walker in under 2 hours, or
    shared by a few people on a lunch break. Longer quests are
    realistic for a group of 3 to 6 and a real push for 2.
  - Quest coins come on top of the daily reward (150 coins at the
    day's coin target); the same steps count for both. A shop item
    (300 to 3,000 coins) should take a few quests, not one; the
    Weekend Journey is the big prize.

  Stage A offers Sprint and Patrol; the others are in the database but
  switched off (section 10).

  Optional later: a rotating "Quest of the Week" with a special name,
  a slightly higher reward, and a one-off badge or pet for finishing
  it.

======================================================================
4. THE GOAL
======================================================================

  - The goal is a single number for the group: the type's base goal
    (section 3) plus 10% for each party member after the second, up to
    8 members (x1.6), rounded to 100 steps.
      2 members -> x1.0    3 -> x1.1    4 -> x1.2
      6 members -> x1.4    8 or more -> x1.6
  - Every step from every party member counts in full. There is no
    limit on how much one person can add: if one member walks the
    whole goal of a Sprint, the quest is complete.
  - The goal is fixed when the quest is started, for the party as it
    was then. People leaving the group later do not change it.
  - The start sheet shows each type's goal for the group's current
    party size.
  - Same formula on the server (quest_goal) and in the app
    (questGoal in src/quests/rules.ts).

  Why it grows, and only a bit:
  - With one goal for every size, a big group found quests far easier
    than a small one. Growing the goal evens that out.
  - It grows much slower than the group (8 people get 1.6 times the
    goal, not 4 times), so a big group still finishes more easily, and
    one person can still carry the party.
  - Coins are shared too: each member's share gets smaller as the
    group grows (section 5).
  (Until October 2026 the goal did not change with the party.)

  Difficulty choice (optional, later): the starter can pick Easy
  (x0.75 goal), Normal (x1), or Hard (x1.5 goal), on top of the party
  size. Difficulty only changes the goal; coins still follow the steps
  walked, so a Hard quest pays more because it needs more steps.

======================================================================
5. COINS
======================================================================

  When a quest is completed, the coins are worked out in three steps
  once the window and grace period are over:

  1. STEP COINS: the group earns 1 coin for every 100 steps it
     walked during the window. Steps past the goal count too, up to
     150% of the goal. (Was 1 coin per 200 steps.)
       Example: a Sprint group walks 10,000 steps -> 100 step coins.

  2. GROUP SIZE BONUS: step coins are multiplied by
       1 + 0.2 for every member after the first,
     up to a maximum of x2.5 (reached at 8 members).
       2 members -> x1.2    4 members -> x1.6
       6 members -> x2.0    8+ members -> x2.5
       Example: the same Sprint group has 4 members -> 100 x 1.6 = 160.

  3. SHARE: the result is split equally between every party member,
     rounded to whole coins, with a minimum of 10 coins each.
       Example: 160 / 4 = 40 coins each.

  Why it works this way:
  - More steps, more coins: the group is rewarded for walking past
    the goal, not just reaching it. The 150% limit stops very long
    walks from paying without end. Since a bigger party has a bigger
    goal, it also has more steps that can pay.
  - Bigger group, bigger total: a group of 8 earns more coins in
    total than a group of 2 for the same steps.
  - But a smaller share each: the bonus grows slower than the group,
    so each member's share shrinks as the group grows. A big group
    that lets one person carry an easy quest does not flood everyone
    with coins.
  - Equal shares: every member gets the same coins, whether they
    walked 20,000 steps or none. The rate (1 coin per 100 steps) is
    a little over half the daily reward's rate (150 coins for about
    8,500 steps), because the same steps also count toward each
    walker's daily reward. At 1 per 200 a 2-person Sprint paid 36
    coins each, too little for two hours of shared effort; now 72.
  - No prize for the top walker. The progress bar already shows who
    carried the party; that recognition is the reward.

  When a quest fails: no coins. Show how close the group got ("So
  close! 87%") to encourage another try, but do not pay consolation
  coins; they would make failing a free way to collect coins.

  Same rules as the rest of the coin system:
  - Quests need step sharing: only members sharing steps are in the
    party, and only they can start one (section 8), exactly like the
    daily reward.
  - The server decides everything: the party, progress, success and
    payment. The phone only shows what the server says.
  - Each member can be paid at most once per quest.
  - Daily cap: quest coins per person are capped at 400 a day (was
    600), room for about three quests. Because members earn whether
    or not they walked, the cap stops someone collecting rewards from
    many groups without moving. The cap is above anything Sprint or
    Patrol can pay (at most 135 each). A 2-member Day Trek (450) or
    Weekend Journey (1,080) would go over it: look at the cap again
    before turning those quests on.

======================================================================
6. COUNTING STEPS DURING THE QUEST WINDOW
======================================================================

  Quest steps are read hour by hour from each member's phone, so
every step taken inside the quest window counts, no matter when the
member opens the app.

  Why not use the daily totals the app already uploads?
  - Those are one number per day, sent only when the app is open.
    A quest window is a few hours, so the server would have to guess
    which of the day's steps fell inside it. Anyone who walked before
    opening the app would lose those steps, which hurts most when one
    person is carrying the party.

  How it works:
  1. The phone already keeps a timed record of steps: Apple Health
     on iPhone, Health Connect on Android. It keeps counting while
     the app is closed.
  2. Whenever a party member's app syncs during the quest (or within
     the grace period after it), the app asks the phone for that
     member's steps inside the quest window, split into hours:
       6:15 - 7:00   1,240 steps
       7:00 - 8:00   3,020 steps
       8:00 - 8:15     410 steps
     The first and last pieces are cut to the exact quest start and
     end, so nothing outside the window counts.
  3. The app uploads these hourly numbers for the quest. Each upload
     replaces that member's previous one, so syncing many times never
     double counts; the numbers simply grow as the member walks.
  4. A member's quest steps = their hourly numbers added up. The
     group total = every party member's quest steps added up.
  5. Crossing midnight needs no special handling: the window is two
     exact moments, and the phone counts the steps between them.

  What this means for players:
  - Steps count from the first second of the quest, even if the app
    was closed the whole time. Opening the app only SENDS the steps;
    it no longer decides which ones count.
  - The live progress bar only moves when members' apps sync, since
    the app cannot upload in the background. Members who want the
    group to see their progress can open the app now and then. A
    member who only opens the app after walking still gets every
    step counted, as long as it is within the grace period.

  Grace period:
  - Steps only count if they were TAKEN inside the window, but they
    can be UPLOADED up to 1 hour after it ends. When the window
    closes, tell every member: "Time's up! Open the app within an
    hour so your steps are added."
  - A quest is only marked failed after the grace period; it can be
    marked completed at any moment the goal is reached.

  Protections:
  - Manually typed-in steps never count: the same filters the app
    already uses for daily steps (Apple Health's "entered by the
    user" mark, and Health Connect's "manual entry" recording
    method) apply to quest steps.
  - A sanity limit per person per hour: any hour over 15,000 steps
    is rejected as impossible. Hourly numbers make this check exact,
    and it matters because one person can carry a quest.
  - The server decides the window, using its own clock: uploads for
    hours outside the window, before a quest set for later begins,
    or arriving after the grace period, are refused.
  - A member's quest steps can never be more than their step total
    for those days. If the two disagree, the server keeps the
    smaller number.

7. WHAT PEOPLE SEE
======================================================================

  A) Group page: quest card at the top

     +------------------------------------------+
     |  FOREST SPRINT              1h 12m left  |
     |  [###############.........]  5,980 /     |
     |                               9,600      |
     |                                          |
     |  Priya  ##########....   3,860           |
     |  Sam    ####..........   1,410           |
     |  You    ##............     710           |
     |  Hana   ..............       0           |
     |                                          |
     |  So far: about 12 coins each             |
     |  (keep walking: up to 29 each at 150%)   |
     +------------------------------------------+

     - The members' bars show each person's share of the group
       total, so it is easy to see who is carrying the party.
     - The party's characters (and pets) could walk along the
       progress bar like the step road, moving forward as the total
       grows. That reuses the walking scene already built.
     - "You" is highlighted.
     - With no quest: a short explanation and a "Start a quest"
       button, or why one can't start yet (fewer than 2 people, or
       fewer than 2 sharing steps), and the cooldown if resting.
     - A quest set for later: "The Sprint starts at 6:00 PM: 9,600
       steps together in 2 hours."

  B) Starting a quest (a sheet from the group page)

     - Pick a type: Sprint / Patrol (later Expedition / Day Trek /
       Weekend Journey), each showing length, the group goal for this
       party's size, and the coins each member would get.
     - Pick when: "Right now", or a set time within 24 hours.
     - A note: "The quest starts for everyone sharing steps (4
       people). Nobody has to accept." Anyone not sharing is named:
       "Hana isn't sharing steps, so won't be in it."
     - Button: "Start quest".

  C) Voting: removed in October 2026. There is no Accept / Decline
     card, no waiting list and no vote deadline.

  D) The end

     - Completed: a celebration screen with the party's characters
       jumping, total steps, time taken, who walked the most, and
       how the coins were worked out: "10,000 steps -> 50 coins,
       x1.6 for 4 members -> 80, shared: +20 coins each".
     - The celebration shows as soon as the goal is reached; the
       coins arrive after the window and grace period end.
     - Failed: "The party reached 87%. Try again in 1 hour."
     - Both appear the next time each member opens the app, if they
       missed it live.

  E) Quest history (inside the group page)

     - Past quests with result, date, party and coins earned.
     - Group stats: quests completed, best Sprint time, longest
       success streak.

  Notifications (needs push notifications, which the app does not
  have yet; until then, show these as banners when the app opens):
  - "Priya started a Forest Sprint. It begins at 6:00 PM."
  - "Your Forest Sprint is on: 4,000 / 9,600 with 1h left."
  - "Halfway there: 4,800 / 9,600 with 1h left"
  - "30 minutes left, 800 steps to go!"
  - "Time's up! Open the app within an hour so your steps are added."
  - "Goal reached! Keep walking until 8:15 PM to earn more coins."
  - "Quest finished: 10,000 steps. +20 coins"

8. TRICKY CASES
======================================================================

  - A member does not share their steps:
    They are not in the party and earn nothing, but they don't stop
    anyone else: the quest starts for the members who do share.
    They can't start a quest themselves until they turn sharing on.
    (Before voting was removed, one non-sharing member blocked every
    quest in the group.)

  - A member never opens the app:
    No longer blocks anything. If they share steps they are in the
    party; their phone's steps still count whenever they next open
    the app within the window and grace period, and they are paid
    like everyone else if the quest succeeds.

  - Someone joins the group after a quest is started (even one set
    for later that hasn't begun):
    They are not part of it: no steps counted, no coins. They can
    take part in the next one.

  - Someone leaves the group during a quest:
    Their steps so far still count toward the total, but they are
    not in the final share: the coins are split between the members
    who stayed. The group size bonus and the goal use the party size
    when the quest was started, so leaving does not shrink either.

  - Someone turns off step sharing during a quest:
    Their steps so far count; new steps stop counting; like leaving,
    they are not in the final share.

  - Members who did not walk at all:
    They are paid anyway if the quest succeeds; this is the point of
    "one person can carry the party". The daily cap (section 5) and
    the 1-hour cooldown keep it from being abused.

  - Nobody asked me before starting a quest:
    That's the rule now: any member can start one, and the party has
    no say. A quest costs nobody anything, and failing pays nothing,
    so there is nothing to lose by being in one. There is no way to
    cancel a quest once started.

  - The group is very small:
    Quests need at least 2 members sharing steps. Solo players already
    have the daily reward.

  - Time zones:
    The window is an exact start and end moment, shown in each
    member's own time. Step counting uses those exact moments, so
    time zones and midnight make no difference (section 6).

  - Apple Health or Health Connect permission missing:
    A member sharing steps is still in the party, but their steps
    will not count until they allow access. Show the same "Open
    settings" help as the Today tab.

  - Two groups at once:
    A person can be in quests in two groups at the same time, and
    their steps count for both. That is fine: they really walked
    them. The daily cap keeps it in check.

  - The goal is reached during the grace period:
    Counts as completed. Only steps TAKEN inside the window count,
    so these are steps that were uploaded late, not walked late.

======================================================================
9. WHAT THE DATABASE NEEDS TO REMEMBER (plain words)
======================================================================

  For each quest:
  - which group, which type, who started it
  - the start and end moments
  - the group goal and the party size (both fixed when it's started)
  - after it ends: the final step total, the step coins, the group
    size bonus, and the coins paid to each member
  - its state: active, completed, failed, cancelled
  - when it was completed, if it was
  - Kept only for quests from before October 2026: the vote deadline,
    who declined, and each member's vote (quest_votes). Nothing new
    writes them.

  For each party member during the quest:
  - their steps for each hour of the window (replaced on every
    upload), and when they last uploaded
  - their quest steps so far (those hours added up)
  - whether they have been paid, and how much

  Who can see what:
  - Only members of the group can see its quests and progress, just
    like they can already see each other's steps.
  - Members can only start a quest and send their own hourly steps,
    and only through the server's quest functions (propose_quest,
    upload_quest_steps), which check sharing, the window, the grace
    period and the per-hour limit. Nobody can write the group total,
    the party, the result or payments directly.

======================================================================
10. BUILDING IT IN STAGES
======================================================================

  Stage A - The core loop (built)
    Sprint and Patrol only. Start, active, completed or failed,
    everyone paid. Hour-by-hour step counting (section 6) with the
    1-hour grace period. Quest card on the group page with a progress
    bar, countdown and per-member bars. Banners instead of push
    notifications. October 2026: voting removed, goal grows with the
    party.

  Stage B - Make it feel good
    Characters walking along the progress bar, the celebration
    screen, quest history, group stats.

  Stage C - More quests
    Expedition, Day Trek, Weekend Journey, themed quest names,
    Quest of the Week.

  Stage D - Reach
    Push notifications (so the start, halfway and time's-up messages
    reach people with the app closed), difficulty levels.

  For testing, supabase/dev/fake_quest.sql puts the eight fake players
  in a group with you, with no quest ('none'), a quest under way with
  some hourly steps already uploaded ('active'), or one about to pay
  out ('ending').
