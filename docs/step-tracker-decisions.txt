STEP TRACKER - ALL DECISIONS (ONE PLACE)
Every decision recorded in the blueprints in docs/, gathered so they
can be reviewed and changed. Each one has an id (e.g. GOAL-6), points
back to its blueprint, and has a "Change to:" line. Leave it empty to
keep the decision; write the new choice there to change it.
Last checked: October 2026

This file is a summary. The blueprint named in each section has the
full reasoning, the options not chosen and the tests. If the two ever
disagree, the blueprint wins until it is updated.

4. GROUP QUESTS (step-tracker-group-quests.txt, sections 2-5 and 8)
======================================================================

  This blueprint has no numbered decisions list; these are the rules
  it settles.

  QUEST-1   Any member of the group can start a quest. Nobody has to
            accept and nobody can decline: it is on at once for every
            member sharing steps.
            (Changed October 2026: every member had to accept.)
            Change to:

  QUEST-2   No voting. One quest under way per group (started now, or
            at a set time within 24 hours); 1-hour wait after a quest
            ends before the next one.
            (Changed October 2026: quests were voted on first.)
            Change to:

  QUEST-3   1-hour grace period after the window for late uploads.
            Only steps taken inside the window count.
            Change to:

  QUEST-4   Quest types (goals for 2 people; see QUEST-5):
              Sprint            2 hours      8,000 steps
              Patrol            4 hours     15,000 steps
              Expedition        8 hours     30,000 steps
              Day Trek         12 hours     50,000 steps
              Weekend Journey  48 hours    120,000 steps
            Change to:

  QUEST-5   The goal is one number for the group, and one person can
            carry the party. It grows a bit with the party: the type's
            goal (for 2) plus 10% for each member after the second, up
            to 8 members (x1.6), rounded to 100. A Sprint for 4 is
            9,600 steps.
            (Changed October 2026: was the same for any group size.)
            Change to:

  QUEST-6   Coins: 1 coin per 100 steps walked, counting up to 150%
            of the goal.
            (Changed October 2026: was 1 coin per 200 steps. COIN-3.)
            Change to:

  QUEST-7   Group size bonus: x(1 + 0.2 per member after the first),
            max x2.5 at 8 members.
            Change to:

  QUEST-8   Shared equally, rounded, minimum 10 coins each; members
            who didn't walk are paid too. No prize for the top
            walker.
            Change to:

  QUEST-9   Failed quests pay nothing (show "So close! 87%").
            Change to:

  QUEST-10  Daily cap: 400 quest coins per person per day.
            (Changed October 2026: was 600. COIN-3.)
            Change to:

  QUEST-11  Quests need step sharing to take part; minimum 2 members
            sharing steps.
            Change to:

  QUEST-12  Joining after a quest is started (even one set for later):
            not in it.
            Change to: 

  QUEST-13  Leaving, or turning sharing off, mid-quest: steps so far
            count, but no share of the coins. The size bonus and the
            goal use the party size when the quest was started.
            Change to:

  QUEST-14  Missing Health permission: can still accept; steps don't
            count until access is allowed.
            Change to:

  QUEST-15  Quests in two groups at once are allowed; the same steps
            count for both (the daily cap keeps it in check).
            Change to:

  Later, not decided: difficulty levels (Easy x0.75, Normal, Hard
  x1.5) and a "Quest of the Week".
