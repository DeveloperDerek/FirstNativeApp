STEP TRACKER - LONGER HISTORY, AND REMINDERS (BLUEPRINT)
Two things that keep people coming back:
  A. History beyond the last 7 days: week, month and year
  B. Opt-in reminders: "still time for your goal", "keep your streak"
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database, phone and screens need, and
the decisions it rests on (section 6, all approved October 2026).
Items 2c and 2d of step-tracker-recommendations.txt; step 6 of its
suggested order.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  - History shows the last 7 days only, from a copy kept on the phone
    (steps-by-day:<account>, pruned to 7 days on purpose,
    step-tracker-step-refresh.txt), with an average.
  - The server keeps every day uploaded since the person started
    sharing (daily_steps), and each day's goal (daily_goal_changes).
    That is what coins and streaks are worked out from.
  - The phone uploads only the last 7 days on each refresh. A day that
    was more than 7 days old the next time the app was opened is never
    uploaded, so it is missing on the server for good, even though
    Health has it.
  - Streaks exist only for people who share steps: my_streak answers
    "not sharing" for everyone else, like coins.
  - Someone who stops sharing keeps the days already uploaded on the
    server; nothing more is added.
  - Apple Health / Health Connect on the phone hold the person's whole
    step history, from before they had the app too, whether or not they
    share. On iPhone, one query returns one total per day for a long
    range. On Android, the totals are one query too, but taking out
    hand-typed steps means reading every record in the range (getSteps
    in src/health/steps.android.ts already does this, page by page).
  - Notifications: only server pushes for friend requests, and those
    are off until push is set up (it needs the paid Apple Developer
    account for iPhones, and Firebase for Android). Nothing reminds
    anyone to walk. Tapping a notification already opens the screen in
    its data.url (use-push.ts), whether or not push is set up.

======================================================================
2. PART A - LONGER HISTORY
======================================================================

  VIEWS (DECISIONS 2, 3 AND 8)
  - Decided: three tabs at the top of History:
      Week    the last 7 days as bars (as today), each day judged by
              the goal you had that day. Going back moves 7 days at a
              time.
      Month   a calendar month (1st to last day), as bars, like
              Week (DECISION 3).
              The current month runs to today. Going back moves one
              calendar month.
      Year    a calendar year, January to December: 12 bars, one per
              month, each the average steps per day that month. The
              current year runs to this month. Going back moves one
              year.
  - Calendar months and years (not "the last 30 days / 12 months")
    because going back then lands on periods people recognise, and the
    year's monthly sums line up with the Month view.
  - With each view: total steps, average per day, best day (and its
    date), and days at goal (e.g. "18 of 30"), each day judged by its
    own goal.

  DAYS WITH DATA, ZERO DAYS AND MISSING DAYS
  - The same rules in Week, Month and Year, from the server or Health:
      - A day WITH DATA is a day whose total is known. A known total of
        0 is a real zero: it counts in the average, and it is a day not
        at goal.
      - A MISSING day has no known total. It is not counted as zero:
        it is left out of the total, the average and "days at goal"
        (so "18 of 30" becomes "18 of 27" with 3 missing days, and the
        screen says how many days are missing).
      - Average per day = total steps / days with data (zeros
        included). One day of 10,000 and one confirmed 0 is 5,000, not
        10,000.
      - The Year view's monthly average is the same thing for a month:
        that month's total steps / that month's days with data. The
        server (my_history_by_month) and the phone use this one
        definition, so a month's bar in Year equals the average shown
        in that month's Month view.
  - Sharers: a day with data is a day uploaded to the server. The phone
    uploads every one of the last 7 days on each refresh, 0 included,
    so an uploaded 0 is a real zero. A day never uploaded is missing
    ("not synced").
  - Non-sharers: Health returns a number for every day, including
    days before the person had any step data. Days before the first
    day with any steps in Health are missing; from that day on, every
    day is a day with data, zeros included.
  - A KNOWN LIMITATION, ON PURPOSE: Health gives no way to tell a real
    0 from "nothing recorded". So a genuine zero-step day before the
    first day with steps (e.g. a new phone that sat in its box for a
    few days) is treated as missing, not as 0. This only affects the
    days at the very start of someone's Health history, and only
    non-sharers' History.
  - (On iPhone, steps refused in Health look like 0 everywhere. That is
    already handled on Today by the "no steps? check Health access"
    hint; History shows the same hint when every day is 0.)
  - The current and best streak are all-time numbers, not per period,
    so they are shown once at the top of History, not with each view.
    For sharers only (DECISION 9).
  - Swipe or arrows to go back a week / month / year; no going past the
    first period with any data.

  WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (DECISION 1)
  - Decided: the SERVER for people who share steps, the PHONE for
    people who don't.
      - Sharing: the days uploaded (daily_steps) and each day's goal.
        That is exactly what coins and streaks use, so History, the
        streak and the coin history always agree.
      - Not sharing: read straight from Apple Health / Health Connect,
        on the phone only, never uploaded. The same views, judged by
        the same goals (which are kept whether or not you share).
  - TODAY, AND THE LAST 7 DAYS, ALWAYS COME FROM THE PHONE. The server
    only has what was last uploaded, so for a sharer who has walked
    since, it would show fewer steps than the Today screen. The phone's
    7-day copy is refreshed with every upload, so for those days the
    phone and server agree except for the newest steps.
  - Gaps for sharers: days before someone started sharing, and days
    never uploaded (more than 7 days old when the app was next opened,
    section 1), are empty on the server. History shows them as "not
    synced" (a faint bar or a dash), not as zero steps, so a week away
    from the app doesn't look like a week of not walking. They stay
    empty for this release: filling them in from Health would make
    History disagree with the streak and coins.
  - Explained on the screen: a view with "not synced" days has a line
    under it ("3 days not synced. What's this?") that opens a short
    note: "Your steps are sent when you open the app, for the last 7
    days only. Days that were older than that weren't sent, so they
    don't count toward your streak or coins and can't be shown here."
  - Someone who has stopped sharing is a non-sharer: History reads
    Health, and the days still on the server are not shown.
  - Not chosen: the phone for everyone. Longer history for sharers
    too (from before the app, and no gaps), but History could then
    disagree with the streak and coins (e.g. a day the phone counts
    that was never uploaded), which is confusing.
  - Either way, only YOUR history. Friends and group mates see what
    they see today (the rankings and the player card), nothing more.

  STREAKS FOR NON-SHARERS (DECISION 9)
  - Decided: none. Streaks stay a sharing feature like coins: no
    streak line in History, and only the plain reminder text. History
    can say "Share your steps to keep a streak" in its place.
  - Not chosen: a streak worked out on the phone from Health. It
    would follow different data from the server's (it counts days
    never uploaded), so turning sharing on could make the streak
    shorter, which is confusing, and it would be one more thing to
    keep in step with the server's rules.

  IN THE DATABASE
  - my_history(from_day, to_day, my_today): EVERY day in the range,
    each with the goal that applied that day, and its steps if
    uploaded (null if not: a missing day). Not sharing: the goals
    only, every step null; the phone takes the steps from Health and
    judges them by these goals.
  - my_history_by_month(year, my_today): the Year view for sharers. One
    row per month: days with data, total steps, days at goal (each
    day by its own goal), and the month's best day and its steps (so
    the Year view can show its best day too). A year is one small answer, not 365 rows.
    Not sharing: the goals only, through my_history over the year (366
    small rows at most), since the phone has the steps.
  - Everything is checked on the server, never left to the phone:
      - whose: always the signed-in person (auth.uid()); there is no
        "person" argument, so no one else's history can be asked for
      - signed out or inactive account: refused, as in my_streak
      - my_today must be "today" somewhere on Earth
        (is_today_somewhere, as my_streak and my_goal)
      - from_day not after to_day; to_day not after my_today; at most
        366 days per call (a whole leap year); year not after
        my_today's year
      - steps only when sharing (has_sharing_consent), as specified
    Needs nothing new in the read rules: you can already read your own
    steps and goal changes.

  WHERE PHONE AND SERVER MEET (SHARERS)
  - Each day comes from exactly one place: the last 7 days (today and
    the 6 before) from the phone's copy, every older day from the
    server. Never both, so nothing is counted twice. (A phone day the
    copy doesn't have yet, e.g. on a new phone before its first
    refresh, keeps the server's value.)
  - Every day's goal comes from the server (my_history), for the phone
    days too. A goal change inside the last 7 days is therefore judged
    the same way as on the server.
  - The Year view: a month that overlaps the last 7 days (this month,
    and early in a month the month before too) is built from the day
    rows of my_history, merged as above, not from my_history_by_month.
    Older months come from my_history_by_month. So this month's bar
    matches the Month view and Today.
  - The streak is never worked out on the phone: it is my_streak from
    the server, shown as it is.
  - Stopping sharing turns the person into a non-sharer: all of
    History reads Health. Resuming sharing after a gap: the gap is
    missing ("not synced") on the server, except the last 7 days, which
    the next refresh uploads as usual.

  WHERE GOALS COME FROM
  - Goals are kept on the server for everyone (daily_goal_changes),
    sharing or not, and the phone keeps no copy of the goal history.
    The goal is 10,000 for everyone since personal goals were removed
    (step-tracker-decisions.txt, GOAL-15); older days keep the personal
    goal they had. So History judges every day by the right goal after
    signing out and in, reinstalling, or a new phone, for sharers and
    non-sharers alike.
  - Without a connection: the views that need older goals show
    "Couldn't load your goals" with Retry, rather than judging days by
    the wrong goal. (The Week view keeps working from my_goal's 7 days,
    as today.)

  WHAT "TODAY" MEANS
  - Today is the phone's own date in its current time zone (dayKey,
    as Today, uploads and goals use). The server never decides today;
    it only checks that the phone's date is plausible. So History,
    Today and the reminders always agree on the same phone.
  - Around midnight and after changing time zone, History refreshes
    its "today" whenever it comes back to the screen, so it doesn't
    show yesterday as today.

  ON THE PHONE (people not sharing)
  - One Health query per view: day-by-day totals for the range, with
    the same trusted sources and the same "typed-in steps don't count"
    rule as Today (the anti-cheat rules in src/health).
      - iPhone: one statistics-collection query with a daily interval,
        filtered as getSteps is now.
      - Android: the daily totals in one aggregate-by-period query, then
        the hand-typed records read page by page and taken off the day
        they belong to.
  - On Android, reading every record for a whole year may be slow.
    Before building it, measure it on a real phone with a large dataset
    (a year of a phone plus a watch, the heaviest case): the time for
    the Health reads and for the working out, separately. About 2
    seconds is a guide for "feels slow", not a hard rule.
  - Try to make it faster before limiting it:
      - read the months of the year at the same time instead of one
        long read
      - keep past months' answers on the phone for the session (they
        rarely change), so going back and forth reads Health once
      - show the months as they arrive rather than waiting for all 12
  - Only if it is still slow after that: the Year view for non-sharers
    on Android covers the last 90 days (DECISION 5), and the screen says
    so ("Showing the last 90 days. Earlier months take too long to read
    from Health Connect on this phone.").
  - Otherwise not stored: read again each time History opens (Health
    is the source; the 7-day copy stays as it is).

3. PART B - REMINDERS
======================================================================

  LOCAL, ON THE PHONE (DECISION 4)
  - Decided: reminders are scheduled by the app on the phone itself
    (local notifications), not pushed by the server. That means:
      - they work now, without the push setup, the paid Apple account
        or Firebase (expo-notifications schedules local notifications
        with remote notifications turned off)
      - no server, no tokens, nothing about you leaves the phone
      - they know only what the app knew when it was last opened
  - Not chosen: server pushes, with today's uploaded steps. Needs push
    set up first, and the server only knows what was last uploaded,
    so it isn't fresher anyway.

  WHAT AND WHEN (DECISION 6)
  - Off until turned on (Profile > Reminders), asking for notification
    permission then, never on first launch (like push today). On
    Android 13 and later that is the notification permission prompt;
    no exact-alarm permission is needed (a few minutes late is fine).
  - One a day at most, at a time you pick (default 7pm), and never
    once today's goal is reached:
      - TODAY'S reminder, with a streak of 2 days or more: "Keep your
        6-day streak going. Still time to reach your goal today."
      - every other reminder: "Still time to reach your goal today."
    Tapping it opens Today (data.url "/", handled by the existing tap
    code in use-push.ts).
  - Only today's reminder mentions the streak, and only from a streak
    the server confirmed at the last refresh. No reminder ever says a
    streak is still going when the app can't know. The text is fixed
    when it is scheduled, and a streak quoted for a later day may
    already be broken by then (someone with a 6-day streak who stops
    opening the app and misses a day would still be told "keep your
    6-day streak" days later).
  - The numbers in it are as of the last time the app was open, so it
    doesn't say how many steps are left (the phone can't check Health
    at reminder time without running in the background, which the
    phones limit).
  - A reminder that comes while the app is open is shown as a banner
    too (a notification handler is set for the app in the foreground).

  WHERE "GOAL REACHED" AND THE STREAK COME FROM
  - Goal reached today: the phone's Health total at the last refresh
    against today's goal. That is newer than the server's today_done
    and works for non-sharers too.
  - Streak: my_streak's current streak, for sharers only (DECISION 9).
    Non-sharers always get the plain text.

  HOW IT STAYS RIGHT
  - Each time the app refreshes steps, it cancels its reminders and
    schedules the next ones, each as a one-off reminder for that date
    and time (not a repeating daily one, which can't skip just today):
      - today's, if the time hasn't passed and the goal isn't reached
      - one for each of the next 6 days
  - So reaching the goal (and opening the app) cancels today's;
    changing the time or turning reminders off takes effect at once;
    and someone who stops opening the app gets reminders for a week,
    then nothing (it doesn't nag a lapsed user forever).

  SAFE TO DO AGAIN AND AGAIN
  - Each reminder has a fixed id made from its date (e.g.
    "goal-reminder:2026-10-11"), passed as the identifier when
    scheduling. Checked in the installed expo-notifications (58.1.2):
    scheduleNotificationAsync takes an optional identifier and only
    makes up a random one when none is given;
    getAllScheduledNotificationsAsync lists the scheduled ones with
    their ids; cancelScheduledNotificationAsync cancels one by id.
  - Every rescheduling first cancels all of the app's goal reminders,
    then schedules the new ones. So no duplicate can build up even if
    a phone doesn't replace a reminder scheduled again with the same
    id. (iPhone does replace it; Android is checked on a device, see
    section 5.) The fixed id is a second safety net.
  - Cancelling removes only scheduled notifications whose id starts
    with "goal-reminder:". It never cancels everything, so any other
    notification the app shows later (pushes, quest alerts) is left
    alone.
  - One rescheduling at a time: if the app opens or refreshes again
    while it is still rescheduling, the second run waits for the first
    and then runs on the latest setting and steps. Opening the app five
    times in a row ends with at most one reminder per date.

  WHEN SOMETHING GETS IN THE WAY
  - Permission refused when turning reminders on: the switch stays
    off, and Profile > Reminders says "Notifications are off for this
    app" with a button to the phone's Settings.
  - Permission taken away later in Settings: each time the app opens it
    checks the permission before scheduling. If it is gone, nothing is
    scheduled and Profile > Reminders shows the same line and button.
    The setting stays on, so turning the permission back on in Settings
    brings reminders back at the next open, without turning them on
    again.
  - Time zone changed: reminders are set for the phone's local time
    when scheduled. On the next open they are all scheduled again, so
    they follow the new time zone from then on. Until then, they may
    come at the old time.
  - The phone removed scheduled reminders (after an update, a restore,
    clearing the app's notifications, or a restart on some Android
    phones): every open cancels and schedules them all again from the
    setting, so they come back the next time the app is opened. The
    setting is the source; the scheduled reminders are only a copy.
  - The setting (on, and the time) is kept on the phone per account
    (reminders:<account>). Signing out cancels the reminders and
    removes the setting, in clearLocalUserData (src/auth/local-data.ts),
    so a different account on the same phone starts with reminders off.

  QUEST PUSHES (DECISION 7)
  - The notifications blueprint lists pushes for group quests (a quest
    proposed, about to end, won). Those need server push, which isn't
    set up. Decided: not in this step; they come with turning push
    on (with the paid Apple account and Firebase).

4. BUILDING ORDER
======================================================================

  1. History, server side: my_history and my_history_by_month.
  2. History screen: the three views, the stats, going back in time,
     each day judged by its own goal, today and the last 7 days from
     the phone, "not synced" days, and the streak once at the top.
  3. History for non-sharers from Health (DECISION 1). Time a year's
     read on an Android phone first, and add the 90-day limit if
     needed (DECISION 5).
  4. Reminders: the Profile setting and time, permission, scheduling
     on every refresh, the foreground banner, cancel on sign-out,
     opening Today on tap.
  Then one build with everything (and the earlier steps), for your
  friend to try.

5. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  - my_history returns only your own days, every day in the range,
    each with the goal that applied (a goal change in the middle of
    the range shows both); a day not uploaded has null steps, an
    uploaded 0 has 0; more than 366 days in one call is refused;
    from_day after to_day, or to_day after my_today, is refused; a date
    that isn't "today" somewhere is refused; signed out or inactive:
    refused; not sharing: the goals, with every step null.
  - my_history_by_month: the sums match the days added up, uploaded
    zeros count as days with data, days at goal are counted by each
    day's own goal, a year after my_today's is refused. Each month's
    total / days with data equals the average of that month's days from
    my_history, for a month that has zero-step days AND missing days.
  - Someone else's history can't be asked for, and a non-sharer's
    goals come back after their goal was changed from another phone.

  APP (npm test)
  - The stats from a list of days: total, average, best day, days at
    goal by each day's goal. 10,000 and a known 0 average 5,000;
    10,000 and a missing day average 10,000; missing days are left out
    of "days at goal" and counted as missing.
  - Non-sharers: days before the first day with steps in Health are
    missing; zeros after it are real zeros. A Health history that
    starts with several zero days before the first day with steps:
    those days are missing, left out of the average and "days at
    goal".
  - Year matches Month: for a month with zero-step days and missing
    days, the Year bar equals the Month view's average, for sharers
    (from the server sums) and non-sharers (from Health), and for the
    current month (merged with the phone's last 7 days).
  - Building the views: a calendar month with no days, a goal change
    in the month, a leap year's February, the current month and year
    running only to today, and going back a period.
  - Merging for sharers: today and the last 7 days come from the
    phone, older days from the server; days the server doesn't have
    show as "not synced", not zero. For each case below, the totals,
    days at goal and streak count each day once and use that day's own
    goal:
      - walked more after the last upload: today matches Today
      - goal changed inside the last 7 days: days before and after the
        change judged by their own goals
      - History opened just after midnight: the new day is today, and
        yesterday moves into the phone's 6 earlier days
      - stopped sharing: everything from Health, no streak
      - resumed sharing after a gap: the gap is "not synced", the last
        7 days come from the phone
      - a 7-day window that crosses into last month: both months' Year
        bars match their Month views
  - Non-sharers: no streak, and the "share your steps" line instead.
  - Android: hand-typed records taken off the right day.
  - The reminder plan: given now, the reminder time, whether today's
    goal is reached and the streak, which reminders to schedule (none
    today once reached; none today after the time; 6 more days; none at
    all when off), and the text: today's with and without a streak,
    and the later days' NEVER mentioning the streak; non-sharers
    always plain; permission gone: nothing scheduled, setting kept.
  - Rescheduling again and again: run it several times at once and in
    a row; the schedule ends with at most one reminder per date, every
    id starts with "goal-reminder:", and a notification with any other
    id is never cancelled.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - History: Week, Month and Year show; going back a month works;
    numbers agree with Today and the streak; today's bar matches Today
    right after walking, before the next upload.
  - A sharer who hasn't opened the app for over a week: the missed
    days show "not synced".
  - Not sharing: History still shows a month, from Health, and no
    streak.
  - "Not synced": the note opens and explains.
  - Goals: a goal changed near the end of a month, then the new month:
    both months judge each day by its own goal, in Month and Year.
    Sign in on a new phone (sharer and non-sharer): the same days at
    goal as on the old phone.
  - Health access refused (iPhone) and taken away later (iPhone and
    Android): History shows the "check Health access" hint, not a
    history of real zero days; on Android it says access is off.
    Turned back on: History shows the real steps.
  - Android: how long a year's History takes for a non-sharer, with a
    large dataset (Health reads and working out timed separately),
    before and after the speed-ups. The 90-day limit goes in only if
    it is still too slow after them.
  - Midnight: History open across midnight shows the new day as today
    after coming back to it.
  - Reminders: turned on, permission asked once; at the time, the
    reminder comes (also with the app open); after reaching the goal
    and opening the app, it doesn't; turned off, it doesn't; after
    signing out, it doesn't; tapping it opens Today. Permission turned
    off in Settings: Profile says so; turned back on: reminders return
    at the next open. Time zone changed: after opening, they come at
    the picked time in the new zone.
  - Reminder timing: open the app just before midnight and again just
    after (today's and tomorrow's reminders move along a day); open it
    a minute or two before the reminder time without reaching the goal
    (it still comes) and after reaching it (it doesn't).
  - Reminder ids, on BOTH an iPhone and an Android phone, reading the
    list of scheduled notifications (a debug screen or log of
    getAllScheduledNotificationsAsync):
      - change the reminder time: each date still has exactly one
        goal reminder, at the new time
      - open the app five times quickly: still at most one per date
      - turn reminders off: no "goal-reminder:" left
      - with a test notification of another id scheduled: it is still
        there after rescheduling and after turning reminders off
      - schedule the same id twice directly: note whether Android
        replaces it (the cancel-first step covers it either way)

6. DECISIONS (APPROVED, OCTOBER 2026)
======================================================================

  These are final. Build to them; the "not chosen" notes above only
  explain why. Anything not covered here is an open question to ask
  about, not a choice to make while building.

  1. Where history comes from: the server for sharers and the phone
     for non-sharers, with the last 7 days (today included) always from
     the phone and missing days shown as "not synced".
     Not chosen: the phone for everyone.
  2. Views: Week, Month and Year.
  3. Month view: bars, matching Week.
     Not chosen: a calendar with the days at goal filled in.
  4. Reminders: scheduled on the phone (local notifications).
     Not chosen: server pushes.
  5. Non-sharers on Android: measure a year on a real phone with a
     large dataset and try the speed-ups first. Only if it still feels
     slow (about 2 seconds as a guide), limit their Year view to the
     last 90 days and say so on the screen.
     Not chosen: always read a year however long it takes.
  6. Reminders: off until turned on, one a day at most at a time you
     pick (default 7pm), none once the goal is reached, the streak
     version from 2 days and only in today's reminder, and stopping
     after a week without opening the app.
  7. Quest pushes: not in this step; they come with turning push on.
  8. Periods: Week is the last 7 days (as today); Month and Year are
     calendar months and years.
     Not chosen: the last 30 days and the last 12 months.
  9. Streaks for non-sharers: none, as with coins.
     Not chosen: a streak worked out on the phone from Health.
  10. Days without data: a known 0 counts as 0 in the average and
     "days at goal"; a missing day is left out and shown as missing.
     Not chosen: counting missing days as 0.
  11. "Not synced" days for sharers: left empty for this release, with
     an explanation on the screen.
     Not chosen: filling them in from Health.
