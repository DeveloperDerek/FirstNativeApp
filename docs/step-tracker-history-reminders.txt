STEP TRACKER - LONGER HISTORY, AND REMINDERS (BLUEPRINT)
Two things that keep people coming back:
  A. History beyond the last 7 days: week, month and year
  B. Opt-in reminders: "still time for your goal", "keep your streak"
Last checked: October 2026

This is a design blueprint only. There is no code in this file. It
describes what changes, what the database, phone and screens need, and
the choices to make before building (section 6). Items 2c and 2d of
step-tracker-recommendations.txt; step 6 of its suggested order.

======================================================================
1. HOW IT WORKS TODAY
======================================================================

  - History shows the last 7 days only, from a copy kept on the phone
    (steps-by-day:<account>, pruned to 7 days on purpose,
    step-tracker-step-refresh.txt), with an average.
  - The server keeps every day uploaded since the person started
    sharing (daily_steps), and each day's goal (daily_goal_changes).
    That is what coins and streaks are worked out from.
  - Apple Health / Health Connect on the phone hold the person's whole
    step history, from before they had the app too, whether or not they
    share. Both libraries can return one total per day for a long range
    in a single query.
  - Notifications: only server pushes for friend requests, and those
    are off until push is set up (it needs the paid Apple Developer
    account for iPhones, and Firebase for Android). Nothing reminds
    anyone to walk.

======================================================================
2. PART A - LONGER HISTORY
======================================================================

  VIEWS (CHOICE 2)
  - Suggested: three tabs at the top of History:
      Week    the last 7 days as bars (as today), each day judged by
              the goal you had that day
      Month   the last 30 days as bars, or a calendar with the days at
              goal filled in (CHOICE 3)
      Year    12 bars, one per month: the average steps per day that
              month
  - With each view: total steps, average per day, best day (and its
    date), days at goal (e.g. "18 of 30"), and the current and best
    streak.
  - Swipe or arrows to go back a week / month / year.

  WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM (CHOICE 1)
  - Suggested: the SERVER for people who share steps, the PHONE for
    people who don't.
      - Sharing: the days uploaded (daily_steps) and each day's goal.
        That is exactly what coins and streaks use, so History, the
        streak and the coin history always agree. Days before someone
        started sharing are simply empty.
      - Not sharing: read straight from Apple Health / Health Connect,
        on the phone only, never uploaded. The same views, judged by
        the same goals (which are kept whether or not you share).
  - Alternative: the phone for everyone. Longer history for sharers
    too (from before the app), but History could then disagree with
    the streak and coins (e.g. a day the phone counts that was never
    uploaded), which is confusing.
  - Either way, only YOUR history. Friends and group mates see what
    they see today (the rankings and the player card), nothing more.

  IN THE DATABASE
  - my_history(from_day, to_day, my_today): your own days in the range,
    each with its steps and the goal that applied that day. At most a
    year and a few days per call, and only for yourself (the server
    takes you from the sign-in). Needs nothing new in the read rules:
    you can already read your own steps.
  - The year view adds the days up by month on the server, so a year is
    one small answer, not 365 rows.

  ON THE PHONE (people not sharing)
  - One Health query per view: day-by-day totals for the range, with
    the same trusted sources and the same "typed-in steps don't count"
    rule as Today (the anti-cheat rules in src/health). On Android,
    taking hand-typed steps out of a whole year means reading every
    record; if that is slow, the year view for non-sharers is limited
    to the last 90 days (CHOICE 5).
  - Not stored: read again each time History opens (Health is the
    source; the 7-day copy stays as it is).

======================================================================
3. PART B - REMINDERS
======================================================================

  LOCAL, ON THE PHONE (CHOICE 4)
  - Suggested: reminders are scheduled by the app on the phone itself
    (local notifications), not pushed by the server. That means:
      - they work now, without the push setup, the paid Apple account
        or Firebase
      - no server, no tokens, nothing about you leaves the phone
      - they know only what the app knew when it was last opened
  - Alternative: server pushes, with today's uploaded steps. Needs push
    set up first, and the server only knows what was last uploaded,
    so it isn't fresher anyway.

  WHAT AND WHEN (CHOICE 6)
  - Off until turned on (Profile > Reminders), asking for notification
    permission then, never on first launch (like push today).
  - One a day at most, at a time you pick (default 7pm), and never
    once today's goal is reached:
      - with a streak of 2 days or more: "Keep your 6-day streak going.
        Still time to reach your goal today."
      - otherwise: "Still time to reach your goal today."
    Tapping it opens Today.
  - The numbers in it are as of the last time the app was open, so it
    doesn't say how many steps are left (the phone can't check Health
    at reminder time without running in the background, which the
    phones limit).

  HOW IT STAYS RIGHT
  - Each time the app refreshes steps, it cancels its reminders and
    schedules the next ones:
      - today's, if the time hasn't passed and the goal isn't reached
      - one for each of the next 6 days
  - So reaching the goal (and opening the app) cancels today's;
    changing the time or turning reminders off takes effect at once;
    and someone who stops opening the app gets reminders for a week,
    then nothing (it doesn't nag a lapsed user forever).
  - Signing out cancels them (they belong to the account), and a
    different account on the same phone starts with reminders off.

  QUEST PUSHES (CHOICE 7)
  - The notifications blueprint lists pushes for group quests (a quest
    proposed, about to end, won). Those need server push, which isn't
    set up. Suggested: not in this step; they come with turning push
    on (with the paid Apple account and Firebase).

======================================================================
4. BUILDING ORDER
======================================================================

  1. History, server side: my_history and the monthly sums.
  2. History screen: the three views, the stats, going back in time,
     each day judged by its own goal.
  3. History for non-sharers from Health (CHOICE 1), with the 90-day
     limit on Android if needed (CHOICE 5).
  4. Reminders: the Profile setting and time, permission, scheduling
     on every refresh, cancel on sign-out, opening Today on tap.
  Then one build with everything (and the earlier steps), for your
  friend to try.

======================================================================
5. TESTS
======================================================================

  DATABASE (supabase/tests/database)
  - my_history returns only your own days, each with the goal that
    applied (a goal change in the middle of the range shows both);
    more than about a year in one call is refused; a date that isn't
    "today" somewhere is refused; signed out or inactive: refused.
  - The monthly sums match the days added up, and days at goal are
    counted by each day's own goal.
  - Someone else's history can't be asked for.

  APP (npm test)
  - The stats from a list of days: total, average (over days with
    data), best day, days at goal by each day's goal.
  - Building the month and year views (a month with no days, a goal
    change in the month, a leap year's February).
  - The reminder plan: given now, the reminder time, whether today's
    goal is reached and the streak, which reminders to schedule (none
    today once reached; none today after the time; 6 more days; none at
    all when off), and the text with and without a streak.

  ON A PHONE (DEVICE-TESTS.md)
  - History: Week, Month and Year show; going back a month works;
    numbers agree with Today and the streak.
  - Not sharing: History still shows a month, from Health.
  - Reminders: turned on, permission asked once; at the time, the
    reminder comes; after reaching the goal and opening the app, it
    doesn't; turned off, it doesn't; after signing out, it doesn't.

======================================================================
6. CHOICES TO MAKE BEFORE BUILDING
======================================================================

  1. Where history comes from: the server for sharers and the phone
     for non-sharers (suggested), or the phone for everyone.
  2. Views: Week, Month and Year (suggested), or fewer.
  3. Month view: 30 bars (suggested; matches Week), or a calendar with
     the days at goal filled in.
  4. Reminders scheduled on the phone (suggested), or server pushes
     (needs push set up first).
  5. Non-sharers on Android: if a year of Health data is slow, limit
     their year view to 90 days (suggested), or always read a year.
  6. Reminders: off until turned on, one a day at most at a time you
     pick (default 7pm), none once the goal is reached, a streak
     version from 2 days, and stopping after a week without opening the
     app (suggested).
  7. Quest pushes: not in this step; with turning push on later
     (suggested).
